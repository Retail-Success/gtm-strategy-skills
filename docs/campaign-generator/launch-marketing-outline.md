# What Marketing Does for a Launch Campaign

**Source:** the GTM skills in `.claude/skills/` (Phases 4, 6–12). Every row cites the skill and task it comes from.
**Status:** outline only. No copy is generated. The feature is on hold and not deployed (see `README.md`).
**Use:** this is the checklist a launch produces in place of generated copy. Rows that run on a channel can still become tracked `campaigns`.

**Owner key:** **M** = Marketing · **S** = Sales/CSM · **P** = Product · **All** = whole launch team

## 7. The feature webinar (prospects + current clients)

**What the skills say.** `executing-launch` T5 lists a *"live webinar / demo"* as the launch event for B2B and complex products. It covers:
- **Format:** date, time zone, goal, target attendance, platform.
- **Run-of-show:** a hook in the first 60 seconds, then demo → story → proof (as in the launch email), live Q&A, and one clear CTA.
- **Promotion:** use the chosen channels and the support network; reminders at T−7, T−3, T−1 and T−1h.
- **After:** follow-up email within 2 hours and the recording within 24 hours.
- **Contingency:** pre-recorded demo if the tech fails; record for async if attendance is low.

**How to fit it to two audiences.** `crafting-positioning` T3 says each audience gets its own message.
- **Current clients' home offices** are invited through CSMs. Their CTA is *"turn it on for your reps"*, or *"finish payment setup"* where it's pending.
  - Per `06-brand-architecture-2027.md`, a client webinar leads with **Wayroo** "from ByDesign".
- **Prospects** are invited by sales and partners. Their CTA is *book a demo*.
- **One webinar, two invite lists, two follow-ups** is the simplest version. Track registrations and attendance by list so each audience shows up as its own result.

**Already in the repo.** `08-bydesign-2027-gtm-plan.md` schedules a **client webinar on 2027-02-24** (Sarah Percell), three weeks before a Mar 15 POS date. Two options:
- preview POS in that webinar;
- hold a separate launch-week webinar.

**Decide which.** `12-abm-program.md` also proposes joint webinars with consultant partners for Tier 1 prospects.

**Watch:** prospects and clients in the same room. Don't show client data or names unless the client has agreed.

## 8. Paid: industry-specific publications

**What the skills say.** `running-marketing` T10 sets the rules for any paid channel:
- **Paid only after** validated messaging, a converting landing page and some organic traction.
- **Start with one platform.**
- **Send each ad to a dedicated landing page.**
- **Judge on cost per acquisition, not clicks.**
- **Retarget from day one.**

`running-marketing` T8 treats industry publications as *earned* media: Tier 3 (guest columns, features). **Buying placements in trade publications isn't covered by any skill. It's an addition, marked ➕.**

**What the repo shows:**
- **2026 results.** Meta paid social tied to **DSU, DSN and The Juice** produced **~325 sessions and no leads** (`08-bydesign-2027-gtm-plan.md` §1). The Juice is credited with 4 Salesforce opportunities in 2026 by source.
- **2027 rules** (same file, §5): no more than **30% of paid spend on an unproven channel**, a monthly review, and **kill a channel with no qualified opportunity in 60 days**.
- **Spend data is missing.** 2026 spend by channel is still an open question (Sarah, due 2026-12-11), so cost per opportunity can't be computed yet.

**What marketing does:**
1. **Pick 1–2 publications.** Choose where DSO executives actually read, and use the 2026 opportunity data to choose.
2. **Pair paid with earned.** Pitch a feature story or guest column to the same publication (T8), and send the press release (`preparing-launch-assets` T5).
3. **Give every placement its own landing page and UTM.** It needs a webinar or demo CTA, not a generic homepage link.
4. **Set the kill line before booking:** cost per qualified opportunity, read at 60 days.

## Where the repo already applied this for Wayroo feature releases

The skills above are general. The repo's own outputs add these rules for launches to our reps. Keep them when the outline is used for Motion 14:

- **Before any send:** publish the help article, tell Sales and CSMs what goes out and when, and pull the demand count (`outputs/08-revolution-ad-campaign.md` §6, release checklist).
- **Order:** DSO email, then rep email, then back-office ad refresh. If the feature needs payment setup, add a separate rep email for accounts where it's pending (same file).
- **First 14 days:** route the how-to through field leaders and capture testimonials (`outputs/10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md`, Phase 2).
- **Words:** follow `outputs/06-messaging-house.md` §9 (for example "card payments on your phone", not "POS"; "included", not "free").
