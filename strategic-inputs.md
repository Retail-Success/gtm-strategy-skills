**Three primary segments:**

| Segment | Definition | Lead Pillar | Variant File |

- 100% catalog-only with zero vendor event activity (Wayroo's biggest features don't apply)
- $1B+ DSOs already deeply integrated with Rallyware (co-existence more probable than displacement)

**New ICP under partnership (added May 2026):** AiCE individual sellers / creators (Motion 3' in AiCE expansion strategy). This is a **direct-to-creator** population, not a DSO buyer. **Phase 6 positioning does not currently cover this segment** — build before generating creator-economy GTM content.

## 3. Positioning (Buyer Vocabulary)

**Category frame:** *Native end-to-end commerce platform for direct selling.*

**Wayroo specifically:** *The rep business operating system* — ERP-tier scope (inventory + orders + payments + financial records + shipping + storefront).

**Lead differentiator hierarchy (in this order, tested with Color Street + Jordan Essentials):**
1. **POS + ProPay rep transaction infrastructure** — the rep experience layer. Brian B (Color Street CEO) reframed this unprompted as *"a sales tool ... how the field's using it, getting in front of customers, sell more, make their lives easier."*
2. **Native back office ↔ rep app integration** — the architectural advantage. CTO buying argument.
3. **Three-pillar FTC compliance** — supporting structural argument, **not the lead.** Compliance is a late-stage close move, not an opener.

**Internal vocabulary to avoid in buyer-facing artifacts:**
- "Revenue engine" → use *"sales tool"* or *"rep business operating system"* instead
- "Leverage" / "harness" / "unlock" / "robust" / "cutting-edge" → don't use
- Em dashes → use commas, periods, or parentheses

## 5. Pricing Philosophy

Don't generate pricing variants that contradict these:

- **Three-way revenue alignment:** Wayroo earns from transactions, not just subscriptions. *"The more your reps sell, the cheaper Wayroo gets."*
- **eSuite pass-through:** DSO bundles Wayroo's $1/user/month into the existing eSuite tech fee charged to reps. Net-zero per-user cost for corporate.
- **Per-active-rep billing, not nominal seats.** With seller-vs-hobbyist ratios as low as 20/80 (Color Street), nominal-active-rep pricing math overestimates DSO cost by 5x. Always qualify the real-seller denominator before quoting.
- **Spendback:** Drops DSO wholesale processing fee from ~3% to 1%. Three-account validated (Paparazzi quantified + Nancy "big benefit" + Brian "that's great"). Use as the CFO-level economic opener in Hybrid DSO motion.
