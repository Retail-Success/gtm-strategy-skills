# The Trade, Stated Honestly

**White-label was the only remaining paid Wayroo surface.** This program gives it away three times.

| What it buys | What it costs |
|---|---|
| **3 ProPay-approved accounts** — the only state in which payments revenue exists | **The first three sales of the only paid surface**, forgone |
| **Branded deployments.** The one 28%-in-48-hours datapoint we own (JE) was branded and corporate-announced | **A precedent.** DSO #4 knows three peers got it free |
| **A stated $25,000 anchor** in the market | The anchor is now public and must be defended |
| **3 independent proof points** instead of 1 | Staff time on three simultaneous implementations |

## Two rules that protect the price

**1. Always state the $25,000.** A gift with a number is a discount; a gift without one becomes the new price. Every conversation, every document, every slide.

**2. Bound it explicitly: "the first three pilot partners."** Scarcity is what stops this becoming the standard offer — and it is also what makes saying yes quickly worth something. **Never describe it as "the pilot pricing."** It is a founding-partner arrangement with a closed door.

> ⚠️ **The unbranded question goes untested exactly where it could best be tested.** [`06-positioning-statement.md`](06-positioning-statement.md) resolves the unbranded-trust problem with *"the product self-authenticates through data, not branding."* **The 55-client rollout is unbranded; these three will not be.** Instrument first-run comprehension at a branded pilot **and** at an unbranded account, or the question that governs the other 52 accounts stays open. Cheap to do, and easy to forget.

# Selection Criteria

Rank candidates on these five. **The first is a hard gate — everything else is worthless without it.**

| # | Criterion | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 🔴 **ProPay-approvable, and willing to start now** | **Gate** | No approval = no selling tools = no payments = **the pilot measures nothing that matters** |
| **2** | **Meaningful in-person selling cohort** | High | R1 is the persona the selling tools serve. A catalog-only field cannot exercise the product. |
| **3** | **A convention or field event on the calendar** | High | The only launch mechanic with evidence behind it — JE's 28%/48hrs was convention-driven |
| **4** | **Willing to be a named reference** | High | The proof asset is the point. **Get this in writing up front**, not after the results are good. |
| **5** | **Rep base 1,000–10,000** | Medium | Large enough to be evidence, small enough to support properly |

## Deliberate portfolio design — do not pick three of the same DSO

Three identical accounts produce one datapoint three times. **Spend the three slots on three different questions:**

| Slot | Profile | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **C&C-heavy**, convention-driven | Can we reproduce JE's 28%? **The replication test.** |
| **2** | **Catalog-heavy** (R3-dominant) | 🔴 **Does the product work without in-person selling?** The biggest untested assumption in the library — and the one the Aice pairing rests on. |
| **3** | **Large, or on a non-Freedom back office** | **Does the back-office-agnostic claim hold?** Directly tests the Wayroo-led entry thesis (`my-gtm-context.md` §14). |

> **Slot 2 is the most valuable and will be the most tempting to skip.** A catalog-heavy DSO will show worse adoption numbers than a C&C one. **That is the finding, not a failure** — and discovering it at 3 accounts is enormously cheaper than discovering it at 55.

# The Offer — How to Present It

**Audience: H1 (CEO/Founder) primary, H3 (VP Sales) secondary.** See [`06-persona-positioning.md`](06-persona-positioning.md).

> **We're taking three companies into a founding pilot for Wayroo.**
>
> Your reps get the full app free — inventory that fills itself from their wholesale orders, their back-office numbers on their phone, card payments on their phone, invoicing, event checkout. **They're already paying for Revolution; this rides inside it.**
>
> **For the three founding partners we're also doing custom branding at no charge — that's a $25,000 build, free.** Your name, your look, your field's app.
>
> **What we need from you is one thing: ProPay approval**, so the payment tools switch on for your reps. It's underwriting paperwork, not a purchase.
>
> **And we'd want to talk publicly about how it went.**

## What each side commits

| **ByDesign gives** | **The DSO gives** |
|---|---|
| Wayroo Essentials + all selling tools, free | **ProPay approval** — the one hard requirement |
| **Custom branding — $25,000, free** | A launch moment (convention, field call, or announcement) |
| Launch support + field enablement | **Named reference rights, agreed up front** |
| Adoption and GMV reporting they don't have today | A named internal point of contact |

> 🔴 **Get the reference commitment in writing at signing.** Asking for it after the results are in converts far worse, and if the results are poor you will not get it at all — which is precisely when an honest account of what happened is most valuable internally.

# What the Pilot Must Measure

**The pilot's output is evidence.** Undefined measurement means three accounts and no findings — which is what happened at JE, where no post-launch adoption data exists at all.

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Download rate** vs. rep base | Replication test against JE's 29.2% |
| 🔴 **Transact rate** — % who complete a sale | **The revenue metric.** Never measured anywhere. |
| **GMV per transacting rep** | Tests the Paparazzi ~$7,750/yr benchmark on a second account |
| **Time from download → first transaction** | Activation friction |
| 🔴 **Adoption curve past day 7** | **The specific gap JE left.** Do not repeat it. |
| **Persona mix** (R1/R2/R3 by wholesale + downline data) | Which personas actually adopt |
| **Branded vs. unbranded first-run comprehension** | The one question these accounts uniquely can answer |

> ### The measurement plan ships before the pilot, not after.
>
> Jordan Essentials launched, produced a 764-download number, and generated **no curve, no transact rate, and no baseline.** The account is still the entire evidence base for the motion. **Do not run three more of those.** See [`04-metrics-and-analytics.md`](04-metrics-and-analytics.md).

# Risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Pilot DSOs don't complete ProPay** | **Critical** | Make it the gate, not a follow-up. **No approval, no pilot slot** — offer it to the next candidate. |
| 🔴 **$25,000 anchor becomes the expected free offer** | High | State the number every time; bound to "first three, closed after" |
| 🟠 **All three chosen C&C** — one finding, three times | Med-High | Enforce the three-slot portfolio, especially slot 2 |
| 🟠 **Branded pilots leave unbranded rollout untested** | Medium | Instrument one unbranded account in parallel |
| 🟠 **Reference rights refused after weak results** | Medium | Written at signing |
| 🟡 **Three simultaneous implementations exceed capacity** | Medium | Stagger starts; confirm support load with Autumn + Cassie |
