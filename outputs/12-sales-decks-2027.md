# Deck A — ByDesign + Wayroo (DSOs that need a back office)

### A1 — Title
**Copy:** *ByDesign runs the business. Wayroo runs the field.* Subline: "A back office and a field app, built as one system." [Prospect logo] · [date]
**Design:** Two-tone split. ByDesign mark left, Wayroo mark right, the prospect's logo centred under both. No product screenshots yet.
**Speaker notes:** Thirty seconds. "Today is about your field and your home office in one picture. I'll ask more than I show."

### A2 — Their world
**Copy:** *Your home office runs on one system. Your field runs on ten.*
- Commissions, orders and genealogy in your back office.
- Reps selling through Square, Venmo, Facebook groups and spreadsheets.
- Reps paying $60–100 a month of their own money for those tools.
**Design:** Left: one back-office block. Right: a scatter of 6–10 app tiles around a phone.
**Speaker notes:** Ask: "Which of these do your reps use?" Let them name them. Their list beats ours.

### A3 — The problem
**Copy:** *What happens in the field never reaches the home office.*
- You can't see rep sales outside your own cart.
- Customers belong to Square or Facebook, not to you.
- Every platform you add is another contract, another integration, another login.
**Speaker notes:** For a platform switcher (Ellie MD on TrinitySoft): "And your current platform makes it harder, not easier. What made you start looking?" Their answer is the deal.

### A4 — The cost of doing nothing
**Copy:** *The field keeps leaving your rails.*
- **Visibility:** compliance and tax questions with no record behind them.
- **Retention:** new reps who never make a first sale quit. The tools that would help them cost money before they earn any.
- **Platform risk:** every renewal on an aging back office is another year of workarounds.
**Design:** Three columns, one icon each. No invented numbers. Add the prospect's own numbers from discovery.
**Speaker notes:** Ask for their rep churn and how many reps sell in person. Write both on this slide in the follow-up version.

### A5 — The solution
**Copy:** *One system, from commission run to card tap.*
- **ByDesign** runs commissions, orders, enrollment and inventory.
- **Wayroo** puts each rep's sales, inventory, customers and payments on their phone, connected from day one.
- **Aice** brings training, community and content inside Wayroo.
**Design:** Three stacked bands: ByDesign (home office) → Wayroo (field) → Aice inside Wayroo. One arrow up labelled "every sale, back to you."

### A6 — How it works (four steps)
1. **Move your back office** to ByDesign, with our migration team and a plan for your next commission run.
2. **Reps download Wayroo.** Their inventory, sales and downline are already there.
3. **Turn on selling:** tap to pay, invoices and a storefront, with payouts to the rep.
4. **See the field:** every rep sale and customer flows back to your home office.
**Speaker notes:** Step 1 is the fear. Spend time there: name the migration lead and the reference client before they ask.

### A7 — Comp plans, built with the engine that pays them
**Copy:** *The plan you design is the plan that pays.* Our comp-plan team designs or reviews your plan on the same engine that runs it. No translation between the consultant's spreadsheet and the commission run.
**Speaker notes:** Keep for startups and for DSOs changing their plan with the move. Skip for a straight migration.

### A8 — Proof
**Copy:** Choose per room:
- **Paparazzi** *(sign-off required)*: "Reps on Wayroo, about a fifth of the field, sold $31M in 2025." ⚠️ Don't say "through Wayroo": the share of the $31M on Wayroo's payment rails is unconfirmed (`my-gtm-context.md`).
- **Jordan Essentials:** live on ByDesign and Wayroo since July 2026. No sales or adoption numbers (no rep sale was on record as of 2026-11-01).
- **References:** Healthy Home, J.Hilburn, Benevita, available for a call.
**Design:** One big number or quote, logos underneath. Leave a slot for the LivePURE case study (due 2027-04-15).
**Speaker notes:** Offer the reference call here, not at the end. PlanNet asked for references after its demo.

### A9 — What's next for the field (2027)
**Copy:** *Shipping in 2027:* Dropship from your catalog (Q2) · returns and exchanges · rep discounts and promo codes · inventory checks. "You'll get them as they ship."
**Speaker notes:** Only items on the roadmap; no dates beyond the quarter. Check Feature Pulse the week of the call.

### A10 — Next steps
**Copy:** *Three steps, 30 days.* 1) Technical discovery with your IT lead (60 min). 2) Migration plan and comp-plan review. 3) Proposal, with Wayroo included.
**Speaker notes:** Book step 1 before the call ends, with a date. Send the one-pager within the hour.

# Deck B — Wayroo, powered by ByDesign (DSOs that keep their back office)

### B1 — Title
**Copy:** *Wayroo: your back office, in every rep's pocket.* Subline: "Powered by ByDesign." [Prospect logo]
**Speaker notes:** Say early: "You keep your back office. Wayroo connects to it." That removes the switching fear in the first minute.

### B2 — Their world
**Copy:** *Your reps run their business on their phone. Mostly on apps you can't see.* Square for payments · Facebook for selling · notes and spreadsheets for inventory · $60–100 a month out of their own pocket.
**Speaker notes:** For LivePURE-type rooms with a field leader present, let the leader describe a rep's week.

### B3 — The problem
**Copy:** *Sales happen off your rails.* No record of rep sales or customers · stock reps carry is invisible · new reps pay for tools before they earn.

### B4 — The cost of doing nothing
**Copy:** *Every month, more of the field's business happens somewhere else.* Lost customer data · compliance with no paper trail · reps who stall before their first sale.

### B5 — The solution
**Copy:** *Wayroo connects to the back office you already run.*
- **Sell anywhere:** tap to pay on the rep's phone, invoices with payment links, a storefront.
- **Inventory that keeps itself:** it loads from the wholesale order and updates as reps sell.
- **See the business:** sales, downline and KPIs on the phone.
- **Grow the field:** training, community and content from Aice, inside Wayroo.
**Speaker notes:** Lead the field leader with tap to pay. Lead the home office with visibility. Never lead the home office with tap to pay (house rule).

### B6 — How it works
1. **We connect Wayroo to your back office** (integration scoped in discovery).
2. **Your brand on the app:** branding available (scope in the proposal).
3. **Reps download and sell:** inventory, payments and payouts in one app.
4. **You see the field:** every sale and customer, back to you.
**Speaker notes:** Don't promise integration timing before technical discovery. LivePURE runs Concordia; every back office differs.

### B7 — Proof
**Copy:** Paparazzi *(sign-off required, ~20% of the field)* · Jordan Essentials · the LivePURE story from Q2.
**Speaker notes:** If the room asks about adoption, be straight: "Adoption is the work. Here's how we run it with you." Then go to B8.

### B8 — The beta *(only once the offer is approved, D8 due 2027-01-15)*
**Copy:** *Start with a paid beta. Get it back when you go live.* A beta fee, credited against year one when you launch and your reps start selling. Includes the integration, a rep launch plan and a named success lead.
**Speaker notes:** Until D8 is decided, replace this slide with "Pilot terms in the proposal."

### B9 — Next steps
**Copy:** 1) Technical discovery on your back office. 2) A demo in your branding. 3) Beta proposal.
**Speaker notes:** Offer a demo in their own branding. LivePURE asked for one (scheduled 2026-12-02 to 04); update this note with how it landed.

## Shared: discovery before either deck (five questions)

1. Which back office do you run, and when does that contract renew? *(Picks the deck.)*
2. How many active reps, and what share sell in person?
3. What do your reps use today to take payment and track inventory?
4. What can't the home office see that it needs to?
5. Who else decides: IT, finance, the field leaders?

## Shared: objection handling (acknowledge → explore → respond)

| Objection | Explore | Respond |
|---|---|---|
| "Migration is too risky." (A) | "What went wrong last time?" | Named migration lead, a plan for the first commission run, a reference call with a recent mover |
| "We're not switching back offices." (B) | "What would you keep, no matter what?" | "Good. Wayroo connects to it." Then switch to Deck B |
| "Our reps won't use another app." | "Which apps do they use now?" | Wayroo replaces several of them, and inventory is already loaded on first open |
| "Exigo already has a rep app." | "What do your reps do in it today?" | Show depth (inventory from the wholesale order, payments, one record per sale). Never claim Exigo lacks an app |
| "What does it cost?" | "How do you fund rep tools today?" | Pricing in the proposal; the beta credits back when you go live (once D8 is approved) |
| "Is Aice part of you?" | "What do you know about Aice?" | "Aice is our partner. Its training and community run inside Wayroo." |
