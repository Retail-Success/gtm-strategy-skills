# 1. Boards — the migration interview

**Not a purchase. A conversation, and it is overdue.**

Ask Hope or Nancy:
1. *"What did Boards do for your reps?"*
2. 🔴 *"What made you decide to move off it?"*
3. *"What did reps like about it that they'll miss?"*
4. *"What did it cost, and who paid — you or the reps?"*
5. *"How did reps react when you announced the change?"* → **directly informs our own switching-cost story**
6. *"Is there anything Boards did that Wayroo doesn't?"*

**Question 6 is the one people skip and the one that matters.** A defection interview that produces only reasons-we-won is a flattering interview, not a useful one.

---

# 2. Hustle — the $5/active-rep incumbent

**Two routes:** ask Paparazzi directly, or get a rep to walk through it on a screen share.

**Capture:** feature list · what reps actually use daily · does it touch inventory, payments, or the back office · how it is sold to the DSO · what reps would miss.

**The commercial question, and it is worth real money:**
> 🔴 *"Would you drop Hustle if Wayroo + Aice covered it?"*

Paparazzi pays ~$5 × ~20,000 monthly-active reps. **Nobody has asked.**

---

# 3. Square — the free benchmark

**Sign up as a rep would. Time every step.**

| Step | Capture |
|---|---|
| Signup → first sale possible | **Minutes. This is the bar Wayroo's first-run must beat.** |
| Inventory setup | **Exactly how good is the spreadsheet upload?** The "inventory is not a differentiator" correction rests on this. |
| Taking a payment | Tap-to-phone flow, fees, deposit timing |
| Reporting | What a rep can see |
| **What it cannot do** | Anything about the DSO — **the whole Wayroo argument** |

> ### The specific thing to settle
>
> Our positioning asserts *"Square does basic inventory well."* **Nobody at ByDesign has verified that first-hand.** If it is better than we think, the sync argument needs sharpening. If worse, we have been conceding ground we did not need to.
