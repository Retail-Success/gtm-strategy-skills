### §A2 — Chad meeting (Fri 6/19) — the gating conversation

Brian's partner **Chad** has his own stake and his own goal, and we don't yet know it. The single question to answer: **does Chad want to exit at the merge, or come along into the JV?** His answer reshapes the structure before Morgan can firm up the split.

**Why it gates everything:**
- **Chad exits** → his stake must be valued and bought out (cash or equity); changes the swap math and who funds the buyout.
- **Chad comes along** → his stake rolls into the combined entity; affects the cap table, dilution, and Aice's minority %.
- Either way, the **equity-swap ratio and governance can't be finalized** until we know which.

**Objectives for the meeting:**
1. Read Chad's intent honestly — exit vs. come-along — without forcing a premature commitment.
2. If exit: understand his expectations (timing, valuation basis, cash vs. equity).
3. If come-along: understand the role/involvement he wants post-merge.
4. Confirm Brian and Chad are aligned — surface any daylight between the two founders early.

**Tone:** This is discovery, not a pitch. Chad hasn't been in the room; treat it like the founder-to-founder rapport build Scott/Shannon ran with Brian. Don't pressure toward an answer — the value is an honest read.

**Output:** Chad's intent + expectations, fed directly into §B (structure) and §D (open items) before the economics session.

### Modeling notes

- **Equity-swap ratio** is the central unknown — it hinges on a relative valuation of Aice vs. the Wayroo/ByDesign contribution. The nine motions are the *revenue case* for what the combined entity is worth; the POS/payments rails + processor economics are the *capability case* for why Aice's standalone valuation understates the combined value.
- **Payments rev-share** is the highest-LTV line and the clearest "only-possible-combined" value — make sure it's modeled as recurring transaction-volume revenue, not a one-time bump.
- The **25% Aice payments rev-share cap** from the prior per-motion framing is now moot at the entity level (all revenue shared via ownership) — but it's still the reference point for what Aice would have earned standalone, useful for showing the JV upside vs. the partnership alternative.

## §E — Risks / Watch-Items

- **Founder cold-feet toward a clean exit.** The longer the structure stays abstract, the more attractive a simple acquisition looks again. Speed on the model is the mitigation — get a numbers-backed next touch in front of Brian quickly.
- **Valuation gap.** Brian may anchor Aice's value on the ~100k-user headline; we anchor on engaged/transacting value + what the combined entity unlocks. Close the intel asks (§C) before debating a number.
- **Governance friction.** "BD majority" + "Brian leads the brand" must be reconciled into something he experiences as real control over the brand and product, not a figurehead role. Get specific in §B item 4–5.
