# Phase 12 — Video Selling Playbook

**Phase:** 12 — Executing Sales, Task 7
**Purpose:** Personalized video as a sales channel — cuts through inbox noise where text alone fails. A 60–90 second personalized Loom in a cold outreach sequence consistently outperforms text-only follow-ups.
**Last updated:** May 11, 2026
**Derives from:** `outputs/12-outbound-campaign.md`, `outputs/12-sales-deck.md`, all `outputs/06-*` Phase 6 work
**Status:** v1. Three scripts (cold outreach + post-demo follow-up + stuck deal reactivation). Update after 30+ videos sent and view-rate data collected.

## Where Video Fits in the Wayroo Sales Process

| Use Case | When | Why Video Beats Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold outreach (replace Email 3 or 4)** | After 2 emails + LinkedIn touchpoints have surfaced no reply | Pattern interrupt — most prospects haven't watched a personalized video from a vendor; the novelty earns 5–10 seconds of attention you wouldn't get from email 5 |
| **Post-demo follow-up** | Within 2 hours of a Wayroo demo | Memorability — a 90-second recap video of "here's what we covered" outperforms a written summary by retention rate; gives champion something to share internally |
| **Stuck deal reactivation** | After 14+ days of silence from a deal that was active | Lower pressure than a "just checking in" email; personal video signals real human attention vs. drip-campaign automation |
| **Proposal walkthrough** | Sending a Wayroo proposal | Show effort — recording yourself walking through the proposal builds trust vs. emailing a PDF; champion can re-watch and share |
| **Technical scoping prep** (CTO-facing) | Before the Path B integration scoping call | Pre-loads the architecture conversation; CTO can watch async; saves call time |

## Wayroo Video Framework (60–90 Seconds Max)

| Segment | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–5 sec | Say their name. Reference one specific thing about them or their company that proves you researched. |
| **Context** | 5–20 sec | Why you're reaching out. What pattern you've noticed at their company or in their segment. |
| **Value** | 20–60 sec | One specific way Wayroo helps. **Show, don't tell** — share screen of the Wayroo admin portal, a relevant feature, or a screenshot of relevant data (e.g., a competitor pricing page, a public review of their current rep tool). Use one talk-track from the messaging house. |
| **CTA** | 60–75 sec | One clear ask. Specific time options if asking for a meeting. Calendar link in the chat below. |
| **Sign-off** | 75–90 sec | Friendly close. Wave goodbye if showing face. Reinforce that this took 90 seconds, not 30 minutes. |

**Hard rules:**
- **Maximum 90 seconds.** Anything longer loses attention. If you can't say it in 90 seconds, it's two videos.
- **Show your face for at least the hook and CTA.** Webcam-only videos outperform screen-only by ~2x in retention. Hide your face during screen-share segments — that's fine.
- **One CTA. Just one.** "Want a 20-minute call?" not "Want a call, or a deck, or a demo?"
- **Send within 2 hours of recording.** Freshness matters — recipient psychology is "they made this for me just now."

## Three Video Scripts

### Script 1 — Cold Outreach (Daniel-Led)

**Use:** Replace Email 3 in the new-logo outbound sequence (per `outputs/12-outbound-campaign.md` Flow 1, Day 10) when you've already sent Email 1 + Email 2 with no reply.

**Audience:** CEO / Founder / VP Sales at a Tier A or B prospect from `outputs/12-dream-client-list.md`. Example: Trades of Hope CEO.

**Pre-recording prep (5 min):**
- Open prospect's website on screen — main page + careers page if hiring rep ops roles
- Open prospect's LinkedIn — leadership profiles + any recent posts
- Write prospect first name on a notepad — hold up briefly in the hook
- Have one specific recent thing about them ready (product launch, leadership change, milestone)

**Script outline (with talk-track):**

**Hook (0–5 sec) — webcam on full screen, holding notepad with their first name:**
> "Hey [First Name] — quick 90-second video for you, specifically. [Holds notepad.] I saw [specific recent thing — product launch, leadership announcement, conference appearance, rep milestone]."

**Context (5–20 sec) — webcam on:**
> "I work with mid-market direct selling orgs on rep-tool consolidation. We've been seeing a pattern at companies your size: your reps are running their business across 4 or 5 disconnected tools — Square, Excel, Venmo, maybe Shopify — and the fragmentation becomes the bottleneck for activation and retention. Not the 'rep training problem' everyone names."

**Value (20–60 sec) — screen share starts; webcam in corner:**
> "Let me show you what I mean — [share screen of Wayroo admin portal, or a screenshot of the Paparazzi case study stat]. This is one of our deployments. 4,000 active selling stylists generated $31 million in 2025 — 60%+ year over year growth. The headline isn't the revenue. It's that consolidating their reps onto one platform directly cut churn and accelerated time-to-first-sale. The reps stopped losing the patchwork, and corporate started seeing every customer they sold to."

**CTA (60–75 sec) — screen share off; back to full-screen webcam:**
> "Worth a 20-minute conversation to compare notes? I have Thursday afternoon or Friday morning open this week. Calendar link is in the chat below. Or just hit reply with a time."

**Sign-off (75–90 sec) — webcam, friendly close:**
> "[First Name], appreciate the 90 seconds. Hope to talk soon."

**Email body (one sentence + Loom link):**
> Hi [First Name] — 90 second video for you about a pattern I'm seeing at companies your size: [Loom link]. — Daniel

### Script 2 — Post-Demo Follow-Up (Autumn-Led)

**Use:** Send within 2 hours of completing a Wayroo demo. Better than a written follow-up email at retention + champion-sharing.

**Audience:** Whoever was on the demo (CEO, CTO, Founder, VP Sales). Solo or composite depending on attendees.

**Pre-recording prep (3 min):**
- Open the 2–3 most relevant Wayroo screens that were demoed
- Have your notes from the call open (key things they said, what resonated)
- Write their first name + company on a notepad

**Script outline:**

**Hook (0–5 sec) — webcam:**
> "Hey [First Name] — Autumn. Wanted to send a quick 90-second recap from our demo this morning."

**Context (5–25 sec) — webcam:**
> "Three things stood out from our conversation. First, [specific thing they said — e.g., 'your point about the rep onboarding process being a disaster']. Second, [the feature that resonated most — e.g., 'your reaction to the customer record auto-creation flowing to your back office']. And third, [the specific next-step concern — e.g., 'wanting to make sure the integration story holds before going further']."

**Value (25–70 sec) — screen share starts:**
> "I want to show you one thing I didn't get to in detail. [Screen-share the most relevant Wayroo screen for their specific concern — e.g., the receiving tab that shows wholesale orders auto-populating; the admin portal showing rep-level visibility; the consultant cost reimbursement screen.] [30-second walkthrough — show, don't tell.] This is exactly what [specific stylist behavior they care about] looks like in the platform today."

**CTA (70–85 sec) — webcam:**
> "Next step on our side is [specific next step — e.g., 'getting your CTO on a 30-minute technical scoping call to map the Path B integration']. I've got [day/time options] proposed in the calendar invite I just sent. Or if you want to bring someone else in first, just reply and I'll adjust."

**Sign-off (85–90 sec):**
> "Appreciate the time today, [First Name]. Hit reply with anything I should clarify."

**Email body (one sentence + Loom link):**
> Hi [First Name] — 90-second recap from our demo this morning + one thing I didn't get to: [Loom link]. — Autumn

### Script 3 — Stuck Deal Reactivation

**Use:** When a deal has gone dark for 14+ days after active engagement. The Wayroo CEO/CTO has stopped responding to email and LinkedIn.

**Audience:** Whoever was your primary contact during the active deal phase. Often the CEO or Founder.

**Pre-recording prep (3 min):**
- Re-read your last 3 touchpoints with them (what was the last topic discussed?)
- Identify ONE new thing — a relevant industry article, a competitor announcement, a Wayroo product update, a new proof point — that gives you a legitimate non-pestering reason to reach out
- Write their first name on the notepad

**Script outline:**

**Hook (0–5 sec) — webcam:**
> "Hey [First Name] — wanted to send a 60-second video instead of another email."

**Context (5–25 sec) — webcam:**
> "I know we left things at [last specific topic — 'wanting to align with your CFO before moving to proposal' or 'getting clarity on your convention timeline']. I haven't heard back, which probably means either timing's off, priorities shifted, or this isn't the right fit. All three are completely valid. Not pushing — just want to give you a clean way to tell me which one it is."

**Value (25–55 sec) — screen share or stays on webcam, depending on context:**
> "One thing I want to share that's actually new: [the legitimate non-pestering reason — e.g., 'we just had Brian B at Color Street agree to a case study after their reunion launch in August' or 'Aice just announced [thing] — wanted you to see how it changes the competitive landscape for what we discussed']. Took 30 seconds to share that because it directly impacts the conversation we were having."

**CTA (55–75 sec) — webcam:**
> "Two options. One — 15-minute call to revisit, no pressure to pick up where we left off. Two — just reply 'not now, check back in 90 days' and I'll do exactly that. Either works for me. Honest answer is the helpful one."

**Sign-off (75–90 sec):**
> "Either way, [First Name], appreciate the time you've already given. Talk soon."

**Email body (one sentence + Loom link):**
> Hi [First Name] — 60-second video to give you a clean way to tell me where this lands: [Loom link]. — [Sender]

## Production Tips

### Visual setup

- **Camera:** Webcam at eye level (not below). External webcam better than laptop built-in if available.
- **Lighting:** Face a window if possible. Avoid backlighting (window behind you = silhouette).
- **Background:** Clean / neutral. Avoid distracting clutter, but a real office is fine — sterile virtual backgrounds look fake and reduce trust.
- **Notepad with prospect name:** Write large with marker. Hold up briefly during hook. This single move is the most effective personalization signal video provides.

### Audio

- **Headset / external mic > laptop mic.** Better audio is more important than better video.
- **Quiet space.** Background noise destroys retention faster than visual issues.

### Energy

- **Speak 10% slower than feels natural.** Recorded video feels rushed when watched back.
- **Smile briefly at start and end.** Even if your message is serious.
- **Stand up if energy is flat.** Standing on calls and recordings improves vocal projection and energy.

### Editing

- **Don't edit.** Loom raw is the format. Editing signals "produced" which feels less personal. One take is fine; if you flub badly, re-record once.
- **Trim only the beginning** if there's awkward silence before you start talking. That's it.

### Sending

- **Send within 2 hours of recording.** Freshness matters psychologically.
- **Email subject line:** Reference video explicitly. *"Quick 90-second video for you, [First Name]"* outperforms generic subject lines for video opens.
- **Email body:** One sentence + link. Don't summarize the video in the email — that defeats the purpose.

## Performance Metrics

Track per video. Loom shows view data natively.

| Metric | Target | What It Tells You |
|---|---|---|
| **Open rate** (email containing video link) | 50–70% | Subject line + sender reputation + curiosity from "video" mention |
| **Video click rate** (% who clicked Loom link from email) | 25–40% | Email body + subject line + curiosity |
| **Video play rate** (% of clickers who actually pressed play) | 80%+ | If lower, the thumbnail/preview is off-putting; rerecord with better start frame |
| **Watch-through rate** (% who watched 80%+ of video) | 50–70% for cold outreach; 70–85% for post-demo follow-up | Quality of content + length discipline |
| **Reply rate** | 15–25% (cold outreach video); 50–70% (post-demo); 30–40% (stuck deal reactivation) | The headline outcome metric |

**If watch-through is below target:** the video is too long, or the hook didn't land. Rerecord shorter, sharper hook.

**If reply rate is below target despite good watch-through:** the CTA is unclear or asking for too much. Simplify to one specific ask with specific time options.

**Daniel's first-30-days target:**
- Send 20 cold outreach videos (replace email 3 across the first 20 Tier A new-logo accounts)
- 15+ video click rate (75%+)
- 12+ play rate (80%+)
- 3–5 replies (15–25% reply rate)
- 1–2 booked meetings from video specifically

If the 30-day data shows video reply rate > 2x email-only reply rate, scale to all Tier A accounts and add to Tier B for highest-priority subset.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Video longer than 90 seconds | Watch-through drops sharply past 90 sec | Re-script with strict 90-sec cap; or split into 2 videos |
| Reading from a script word-for-word | Sounds robotic; loses trust signal video uniquely provides | Use bullet points behind camera; speak conversationally |
| No clear CTA at end | Prospect doesn't know what to do; defaults to nothing | One specific ask with specific time options. Calendar link in chat. |
| Generic opener ("I came across your website...") | Reveals zero research; identical to spam | Reference one specific thing about them — product, leadership, milestone, recent press |
| Screen-share only (no face) | Misses the human-connection advantage of video | Show face for hook + CTA at minimum |
| Polished / over-produced | Reduces "made just for me" signal | Loom raw — no editing, one take, no intro graphics |
| Same script copy-pasted to multiple prospects | Defeats the entire purpose; recipients can tell | Each video genuinely personalized to its recipient; if you can't, send text instead |
| Recording without a CTA pre-decided | You'll ramble at the end | Write the CTA before you press record |
| Sending to wrong inbox / spam | Wasted effort | Verify email accuracy before sending; track bounce + open rate per send |

## When NOT to Use Video

- **First touchpoint cold outreach.** Video too soon feels invasive. Email + LinkedIn first; video at touchpoint 3 or 4.
- **Highly regulated industries with privacy concerns** (financial services, healthcare DSOs). Most DSO segments are fine, but verify if prospect handles sensitive data.
- **Prospects who explicitly opted out of video** (rare but happens — respect it immediately).
- **When you're tired / distracted / having a bad day.** A flat-energy video damages the relationship more than no video. Send text that day; record fresh tomorrow.
- **When the message requires nuance / negotiation** (e.g., closing terms, contract red-lines). Use video for discovery, demos, recap, and reactivation — not for back-and-forth deal mechanics.
