> **Method.** The 10-element GTM diagnostic. Each element gets the current best answer plus a confidence flag. **Blank and Low-confidence spots are the output** — they are where the work is.

## 1. Target Customer

**The rep.** Independent seller inside a ByDesign client organisation, US, on Revolution Pro. **75,000 of them across 55 active clients.** Runs their business across 6–10 apps at $60–100/month of their own money.

**Secondary: the home office** — not a buyer, not an approver. **A ProPay unlocker.** One ask, and it is the only thing standing between an adopted rep and a transacting one.

**Confidence: HIGH** on who · ⚠️ **LOW on how many are addressable** — US-only is deliberate, and the US slice of the 75,000 has never been quantified. Every sizing number in the plan is gross, not addressable.

## 2. Problem

**The rep pays $60–100 every month, out of pocket, for 6–10 apps that do not talk to each other** — and none of them know what she ordered from corporate or tell corporate what she sold. She reconciles by hand and pays for the privilege.

**The felt version, observed at 764 reps:** she loses the sale when the customer has no cash.

**Confidence: HIGH.** Directly observed at the JE convention; cross-validated on admin burden at 4 accounts.

## 3. Solution

**A free selling app that is already connected to the company she sells for** — her wholesale order and inventory arrive pre-loaded, her back-office numbers are on her phone, and card payments run on her own merchant account.

**Confidence: HIGH** on the product · ⚠️ **MEDIUM on delivery** — selling tools ship as a set and are gated on DSO ProPay approval.

## 4. Value Proposition

**Rep:** *Everything you sell, from one app you already pay for — with your inventory already in it.*

**Home office:** *Better-equipped reps, field visibility you have never had, and margin on every rep who subscribes — for the price of saying yes.*

**Confidence: MEDIUM.** Reasoned and internally coherent. 🔴 **Zero rep or prospect validation.** Nobody outside the company has reacted to either line.

## 5. Pricing

**$0 to the rep**, included with a Revolution Pro subscription they already hold. **$0 to the DSO.** ByDesign earns **~1.5% of every transaction** — and under this motion payments is not the majority of revenue, it is the entirety of it.

Non-Pro reps: **$5/month for Revolution Pro, Wayroo included.** Only paid Wayroo surface is white-label — **and the pilot gives it away three times at a stated $25,000 value.**

**Confidence: HIGH** on structure · ⚠️ **LOW on the rung-2 upgrade** (Wayroo Pro + Aice Pro), which is unpriced and gates the Aice free/paid line.

## 6. Channels

> ### 🔴 One channel matters and it did not exist in the plan until 2026-08-30.

**Revolution in-product placement.** 75,000 logged-in, pre-qualified reps inside software ByDesign owns. **No permission needed, no media spend, deterministic targeting from back-office data.** Full design: [`08-channel-strategy.md`](08-channel-strategy.md).

Secondary: the field-leader growth loop (direct selling is a pre-built referral network) · conventions and field events · Aice as a paired front door.

**Confidence: HIGH that it is the right channel** · 🔴 **UNKNOWN on conversion.** The single largest open number in the plan — and it is now the binding constraint on the whole motion.

## 7. Competition

| | |
|---|---|
| **The real one** | 🔴 **Doing nothing.** Cash, Venmo, inventory tracked by memory. The majority behaviour. **The competitor is a habit, not a product.** |
| **Square** | Free, genuinely good, mature. **Never compete on price here** — Square is $0 too. |
| **Hustle** | $5/monthly-active-rep. **Paparazzi pays it across ~20,000 reps.** |
| **Boards** | Incumbent at JE, being displaced now |

**Confidence: HIGH on Square and the status quo** · ⚠️ **MEDIUM-LOW on Hustle and Boards** — Media Library is a partial answer, Aice is a full one post-merger, but **no feature-level teardown exists.**

## 8. Differentiation

> **Wayroo is the only selling app already connected to the company the rep sells for.**

One claim, structural, and no competitor reaches it without building an integration to ByDesign.

⚠️ **Two things that are NOT the differentiator:** inventory features (Square does them fine — **the sync is the differentiator, not the feature**) and price against Square.

**Confidence: HIGH.** The cleanest claim in the library, and it is provable on first open.

## 9. First 10 Customers

**Already have them** — that is what makes this motion different from every other Power Hour.

1. **Jordan Essentials** — live, 764 adopters, ProPay done, Tap-to-Pay trial in 2–3 weeks
2. **Paparazzi** — 4,000 on Wayroo, **~16,000 un-adopted, ProPay cleared, and paying Hustle $5/rep.** The densest addressable concentration in the base.
3–5. **The three pilot DSOs** — free Wayroo + free branding, selected on ProPay-approvability across a deliberate three-slot portfolio
6–10. Ranked by **demand-counter density**, once the counter ships

**Confidence: HIGH on 1 and 2** · **MEDIUM on the pilot** — candidates not yet named.

## 10. Key Metric

> ### **Transacting reps × GMV per transacting rep.**

A rep is worth **$0 signed · $36 dormant · ~$216 transacting.** Downloads and MAU are the wrong numbers; they get reported because they are what exists.

**Confidence: HIGH on the definition** · 🔴 **the metric has never been measured.**

# The Top 3 Gaps

> ### 1. 🔴 Two engineering events do not exist, and they gate everything.
> `transaction_completed` and the demand-counter tap. Without the first there is no revenue metric. Without the second **there is no way to open a DSO conversation at all**, because permission no longer provides one. **Both are low-effort. Neither has shipped.**

> ### 2. 🔴 The channel conversion rate is unknown, and it is now the binding constraint.
> Acquisition moved from a permission problem to a channel problem. **Nobody knows whether the Revolution placement converts.** Every forecast assumes it does.

> ### 3. 🔴 The entire evidence base is one account at 0.34% of the audience.
> Jordan Essentials. No message testing has ever run. The catalog-only rep — on whom the Aice pairing depends — **has never been observed.**

**All three are cheap to close.** Two are tickets, one is a placement test, one is ten conversations with reps who are already on the phone.
