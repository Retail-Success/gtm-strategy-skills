# Phase 6 — Persona Positioning: Wayroo Essentials (Land-and-Expand)

**Phase:** 6 — Crafting Positioning (persona layer)
**Date:** 2026-08-30 · **Owner:** Sam Atieh
**Derives from:** [`06-positioning-statement.md`](06-positioning-statement.md) · [`06-messaging-house.md`](06-messaging-house.md)
**Evidence base:** [`prospects/color-street.md`](../prospects/color-street.md) (May 1 demo + May 6 technical scoping) · [`prospects/jordan-essentials.md`](../prospects/jordan-essentials.md) (July 17–18 convention launch, ~764 adopters observed)
**Status:** v1. Every persona is grounded in a named, dated reaction from one of two accounts. **Nothing here is message-tested.**

> **What this document adds.** The master positioning resolves the audience asymmetry — reps are led with Tap-to-Pay, the home office never is. That rule is correct and this document does not touch it. But it treats *"the rep"* and *"the home office"* as two undifferentiated blocks, and the evidence from Color Street and Jordan Essentials shows each is at least four distinct people who need different first sentences.
>
> **The three findings that motivated it:**
>
> 1. **The technical gatekeeper persona is missing entirely** — from the master, the messaging house, and the DSO launch kit. The category claim is *"the selling app that comes with your back office,"* which is an **integration claim**, and the person who adjudicates integration claims is a CTO. Color Street's Edmond Kim is the worked example, and he nearly killed the deal over it.
> 2. **Downline visibility is a different persona, not a different feature.** It was a top-tier rep reaction at the JE convention, and it is one of only two hero capabilities that **work in the locked state**. That makes the team-leader rep the highest-yield target at any account waiting on ProPay — which no current asset says.
> 3. **A large share of any rep base will never transact.** Color Street's CEO volunteered 80/20. Under a motion whose only revenue is payments, counting them is a forecasting error, not a rounding error.

# How to use this

Each persona gives you **one first sentence, one thing never to say, and the objection you will actually get.** Find the persona, take the row, then use [`06-messaging-house.md`](06-messaging-house.md) for the full copy.

**The beat assignment is binding.** A persona assigned to beat 3 must not be approached at beat 1 — that is the sequencing rule from the master positioning, applied per person rather than per audience block.

> ### 🔴 Beat assignments revised 2026-08-30 — the approval beat was deleted
>
> **DSO permission is not required to reach reps.** Wayroo is advertised directly inside the Freedom and Revolution back offices, and **inventory plus dashboard widgets work on download with zero DSO involvement.** Three beats became two: **Beat 1 — Adoption (reps, reached directly)** and **Beat 2 — Unlock (home office, ProPay approval)**, plus a parallel **Pilot** track for 3 named DSOs.
>
> **Every H-persona moved.** They are no longer met at an approval conversation, because there isn't one. **They are met at the ProPay conversation, which the field opens on our behalf.**

| | Reps (ICP A — adopters) | Home office (ICP B — **ProPay unlockers**) |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** In-Person Seller | **Beat 1** | **H1** CEO / Founder · **Beat 2** *(+ Pilot)* |
| **R2** Team Leader | **Beat 1** | **H2** CTO / Technical Gatekeeper · **Beat 2 — and it gates the payments line** |
| **R3** Catalog-Only Rep | **Beat 1** | **H3** VP Sales / Field Ops · **Beat 2** *(+ Pilot)* |
| **R4** Hobbyist | **Not a target** | **H4** Finance / Ops · **Beat 2 only** |

> **The H-personas got harder to reach and easier to convince.** No permission conversation means no scheduled opener — but when the conversation does happen it opens with *"forty-seven of your reps are asking,"* which is a materially stronger position than *"may we offer your field a free app."*

# ⚠️ The Revolution Pro overlay — read before using any rep persona

**Pro status is orthogonal to persona.** A rep of any type either holds Revolution Pro or does not, and that single fact changes the commercial sentence completely while leaving the value sentence untouched.

| | **Holds Pro** | **Does not hold Pro** |
|---|---|---|
| Population | The 75,000 launch audience | JE's 510, and the equivalent at every other account |
| Commercial line | **"Included. You already pay for it."** | **"It's $5 a month, and here's what it replaces."** |
| Value line | **Identical — use the persona row below** | **Identical — use the persona row below** |

**Do not build eight persona × two Pro-status variants.** The persona determines what you lead with; Pro status determines only the closing sentence. Pillar 3a/3b in the messaging house already handles that branch — use it and change nothing else.

🚫 **Never give the "included" line to a rep without Pro.** Per [`06-messaging-house.md`](06-messaging-house.md) §2, this is the fastest available way to lose the audience.

# ICP A — The Rep (primary; the adopter)

## R1 — The In-Person Seller

**The stylist at the vendor table.** Carries personal inventory, sells at events, markets, pop-ups, and parties. Currently on Square or Venmo plus a spreadsheet.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | **Cross-account, and the strongest evidence in the library.** Color Street: ~70% of real-selling stylists carry personal inventory; Brian B on rep fragmentation — *"if they're sophisticated, they'll set up their own GoDaddy account... just manually connecting, texting, and then figuring out a way to get it to someone... PayPal, Venmo, that kind of stuff."* Jordan Essentials, July 17–18: **Tap-to-Pay drew the single strongest reaction of the entire launch**, ahead of every other feature shown. |
| **Their job** | Do not lose the sale standing in front of them |
| 🔴 **Lead with** | **Tap-to-Pay.** Then pre-loaded inventory. |
| 🚫 **Never** | Compliance · corporate visibility · "the company can see" · anything about brand control |
| **The objection** | *"I already have Square and it's free."* → Square does not know your wholesale order, does not fill your inventory from corporate, and does not put your customer into the company's system. **Per the master positioning, price is not the argument against Square — integration is the whole argument.** |
| **Confidence** | **HIGH** — two accounts, one directly observed at ~764-rep scale |

> ⚠️ **This persona is systematically undercounted, in both directions, at every account where it has been checked.** JE's ~20% C&C estimate was confirmed understated at launch; Color Street's nominal 20K "active stylists" turned out to be ~4K real sellers. **DSO leadership does not reliably know its own in-person selling share.** Derive it from back-office data where ByDesign holds it (`my-gtm-context.md` §3) rather than accepting a self-report.

## R2 — The Team Leader 🔴 NEW

**The rep who builds and manages a downline.** Measured on team volume and team activity, not only personal sales. Often the account's most influential field voice.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | **Jordan Essentials convention, July 17–18 — direct observation.** *Downline dashboard reports* and *back-office data on the phone without a separate Freedom login* both landed in the top reaction tier, alongside Tap-to-Pay and invoicing. The May 6 record had captured dashboards as a **VP Sales** interest only; the convention confirmed the same appetite exists at rep level, and specifically for downline data. |
| **Their job** | Know what their team is doing without logging into a browser |
| 🔴 **Lead with** | **Your downline, on your phone.** Then the rest of the back-office data. |
| 🚫 **Never** | POS · barcode scanning · vendor-event framing — unless they also sell in person, which many do |
| **The objection** | *"I can already see that in the back office."* → On a desktop, after logging in. Not standing in a hotel lobby at convention, which is where you actually need it. |
| **Confidence** | **MEDIUM** — one account, but directly observed rather than reported |

> ### 🔴 This is the highest-yield persona at a locked account — and two downstream assets already act on it without Phase 6 ever naming the person.
>
> Per [`06-positioning-statement.md`](06-positioning-statement.md), **two of the four strongest JE rep reactions work with the selling tools dark** — dashboard widgets and phone access to back-office data. Both belong to this persona.
>
> **It is already operationalised in two places:**
> - [`07-rep-launch-kit.md`](07-rep-launch-kit.md) §1 puts dashboard widgets at step 2b of first run and notes explicitly that they work with ProPay pending.
> - [`10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md`](10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md) §4 builds its entire adoption loop on the field leader, recommends surfacing **"% of your downline transacting,"** and makes activating leaders first the highest-leverage step in the playbook.
>
> **What was missing is the persona itself.** Because Phase 6 never named this person, they have no row in the messaging-house audience table and no presence in the DSO launch kit — so the enablement layer acts on the insight while the messaging layer has no words for it.
>
> **Consequence for sequencing:** at any account where ProPay onboarding is incomplete, the team leader is the one rep who gets the full product on day one. Everyone else meets a wait. **Lead the locked-account rollout with team leaders** — they convert immediately, they are the field's most credible voices, and they are the population most likely to generate the beat-3 pressure that unlocks everyone else.
>
> This also matters because the back-office data pull is **Wayroo-exclusive and never moves to Aice** (`my-gtm-context.md` §2). It is the capability that keeps Wayroo the front door, and this is the persona that feels it.

## R3 — The Catalog-Only Rep

**Sells from the DSO catalog, carries little or no personal inventory.** Earns commission on replicated-site sales, paid monthly.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | Inferred from segment structure (ICP 1c, `my-gtm-context.md` §3) and from the master positioning's own admission: *"Wayroo's commerce value serves the rep who sells their own inventory. At a field that is mostly catalog-only, the selling tools alone do not earn daily use."* **No direct observation at either account.** |
| **Their job** | Earn more, get paid sooner than the monthly commission cycle |
| 🔴 **Lead with** | **Invoicing with payment links**, then the storefront. **Then Aice** — for this persona the pair is the offer, not a bonus. |
| 🚫 **Never** | POS · barcode scanning · inventory sync — they have no inventory, and leading here tells them the product is not for them |
| **The objection** | *"I don't carry stock, so what is this for me?"* → Payment links for custom orders, your downline on your phone, and Aice for team and content. **Dropship is the real answer and it is not shipped** — do not promise it. |
| **Confidence** | 🔴 **LOW — the weakest-evidenced persona in this document.** Assembled from segment theory, not from a rep. |

> ⚠️ **This persona is the load-bearing assumption behind the Aice pairing**, and it has never been observed. The master positioning's case for launching both products together rests on Aice covering the catalog-only field during the ProPay wait. **If Aice's free tier is drawn too thin, the pair does not cover this persona and the retention bridge does not hold** — see [`05-aice-bundle-and-free-tier-pricing.md`](05-aice-bundle-and-free-tier-pricing.md) Q1. Validate this persona at pilot accounts 2 and 3.

## R4 — The Hobbyist ⛔ Not a target

**Signed up for the rep discount on personal purchases. Does not sell to customers and does not intend to.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | **Color Street, May 6 — CEO volunteered, unprompted.** Brian B: ***"80% of our people are just the hobbyists that buy for themselves. They want the 25% off their customer."*** Followed immediately by *"we will take that off of the calculation, right? Because it's never going to get resold."* |
| **Positioning** | **None. Do not write copy for this persona.** |
| **Why it is in this document** | So it is subtracted from the forecast rather than discovered later |
| **Confidence** | **LOW as a ratio** (one account) · **HIGH that the population exists** (the CEO named it against his own interest) |

> ### The forecasting consequence, which is the entire reason to name this persona
>
> Under Land-and-Expand **payments is not the majority of revenue — it is the entirety of it** (`my-gtm-context.md` §3). A hobbyist can download, hold Revolution Pro, and count as an adopter while generating **$0 forever**. They are not a slow adopter; they are outside the revenue model by construction.
>
> **Every adoption-rate denominator in the plan is at risk from this.** Color Street's 80/20 is a single-account disclosure and must not be applied to the 75,000 — but the direction of the error is known, and the correction is a discovery question, not an assumption:
>
> > *"What share of your reps sell to customers, versus buying at the rep discount for themselves?"*
>
> Pair it with the back-office-derived C&C measurement (§3) rather than trusting either self-report on its own. **Both self-reported rep figures have been wrong at both accounts where they could be checked.**

# ICP B — The Home Office (secondary; **ProPay unlocker**, not approver and not buyer)

> **Revised 2026-08-30. There is one ask, and it is not permission.** ByDesign does not need DSO approval to reach reps or a DSO channel to talk to them. **The only ask is ProPay approval, which unlocks the selling tools and therefore the entire revenue line.** Custom branding is a second, optional, commercial conversation — free to the three pilot DSOs at a stated $25,000 value.
>
> **Nobody in this section is being sold to, and none of them gate adoption.** Every line below has to survive both constraints.
>
> **The opener for all four is the same artifact:** the demand count from their own field. Without it there is no conversation, because nothing else initiates one.

## H1 — The CEO / Founder

**Economic approver, and frequently the field champion in the same person.** At smaller DSOs this is the entire buying committee.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | **Two accounts.** Color Street — Brian B reframed the entire product mid-call: *"I see this as a sales tool, ultimately, where, you know, it's how the field's using it and understanding how they can get in front of customers and sell more and make their lives easier."* Jordan Essentials — Nancy requested the paid start be **pulled forward** to September, cutting short her own free period after seeing the field reaction. |
| **Their job** | Field productivity, activation, retention — and margin |
| 🔴 **Lead with** | **"It costs you nothing, and it pays you."** The economic line: *Wayroo is the reason your reps buy Revolution Pro, and you make margin on every one of them.* Then better-equipped field. |
| 🚫 **Never** | **ProPay onboarding** (that is beat 3) · implementation detail · anything that resembles a project · **compliance as an opener** |
| **The objection** | *"Why would we add another rep app?"* → It is not another app. It replaces six to ten your reps already pay $60–100/month for, and it is the only one wired into the back office you already run. |
| **Confidence** | **HIGH** — two accounts, consistent behaviour |

> ### Two Color Street corrections that apply directly to this persona
>
> **1. This persona reframes operations into field activation, every time.** Brian did it repeatedly and unprompted — technical and support questions came back as *"how does the field use this."* **Do not fight the reframe; open inside it.** The May work drew the right conclusion from this and demoted FTC compliance from the lead to a late supporting argument. That conclusion survives the strategy reset intact and is now standing guidance (`my-gtm-context.md` §4: *"leading with regulatory risk reads as a threat rather than an offer"*).
>
> **2. This persona imposes scope discipline, and it is an asset.** Brian: *"if we want to get to something quick, this is really cool. We keep it simple. We keep it light. We just did a major integration. We're chasing a lot."* **A deliberately incomplete free product is exactly what this persona wants to hear about** — the locked state is not a weakness to apologise for, it is scope discipline they will recognise. Do not over-scope the ask.

> ⚠️ **White-label is the only remaining paid Wayroo surface — and this persona may not want it.** Brian, May 1: *"Function over fashion, like I mean, our ladies aren't like, you know... more if it was functional."* One account, but it is the only direct evidence on white-label appetite the library holds, and it points against the one thing still being sold. **Probe it; do not assume it.**

## H2 — The CTO / Technical Gatekeeper 🔴 NEW — absent from all current Phase 6 and 7 assets

**C-suite technical veto.** Owns the stack, the systems of record, and the answer to *"what happens to our data."* Not an IT manager — this person can stop the motion.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | **Color Street, Edmond Kim — the single best-documented technical objection in the library.** May 1: *"I am veering away from an isolated app. It's got to be within the lens of it being integrated into Shopify."* May 6, the reason underneath it: *"some of the data issues that we were having, like mismatches and whatnot, it's because there's two different sources of truth and we need to figure out like the timing issues or syncing issues."* And the resolution: *"we would replicate the API and just have it directed to your endpoint, so that makes sense."* |
| **Their job** | Not adding a third system of record |
| 🔴 **Lead with** | **Where the data lives, and how many copies of it exist.** Wayroo consumes from the back office; it does not become a competing source of truth. |
| 🚫 **Never** | Feature breadth · rep enthusiasm · adoption numbers · vague integration promises. **This persona reads "seamless integration" as an evasion.** |
| **The objection** | *"That's a second source of truth."* → **Path B.** Wayroo integrates at the system-of-record layer and consumes downstream; it does not introduce a parallel record. Edmond endorsed this architecture on May 6 once it was drawn correctly. |
| **Confidence** | **MEDIUM** — one account, but exceptionally well documented across two calls, and the objection is structural rather than account-specific |

> ### 🔴 Why this persona matters more under Land-and-Expand, not less
>
> The instinct is that a free product needs no technical sale. **That is backwards here**, for three reasons:
>
> **1. The category claim is an integration claim.** *"The selling app that comes with your back office"* is a technical assertion. Every other claim in the positioning — pre-loaded inventory, sales flowing back, same system as commissions — is downstream of it. **This persona is the one who decides whether that claim is true**, and USP 1 is the whole position.
>
> **2. ProPay onboarding is beat 3, and beat 3 routes through technical review** at any account with a CTO. The master positioning treats beat 3 as a paperwork step for the home office. At a Color Street-shaped account it is a stack decision, and there is currently no asset written for the person making it.
>
> **3. Wayroo integrates with Exigo and any other back office** (confirmed 2026-08-08). That is precisely the claim this persona will interrogate first, and it is load-bearing for the Wayroo-led entry thesis (`my-gtm-context.md` §14).
>
> ### Path B, stated properly — it belongs in the positioning library, not in one prospect file
>
> For a DSO running its own commission engine with a storefront in front of it, there are two ways to integrate, and only one survives this persona:
>
> | | **Path A — integrate at the storefront** | ✅ **Path B — integrate at the commission engine** |
> |---|---|---|
> | Wayroo's role | A third sync surface | **Downstream consumer of the existing record** |
> | CTO reading | *"Two sources of truth"* — the objection | **No new system of record** |
> | Scope | Larger, real-time, more failure modes | **Materially smaller** |
> | Verdict at Color Street | Blocked the deal on May 1 | **Endorsed by the CTO on May 6** |
>
> **This was flagged in May for formalisation as a supported Wayroo integration pattern and never was.** It is currently documented only inside [`prospects/color-street.md`](../prospects/color-street.md). It is reusable at any account with a custom or third-party back office — which, under a motion that claims back-office-agnostic integration, is the whole addressable frontier.

## H3 — The VP Sales / Field Operations

**Owns field performance.** Sees the decline before anyone else and is measured on reversing it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | **Jordan Essentials — Hope.** Named declining rep sales and engagement over a 6-month window as the "why now." On Wayroo dashboards pulling Freedom report data: *"important."* Drove the Media Library requirement to control JE branding. |
| **Their job** | Reverse the decline; see which reps are struggling before they quit |
| 🔴 **Lead with** | **Every rep sale becomes a record you can see.** Then proactive coaching — spot the stalled rep in week two rather than month six. |
| 🚫 **Never** | Payments mechanics · merchant accounts · processing rates. **That is H4's conversation.** |
| **The objection** | *"We already have reports."* → In the back office, pulled by you. Not on the rep's phone, and not showing you who has stopped selling. |
| **Confidence** | **MEDIUM** — one account, explicit and repeated |

> ### 🔴 The Media Library persona split — the sharpest persona finding the library holds
>
> **The same feature rated 🔴 High with this persona and 🔵 Low with reps at the same account on the same weekend.** Hope wants brand control; reps were visibly less excited by it than by anything else shown.
>
> **This is not a contradiction to resolve. It is the clearest proof that the persona layer is necessary** — a single "audience" reading would average these into a wrong answer for both.
>
> **Two operating rules:**
> - **Use Media Library in the DSO deck. Keep it out of rep first-run.** (Already correctly captured in the master positioning — this document explains why.)
> - ⚠️ **Its 2026 target attribution is misassigned.** `strategic-inputs.md` §1 credits Media Library against **WAY-1 (Wayroo active users)**. A feature reps do not value will not drive rep adoption. It is a **DSO retention and brand-control** feature. Flagged to Unified Strategy in July; still open.

> **A growth-and-recovery frame is still missing from the library.** What Hope needed to hear was *"Wayroo is the lever to reverse a sales and engagement decline"* — and that is the Paparazzi story ($31M across 4,000 adopters, 60%+ YoY). **It has never been positioned that way, and the case study is still unpublished** — the oldest unresolved item in the GTM plan.

# Cross-Persona Rules

## 1. The audience asymmetry holds at persona level — and gets sharper

| | Reps (R1–R3) | Home office (H1–H4) |
|---|---|---|
| Lead with | **Tap-to-Pay** (R1) · **downline on your phone** (R2) · **payment links** (R3) | **Economics and visibility** |
| Never lead with | Compliance · corporate visibility · architecture | **Tap-to-Pay** — it implies "complete ProPay onboarding," converting a free ask into a project |

**The one refinement this document adds:** *"lead reps with Tap-to-Pay"* is right for **announcement and acquisition**, and wrong for **first-run at a locked account**. There, lead R2 with the downline dashboard — a capability that actually works — and reach the pending selling tools second. The master positioning states this; the persona layer says which rep it applies to.

## 2. Features that split by persona — never average them

| Feature | High with | Low with | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Media Library** | H3 (Hope: brand control) | **Reps (observed 🔵 Low)** | DSO deck only. Never rep first-run. |
| **Tap-to-Pay** | **R1 (highest of anything shown)** | **H1 — actively harmful as an opener** | Rep hero. Home office beat 3 only. |
| **Downline dashboards** | **R2 and H3** | R4 | The rare feature that lands on both sides. Use it at locked accounts. |
| **Inventory sync** | R1 | R3, H2 | **Demo it as already populated, never as a capability** — Square does inventory well; the sync is the differentiator, not the feature. |
| **Compliance / FTC** | H4 (late) | **Everyone else, including H1** | Supporting structural argument. Never an opener. |

## 3. What every persona is told identically

Two claims are safe with all eight and should not be varied:

- **The wholesale order and inventory arrive already in the app.** USP 1, and the whole position.
- **Nothing extra is being charged for the selling tools.** True in every direction; the only branch is Pro status.
