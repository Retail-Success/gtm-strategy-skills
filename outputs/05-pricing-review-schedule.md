# Triggers

**Two are near-certain to fire this quarter.**

| Trigger | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| 🔴 **The 510 conversion rate lands** | **The first real WTP data.** Under 15% weakens the whole Pro-attach thesis. |
| 🔴 **The first transact rate is measured** | Determines whether 1.5% of GMV is a business at achievable adoption |
| **A DSO asks for per-rep pricing or a discount** | Someone testing whether $0 is really $0 |
| **DSO #4 asks for free white-label** | The pilot precedent under pressure — **the rules exist for this moment** |
| **Hustle or Boards changes price** | Our price argument is relative to theirs |
| **Square adds back-office integrations** | Would attack the one durable differentiator |
| **SMS ships** | **Rung 2 becomes definable** |
| **Aice deal signs or dies** | Rung 2 is half Aice |
| **Revolution Pro gets a sunset date** | **Both the category and commercial frames lose their referent** |
| **Support cost per adopter turns out to be material** | The only real variable cost, currently unmeasured |

# What to Review Each Cycle

**Gather first:**
- [ ] Transact rate, and GMV per transacting rep
- [ ] Accounts ProPay-approved (of 55)
- [ ] 510 conversion / Pro attach rate
- [ ] Support tickets per 100 adopters
- [ ] Competitor price changes — Hustle, Boards, Square
- [ ] Any DSO pricing pushback
- [ ] Rung-2 readiness — is SMS on the roadmap?

**Then answer:**
- [ ] Is 1.5% right — could it be higher without resistance? *(It is the only price left; do not discount it.)*
- [ ] Are dormant adopters costing more in support than the Pro attach returns?
- [ ] **Has any transactional feature drifted toward a paid tier?** 🔴 *(The design rule — check every cycle.)*
- [ ] Is the $25,000 white-label anchor holding?
- [ ] Can rung 2 be defined yet?

**Decision:** ☐ no change · ☐ minor adjustment · ☐ major restructure → rerun Tasks 1–6

---

# The Standing Check

> ### Every review asks one question before any other:
>
> ### **Has anything transactional moved behind a paywall?**
>
> Tap-to-Pay, invoicing, POS, storefront, Dropship. **If any of them has, reverse it.** Suppressing a transacting rep costs ~$216/year against an upgrade worth less — **and this is the constraint most likely to be violated by someone reasoning from ordinary SaaS instincts** who was not in the room when it was set.
