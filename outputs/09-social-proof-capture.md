## Why this is a launch task and not a marketing task

**Invariant 5 of the operating model: proof is the binding constraint on every channel.** Zero published case studies. Zero testimonials. The Paparazzi result — 4,000 reps, $31M, 60%+ YoY — **has been unpublished since May.**

And the operating model is precise about why: *"it is not an evidence problem — it is a permission-and-packaging problem. The material exists; nobody has asked."*

> ### Two things make this pilot the most valuable capture window the company has had.
>
> **1. Every existing proof point comes from a buyer. None comes from a user.** Every quote in the repo is a founder or a VP Sales. **Not one is from a rep** — and the rep is now the adopter, the audience, and the revenue event.
>
> **2. Sentiment is perishable and the July launch already proved it.** Jordan Essentials launched in July with no baseline captured and no testimonials collected. That window is gone. **Do not run a second launch the same way.**

## 🔴 Consent at capture, never retrofitted

**This is the process fix, and it is the reason Paparazzi is still unpublished.**

> **Ask for publication permission in the same conversation where you capture the material.** Not later, not "once we know what we'll use." Retrofitting consent is how good material becomes unusable material.

| Source | Ask | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Rep** | *"Can we share this — your first name, your company, and what you said?"* | Same conversation |
| **DSO** | Written approval to name them and cite their numbers | **Written into the pilot agreement**, not requested afterward |
| **Anonymised fallback** | *"A 2,500-rep wellness company"* — agree this upfront as the default if named approval stalls | At pilot agreement |

**Put the case-study clause in the pilot agreement.** Three accounts are about to grant three permissions; **a fourth costs nothing to add now and is near-impossible to add later.**

## Capture protocol

- **Shared folder, not camera rolls.** `launch-proof/[account]/[YYYY-MM-DD]-[type]-[description]`
- **Caption within 24 hours.** Context evaporates — an unlabelled screenshot is worthless in a month.
- **Consent status on every asset:** `approved-named` · `approved-anon` · `pending` · `internal-only`
- **Same metrics, same view, weekly** — a growth curve needs consistent frames
- **Weekly triage, 15 minutes**, in the war-room cadence. Tag the best five as they arrive rather than sorting hundreds later.
