# 3-Year Revenue Model Analysis — Aice JV

**Source:** `Wayroo_Aice-model` (Summary + Assumptions + 3-Year Revenue Model tabs)
**Date:** June 21, 2026
**Purpose:** Pressure-test the partnership/JV revenue model; anchor the OPE endgame; flag reconciliation issues before board/acquirer use.

---

## Structure

The model = **compounding user growth × unit economics**, four segments (ByD Start-up, ByD Enterprise, Wayroo, Aice), Year 0 → Year 3, two growth scenarios.

### Year-0 unit economics (per segment)

| Segment | Users | MRR/user | Monthly eComm vol | Residual % | ARR-Subs | ARR-Residual | Commission |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ByD Start-up | 5 DSOs | $2,500 | $25K/DSO | 0.4% | $150K | $6K | $2,500/DSO |
| ByD Enterprise | 1 DSO | $10,000 | $5M/DSO | 0.4% | $120K | $240K | $10K/DSO |
| **Wayroo** | 15,000 | $1.50 | **$1,200/user** | **1.5%** | $270K | **$3.24M** | 20% of residual |
| Aice | 15,000 | $12 | — | — | $2.16M | $0 | +plug (receives) |

### Two scenarios

| | Year 0 | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Base** (compounding 100%/yr) | $6.19M | $12.37M | $24.74M | **$49.49M** |
| **Upside** (manual +50K users/yr) | $6.19M | $25.96M | $45.74M | **$65.51M** |

Base doubles users annually (Wayroo/Aice 15K→30K→60K→120K). Upside adds 50K Wayroo + 50K Aice users/yr (→65K→115K→165K), front-loading Year 1.

---

## Key findings

### 1. Payments residual is the engine (~half of all revenue)
Wayroo's Year-0 gross ARR is $3.51M, of which **$3.24M (92%) is payments residual**, only $270K subscription. Wayroo is ~46% of total Year-0 net revenue and the fastest-growing line ($25.9M base / $35.6M upside by Y3). **Quantitatively confirms the payments-funded-pricing thesis** — payments is not supplemental, it is the dominant revenue stream and growth lever. Driver = net-new rep eComm GMV ($1,200/user/mo × 1.5%).

### 2. Anchors the OPE endgame — bigger than the deck
Year-3 net revenue: **$49.5M (base) / $65.5M (upside)** — before the ~5-yr exit horizon. Supersedes the earlier "$40–60M illustrative" estimate in the OPE.

### 3. Commissions net to zero in consolidation
Inter-entity transfers: Wayroo −$648K, ByD −$22.5K, Aice +$670.5K → cancel in the consolidated total. Under the JV/merger this is internal allocation; the **consolidated number is the real one.**

## Issue status (two resolved by leadership decision, June 21)

| # | Issue | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deck ($21M) vs. model ($49.5–65.5M) at Year 3 | ✅ **RESOLVED — the model is the source of truth.** Endgame = $49.5M base / $65.5M upside Yr3. The deck's $21M SOM is superseded; re-size or retire it as a sizing artifact. |
| 2 | Does $1,200/user/mo hold as users scale 15K→120–165K? | ✅ **RESOLVED — hold $1,200/user constant.** Rationale: payments residual is **not gated on cash-and-carry.** Even non-C&C Aice users (creators/catalog reps) generate payments revenue when they run transactions through the platform via **invoicing / payment links.** So residual-per-user does not collapse as the mix shifts away from C&C — the payments engine scales with *any user who transacts.* |
| 3 | Aice blended $12/user/mo across all users | ⏳ Open — real tiers $9.99–$49.99 with likely free/trial base; validate blended ARPU in Phase 5. Lower priority. |
| 4 | Incremental vs. total ambiguity | ⏳ Open — Year-0 net $6.19M vs. existing ~$8–10M ARR. Clarify whether model is incremental JV revenue or total combined. |
| 5 | 100% compounding labeled "base" | ⏳ Optional — consider adding a conservative downside case for diligence. |

> **Strategic note (from Decision 2):** invoicing/payment-links make **every transacting user payments-monetizable, regardless of C&C status.** This is a stronger statement of the payments-funded thesis than the model puts on paper — worth surfacing explicitly in positioning and in Phase 5 pricing: the monetizable base = *all transacting users*, not just C&C reps.
