## The rule this script is built on

> **Demo the seam, not the surface.**
>
> A renovated storefront looks like the storefront they already have — because it is the same catalog, the same job, better executed. What they cannot get anywhere else is the **joins**: shop → enrollment, one brand across both, configuration instead of a ticket.

# Live demo — 20 minutes of driving, 10 minutes of listening

Written for a 30-minute client call, one presenter driving, PM in the room.

## 0:00–0:03 · Frame it, and ask before you show

Do not open the product. Open with why this exists — then stop talking.

> *"Before I show you anything: I'm new in the PM seat, and the first thing I put in place was a voice-of-customer program. Two things came back about both our cart and our enrollment — **branding and customization are too hard**, and **performance isn't where it needs to be**. So we're rebuilding both from the ground up. What you'll see is a **prototype** — real, clickable, deliberately unfinished.*
>
> *Before I share my screen — **tell me about your commerce setup today. What's working, what isn't?***"

**Why:** "prototype" makes placeholder data a non-event instead of a credibility hit, and their answer tells you which of the three beats below to spend your time on. **If they answer at length, cut beats, not the conversation.**

## 0:03–0:06 · ⭐ Beat 1 — the seam (LEAD WITH THIS)

Shop page → point at **"Shopping with [rep]"** → then **"Become a Rep"** in the same nav → enrollment flow opens.

> *"Two things worth noticing. First, the shopper is attached to a rep, and that attribution follows them to checkout and into commission — a general-purpose cart has no concept of that.*
>
> *Second, and this is the part I'd want your reaction to: **your next rep is already on your site — they're shopping.** Today they'd have to leave, find a form somewhere else, and start over. Here it's the same journey. A click is expensive."*

⚠ **Ship gate:** the storefront entry point is **client-placed**, not a fixed feature, and the MVP **hands off to the existing cart for payment** (US-12 has no code, blocked on spike Q4). **Say "from the shop", never "without leaving the cart".**

## 0:06–0:12 · Beat 2 — configuration instead of a ticket

Footer **Admin** → Branding Studio. Change **Primary colour** → watch the live preview. Then nav + footer colours, a heading font, a button shape.

> *"Settings on the left. That's not a mockup on the right — it's your actual storefront."*

Then the **Page Configuration** tab — grid columns, category placement, card style, PDP layout.

> *"Branding is the easy half. The complaint we kept hearing was 'every client gets the same layout, and hiding a feature leaves dead whitespace.' So this configures the page itself."*

**⭐ The cost line — here, not earlier:**

> *"This is the ~$4,500 conversation. Customization is a services engagement with us today. Here the everyday version is a setting you change as often as you like — and when you want something genuinely bespoke, we still build it. You're paying for the hard part, not for changing a colour."*

Then **Save Draft / Publish**:

> *"Three states — in progress, saved draft, live. Build your holiday storefront in October, publish it in November."*

Finish on **the surface picker → Enrollment**:

> *"Same tool brands your enrollment flow. Set a colour once."*

**Close the beat on posture, which is what actually landed:**

> *"The point isn't the controls. It's that the answer to 'can it do this?' stops being 'file a request and wait.'"*

## 0:12–0:17 · Beat 3 — the DSO moat, shown not listed

Back to the storefront. Move fast — this is reassurance, not persuasion.

- **AutoShip** — *"promo- and price-change-aware on renewal. Third-party carts treat renewal as a catch-all and quietly bill the wrong amount."*
- **Shop on behalf of a customer** — *"Shopify disallows it. One client still dual-runs our cart just for this."*
- Parties, rank pricing, volume, credits, gift certificates — *"native, not plugins."*
- Country / currency / tax-display strip — *"per-client settings, not a fork of the code."*

⛔ **Do not walk checkout end-to-end** unless the build is Freedom-wired. A stall here costs more than the beat is worth.

## 0:17–0:20 · Beat 4 — only if they are building or buying elsewhere

Use **only** when they've said they're building their own or evaluating a platform. Observation, never warning:

> *"The clients who build their own usually aren't wrong about the problem — they're right, and they stopped believing we'd fix it. What gets underestimated is that the storefront isn't the hard part. The hard part is AutoShip that catches a promo on renewal, and attribution that still resolves to the right rep nine months later. That's what you rebuild every time your back office changes — and you'd be building it alone."*

⚠ **Never name another client.** Use the shape.

## 0:20–0:30 · Stop driving

> *"That's what we've got. What's missing? And if you're building or buying something else — what does it do that this doesn't?"*

Then, on whatever they name:

> *"That's either a setting or something we extend — and which one it should be is exactly what I want your input on."*

**Capability gaps are parity-sweep input, not objections to handle.** Write them down; don't defend.

## ⛔ Do not say

| Don't | Instead |
|---|---|
| A date, or "Q1" as a promise | *"Targeting Q1, and taking the time to get AutoShip, attribution and commissions right rather than rushing them."* |
| "It's ready" | *"The experience is real. The back end is the work in front of us."* |
| Any speed number | *"I won't give you a number I haven't measured on your data."* — **no benchmark exists** |
| "It'll be free" | *"Everyday customization becomes self-serve; bespoke stays a services engagement. Packaging isn't mine to commit to."* |
| Any extension API / SDK / plugin mechanism | *"That's what I'd want to work through with your team. What would you want to extend?"* — **nothing is specified** |
| "Since you're on Shopify…" | *"What are you on today?"* — **check, don't assume** |
| "Enroll without leaving the cart" | *"Enroll from the shop"* |

## Pre-flight

- [ ] Log into the Branding Studio **before** the call, in the tab you'll share
- [ ] Storefront open in a second tab, signed out
- [ ] Know the placeholder-data line if the demo catalog is thin
- [ ] Decide in advance whether you'll click **Publish**
- [ ] Close every other tab

**If it breaks:** *"dev environment — let me reload."* Reload **once**. If it fails twice, go to the conversation; that is the valuable part anyway.
