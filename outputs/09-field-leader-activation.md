# Phase 9 — Support Network & Event Motion: Field Leaders, DSU, The Juice

**Phase:** 9 — Executing the Launch (Tasks 1 and 5, adapted)
**Date:** 2026-08-13 · **Owner:** Sam (design) · Autumn + Cassie (run)
**Reconciles with:** [`10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md`](10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md) §4 (growth loop) and Phase 0.4–0.5 — **referenced, not duplicated**

> ### The support network for this launch is not 50 colleagues. It is field leaders inside three DSOs.
>
> The methodology assumes a founder recruiting friends to amplify a public launch. **That model does not apply here.** Our audience is reachable, permissioned, and organised into an existing referral hierarchy with financial incentives attached. **Direct selling hands us a pre-built amplification network that no other software category gets for free** — the job is activating it, not building one.

# 1. The Support Roster

| Tier | Who | Size | What they do | Why they do it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Field leaders** | Uplines with active downlines | **10–30 per account** | Adopt first, transact first, then drive their teams | **Their commissions depend on downline volume.** The strongest incentive alignment available. |
| **2 — DSO corporate champion** | Whoever granted the three permissions | 1–2 per account | Sends the announcement in their own voice; puts it on the field calendar | They earn margin on every Pro subscriber, and their field looks equipped |
| **3 — Early transacting reps** | First reps to complete a real sale | 20–50 per account | Peer proof, testimonials, event stories | Recognition; being first |

**Tier 1 is the whole strategy.** Playbook §4: *"One activated field leader with 20 downline reps is worth 20 individually-persuaded reps — and costs a fraction of the effort to reach."*

> ### 🔴 The highest-leverage step in the entire launch
>
> **Get Tier 1 transacting before their account launches.** Playbook step 0.4, and it is unambiguous: *"A field leader who has personally made a sale on Wayroo is the most persuasive asset available at the launch event."*
>
> **Jordan Essentials launched without this and still hit 764 reps in 2–3 days.** With it, the number should be higher — and more importantly, those reps should reach a *transaction* faster, which is the only number that matters.
>
> **Quality gate: ≥80% of the Tier 1 cohort has a completed first transaction before their account's launch moment.** Not a download. A transaction.

## Running this across three accounts at once

New for Phase 9 — the playbook was written for one account.

| Constraint | Handling |
|---|---|
| **Cohorts are not interchangeable** | Field leaders belong to a DSO. There is no shared pool; recruit three times. |
| **Recruitment runs through the DSO champion** | Tier 2 identifies Tier 1. We do not know who the real leaders are; they do. **Ask for "your 20 most active sellers," not "your top earners"** — rank and activity diverge. |
| **Capacity** | 🔴 Three cohorts × pre-clearing ProPay × chasing first transactions lands on **two people.** **Stagger account launches by a week each**, or name in advance which account gets the lighter touch. |
| **JE is different** | 764 reps are already installed. **JE's Tier 1 does not need recruiting to adopt — it needs identifying among existing users and pointing at transacting.** Cheapest cohort of the three. |

## The ask package

Give a field leader everything, ready. They are volunteers with a business to run.

- **Their own account working first** — installed, ProPay pre-cleared (playbook 0.5), one real sale completed
- **The field-leader one-pager** ([`07-rep-launch-kit.md`](07-rep-launch-kit.md) §5) — the single highest-leverage asset in the kit
- **Three ready-to-send messages** for their downline: announce · how-to · *"I just made a sale on this"*
- **A named human** (Autumn) who answers within 30 minutes during launch week
- **What not to do:** don't promise Tap-to-Pay dates · don't tell reps it costs anything · don't set up before the launch moment if their DSO wants the event to be the reveal

# 2. Event Motion — DSU and The Juice

**Both are rep-facing moments where Tap-to-Pay is the promoted feature.**

> ⚠️ **The Juice is not plannable yet.** Date and audience unknown, so it appears in no sequence in [`09-launch-execution-plan.md`](09-launch-execution-plan.md). **Supply both and this section applies to it unchanged** — the motion below is event-agnostic.

## 🔴 The dependency that decides both events

**Tap-to-Pay is the promoted feature and it has not shipped.** `my-gtm-context.md` §14 records what happened last time: strongest rep draw at the July JE launch, didn't ship, **~2,500 reps left holding a convention-dated expectation.**

> **Promoting it again at a rep-facing event without it shipping repeats that mistake in front of an industry audience instead of one client's field.**

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| **Ships before DSU** | Promote it. It is the strongest hook available and both events are built around it. |
| **Does not** | **Change the feature, not the date.** Lead with **dashboard widgets and back-office data on your phone** — two of the four strongest rep reactions at JE, both shipping, both working with ProPay pending. |

**Call it by 09-15**, so content is built against reality.

## DSU (October) — the mixed-audience advantage

DSU carries reps *and* home-office attendees. That normally breaks the beat asymmetry — Tap-to-Pay is a rep message and must never open a home-office conversation.

> ### At DSU, the mixed audience is a feature — if the booth knows which conversation it is in.
>
> A home-office attendee watching reps get excited about Tap-to-Pay **is beat 3 arriving on its own.** That is exactly the intended sequence: rep demand pulls the ProPay conversation, rather than us pushing it.
>
> **What makes it work or fail is the follow-up sentence.** *"Your field wants this — here's what turning it on looks like"* is beat 3 landing correctly. *"You'll need to complete merchant onboarding"* is a compliance project, and the ask stops being frictionless.

**Booth staffing rule — brief before the event:**

| Who approaches | Lead with | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Rep** | Tap-to-Pay *(or dashboards, per the call above)* | Get them installed. **Live ProPay onboarding if their DSO is onboarded.** |
| **Home office, unprompted** | Costs nothing · margin on every Pro subscriber · field visibility | Permissions ask. **Never open with ProPay.** |
| **Home office, having seen rep excitement** | 🔴 **Beat 3.** *"Your field wants this."* | *Then* ProPay — as the answer to their reps' demand |

## Run-of-show — both events

Adapted from playbook Phase 1. **The playbook's launch steps are the event steps; this is their event-specific form.**

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Primary CTA** | 🔴 **A first transaction on-site — not a download** (playbook 1.4). A download is a Stage-2 number and earns nothing. |
| **Staffed ProPay onboarding** | Playbook 1.3. **Do not let a rep leave with an app they cannot transact on.** Target ≥60% of downloaders with a submitted application before they leave. |

| **Field leaders on stand** | Tier 1 reps who have already transacted, telling other reps. **Worth more than any staff member we can put there.** |
| **Capture** | Everything — see [`09-social-proof-capture.md`](09-social-proof-capture.md). Event floors are the single richest source of usable proof. |

## Contingency

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| **Tap-to-Pay not shipped** | Feature swap per the call above. **Do not give a date on a stage.** |
| **Rep asks "when?"** | *"It's included and it's coming — I'm not going to give you a date I can't keep."* **Honest beats confident.** ~2,500 reps are already waiting on one. |
| **A rep's DSO hasn't granted permissions** | Register interest, don't sell. Feed the name to Cassie as an inbound signal — a rep asking for it is the warmest possible opening. |
| **Their DSO hasn't onboarded ProPay** | Install them anyway. **Locked-tool tap is a leading indicator**; capture it and add them to that account's demand count. |
