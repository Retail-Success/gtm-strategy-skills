> ### Two sends, and mixing them is the failure mode
>
> **Email is a secondary channel here** — the primary is in-product. But two email moments are real and dated:
>
> 1. 🔴 **The Jordan Essentials Tap-to-Pay trial** — ships in 2–3 weeks, 764 reps, **and it must be segmented on Revolution Pro status**
> 2. **Account activation** — when a new DSO's field becomes eligible
>
> ⚠️ **Rep email addresses generally sit with the DSO.** Where the DSO sends, they own the from-name and we supply copy.

# Send 1 — The JE Tap-to-Pay Trial 🔴 Dated

**🔴 Segment on Pro status before the first send. This is the most likely execution error at the flagship account.**

## 1a — The 254 Pro holders *(a launch — nothing expires)*

**Subject options:**
- **"Tap to Pay is live"** ← recommended
- "The thing you asked about at convention"
- "Card payments are on"

> **Hook** — *You asked for this at convention. It's here.*
>
> **Story** — Card payments are live in your Wayroo app. Your customer taps her card on your phone. No reader, no extra hardware, no waiting.
>
> **Proof** — It works with everything already in there — your inventory from your wholesale orders, your customers, your numbers.
>
> **CTA** — **It's included in what you already pay for. Nothing to turn on.**
>
> **[ Open Wayroo ]**

🚫 **This cohort never receives a day-21 or day-28 message.** Nothing is ending for them, and telling them otherwise manufactures a problem.

## 1b — The 510 without Pro *(a trial, dated from message one)*

**Subject:** **"Tap to Pay is on for you"**

> Card payments are live in your Wayroo app — your customer taps her card on your phone, no reader.
>
> **It's yours free for the next 30 days.** After that it runs on Jordan Central Pro, $5/month, and you can turn that on yourself in about a minute — or do nothing and it simply ends.
>
> **[ Open Wayroo ]**

**Day 7 — their number, not ours:**
> **You've taken $[X] in card payments this week.**

**Day 21 — the ask, in their own numbers. 🔬 Split this send.**

| Arm | Framing |
|---|---|
| **A — plain** | *"Nine days left. Keeping Tap to Pay is $5/month through Jordan Central Pro."* |
| **B — value-framed** | *"Over three weeks you've taken **$[X]** on your phone — sales that used to need cash or a reader. Nine days left; keeping it is $5/month."* |

**The split separates messaging effect from genuine appetite.** The population exists once — there is no second run at this.

**Day 28 — last call. Day 30 — converts or ends cleanly.**

### Design rules
🔴 **Never a silent expiry** — day 21 and 28 are mandatory · 🔴 **Never cut off mid-event** — a rep who booked a fair for day 32 has a real problem; offer a short extension, it is cheap and it converts · ⚠️ **30 days, not 14** — these reps sell at events, and a 14-day window may not contain one.

# Send 2 — Account Activation

**For a DSO whose field becomes eligible. Sent by the DSO where possible.**

**Subject:** **"Your inventory's already in it"**

> **Hook** — *You've got an app you didn't know about, and your last wholesale order is already in it.*
>
> **Story** — Wayroo is the selling app that comes with your back office. Not another tool to set up — it runs on the same system [DSO] does, so your inventory, your customers, and your numbers are already there when you open it.
>
> **Proof** — Take card payments on your phone. Send an invoice with a payment link. Ring up an event and watch inventory count itself down. **Reps tell us they were paying $60–100 a month across six to ten apps to do this.**
>
> **CTA** — **[ Open Wayroo ]** *(branch: "Included with your Revolution Pro" · "Comes with Revolution Pro — $5/month")*

⚠️ **Branch the closing line on Pro status.** Never claim unqualified inclusion to a rep who does not hold it.

**Variants:** **Team leaders** — lead with *"Your downline, on your phone."* · **At a ProPay-pending account** — lead with the data claim, **never with Tap-to-Pay.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **From** | The DSO's own name wherever they will send it — **it converts better and it honours the relationship** |

| **Follow-up** | Non-openers at T+3, different subject line. **Trial cohort only.** |
