> ### Why this matters more here than in a normal Phase 7
>
> **Live training is the most consistently praised thing in the Paparazzi survey.** Reps named Autumn personally — *"Autumn was very thorough and patient"* — and multiple respondents said they return to the workshop every convention, some for the third year.
>
> **That is a proven asset that currently runs once a year, in one room.** This script's job is to capture what works and make it available to a rep who will never attend a convention.
>
> 🔴 **And it has to answer the objection the survey surfaced:** six of seven non-adopters said getting started seemed too complicated. **A demo that opens with a feature tour confirms their fear. One that opens with an already-populated screen refutes it.**

# The Rep Demo — 3 minutes

## 0:00–0:15 · Hook — the problem, in her words

> **"You're at a vendor event. Someone wants two things and she doesn't have cash. What happens next?"**
>
> *[beat]*
>
> **"For most reps, that's a lost sale. Or a Venmo request you're chasing on Tuesday."**

**Screen:** black, or a hand holding a phone. **No logo. No "welcome."**

## 0:15–0:45 · 🔴 The proof — open the app, show her own data

> **"This is Wayroo. Watch what happens when I open it for the first time."**
>
> *[open the app]*
>
> **"That's my inventory. Everything from my last wholesale order — already there. I didn't type any of it in. No spreadsheet, no import, no setup."**
>
> **"It's there because Wayroo runs on the same system your company does. It already knows what you ordered."**

**Screen:** app opens to a **populated inventory screen with real product images and real counts.**

> ### This is the single most important 30 seconds in the script.
>
> **It answers the documented reason reps do not adopt**, and it does it by demonstration rather than assertion. **Never open with a feature tour, a menu walkthrough, or a settings screen.** Every second before the populated screen works against the one thing the demo needs to prove.

## 0:45–1:45 · The core loop — take a payment

> **"Back to that customer. She wants these two."**
>
> *[scan or tap two items]* **"Scan them, or just tap. Inventory's counting down as I go."**
>
> **"She's paying by card — and she taps it right on my phone. No reader."**
>
> *[complete the payment]*
>
> **"Done. She gets a receipt with my company's branding on it. My inventory's updated. And that sale is already back in the home office."**

**Screen:** POS → tap-to-pay → confirmation → the customer's branded receipt.

**Use real product names and real amounts.** Never `Test Item` or `$1.00`.

## 1:45–2:30 · Second value — pick one, by audience

**For R1 (in-person sellers):**
> **"Say she wants something you don't have on you. Send her an invoice with a payment link — she pays from her phone, wherever she is."**

**For R2 (team leaders):**
> **"And if you've got a team — this is your downline, live. Who's selling, who's gone quiet. On your phone, without logging into anything."**

⚠️ **Do not show both.** One second-value beat. Two makes it a feature tour, which is the register this demo is built to avoid.

🚫 **Never demo Reply to Buy.** 3 reps use it, 24 find it challenging.

## 2:30–3:00 · Close

> **"Before: a spreadsheet, Square, a Venmo request, and a sale you might lose."**
>
> **"After: one app that was already set up when you opened it."**
>
> **"It comes with Revolution Pro."** *(branch: **"— which you already have"** for Pro holders · **"— that's $5 a month"** for everyone else)*
>
> **"Open it and your inventory's already in there."**

# The Home-Office Demo — 5 minutes

**Different audience, different order. 🔴 Never open with Tap-to-Pay** — it implies "complete ProPay onboarding," which converts a free ask into a project.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| **0:00–0:30** | *"Your reps are spending $60–100 a month on tools you can't see."* |
| **0:30–1:30** | **What a rep gets with nothing from you** — populated inventory, dashboards, media library. *"This works today, at no cost to you or them."* |
| **1:30–2:30** | **What you see** — every rep sale as a record, field-wide visibility, and **the C&C share number no DSO has.** |
| **2:30–3:30** | **The economic line** — *"Wayroo is the reason your reps buy Revolution Pro, and you make margin on every one of them."* |
| **3:30–4:30** | **The one ask — ProPay.** *"Card payments need a merchant account. It's underwriting paperwork, not a purchase."* |
| **4:30–5:00** | *"[N] of your reps have already asked for it."* |

# Production

**Minimum viable, and it is genuinely enough:** screen recording (Loom or OBS) + a good microphone. **Bad audio kills a demo faster than bad video.**

| Requirement | Note |
|---|---|
| 🔴 **Real data throughout** | Real products, real names, real amounts. A demo with `Test Item` undermines the entire "your own data" claim. |
| **Captions** | Many reps watch on mute |
| 1080p minimum | |
| **Two versions** | 3-min rep · 5-min home office. **Never send one to the other audience.** |
| Length discipline | **If the rep demo exceeds 3 minutes, cut the second-value beat.** |

**Hosting:** unlisted YouTube for embedding; in-app for first run; downloadable for Autumn's live sessions.

# Scale the Thing That Already Works

The live workshop outperforms any video — repeat attendees, named praise, and at least one non-adopter converted in the room (*"I will be using Premier starting today"*).

| Action | Why |
|---|---|
| **Record Autumn's next live session** | The best demo asset already exists and has never been captured |
| **Keep running it live** | The most praised element in the survey. **Do not replace it with video.** |
| **Use the video for the ~16,000 who will never attend** | Reach, not replacement |
| **Build a "switch from Square" variant** | 5 of 7 non-adopters settled on Square |

> **The video is the fallback. The live session is the asset.** Recording it costs nothing and captures a format reps have voluntarily returned to for three years.
