# So What?

## Play offense here — Strength × Opportunity

> ### 1. S2 × O1 — Point the channel at Paparazzi first.
>
> We can advertise in-product to ~16,000 un-adopted reps at an account that has **already cleared ProPay** and already pays a competitor for adjacent tooling. **No gate, no cycle, revenue-ready on day one.** This is the single highest-yield action available and it is not in any current plan.

> ### 2. S1 × O4 — Prove the claim at the moment it is self-evidently true.
>
> The post-wholesale-order confirmation screen is where a rep has just bought the exact stock that will be waiting in the app. **USP 1 needs no persuasion there** — placement does the work that copy otherwise has to.

> ### 3. S3 × O2 — Let Wayroo sell Revolution Pro.
>
> JE produced 510 pre-qualified Pro candidates without a sales conversation. We can identify the same population at all 55 clients from data we already hold.

## Most vulnerable here — Weakness × Threat

> ### 1. 🔴 W1 × T1 — We cannot prove Wayroo beats Square, because we measure nothing.
>
> Square is free and good. The only counter is integration — and *"first open → data seen"* is the event that would prove it lands. **It is not instrumented.** We are making an unprovable claim against a strong free competitor.

> ### 2. 🔴 W2 × T5 — No counter means no DSO conversation, and a grievance risk.
>
> With permission deleted, the demand count is the only opener. Ship the lock without it and we get rep frustration, no artifact, and a home office that hears about the campaign from a rep instead of from us.

> ### 3. W3 × T6 — "Free reads as trial" is the largest untested risk, and we keep building on it.
>
> Every asset uses *included* rather than *free* to manage this. **Nobody has checked whether it works.**

# Top 3 Strategic Priorities

| # | Priority | Why it beats the alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 🔴 **Ship the two events — `transaction_completed` and the demand counter** | They are the two critical weaknesses, they are cheap, and **everything else in this analysis is unmeasurable or unopenable without them** |
| **2** | 🔴 **Run the channel at Paparazzi first, not at a new pilot** | ProPay cleared, ~16,000 addressable reps, a competitor to displace, and revenue possible immediately. **The pilot programme is the right second move, not the first.** |
| **3** | **Test the positioning on 5–10 JE reps** | 764 fresh adopters, free, and it validates or kills a large body of untested work before more is built on it |

> **All three are cheap and none requires a sales cycle.** That is the shape of this motion's advantage — and the reason the biggest risks are execution gaps rather than market ones.
