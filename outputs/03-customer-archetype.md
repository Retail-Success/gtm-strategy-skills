# The Archetype

> ### She is running a real business on a phone, paying for it herself, and nobody built software for the way she actually works.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Independent seller inside a ByDesign client organisation. US. Holds Revolution Pro — or does not yet, and will be asked for $5. |
| **Measured on** | **Personal sales volume and monthly earnings.** Not activity, not logins. |
| **Runs her business on** | **6–10 apps, $60–100/month, out of her own pocket.** Canva, ChatGPT, Square, Boards, Project Broadcast, a spreadsheet. |
| **Sells at** | Vendor events, markets, pop-ups, parties, Facebook, and by text |
| **Gets paid by** | Square, Venmo, PayPal, cash — whatever the customer has |
| **Tracks inventory in** | A spreadsheet, or her memory |
| **What corporate sees** | **Almost none of it** |

## Her day, as observed

She orders wholesale from corporate and enters the SKUs by hand into a spreadsheet. She loads a car for a Saturday market. She sells, takes Venmo from one customer and a card on Square from the next, and writes the sales on paper because scanning each item is slower than the queue. That night she reconciles three sources against one spreadsheet, then logs into the back office to see whether her order posted. **On Sunday she posts to Facebook using a product photo she took herself, because the official one is in a Dropbox folder she cannot find.**

**None of that is selling.** It is the tax she pays to sell — and it is the single most cross-validated pain in the library:

> *"Reps spend lots of time doing admin tasks such as printing shipping labels, maintaining inventory spreadsheets, and none of these activities generate revenue."* — Brian B, CEO, Color Street · **confirmed across 4 accounts**

# What She Reacts To — Directly Observed, 764 Reps

**The JE convention was the first time ByDesign watched this population meet the product at scale.** Every top-tier reaction was a tool that helps her **earn** or **see**. Nothing in the top tier organises, files, or complies.

| Reaction | Feature | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Strongest of anything shown** | **Tap-to-Pay** | **Payment friction is the felt pain.** Not inventory, not admin — the moment the sale nearly doesn't happen. |
| 🔴 High | Invoicing with payment links | Remote and post-event selling |
| 🔴 High | **Downline dashboard reports** | **A second archetype hiding inside the first** — see below |
| 🔴 High | Other dashboard widgets | Her numbers, on her phone |
| 🔴 High | Back-office data without a separate login | Removes the nightly login |
| 🔴 High *(as an unmet ask)* | **Project Broadcast / SMS** | Unprompted, repeated. **A real product gap.** |
| ⚠️ **Under-tested** | **Next-day payout card** | 🔴 **Added 2026-09-02.** JE logged "next-day funding" as 🟡 Medium — but that was framed as *replicated-site commission timing*, not as **money on a card she can spend tomorrow.** Different proposition, likely mis-scored. |

> ### 🔴 The payout card is the gap this archetype missed
>
> Her financial pains include *"waiting ~30 days for replicated-site commission"* and *"losing sales at the point of purchase."* **Wayroo answers both, and this document only credited the second.**
>
> Earnings land on a **branded payout card the next day** — spendable anywhere Mastercard is accepted, moveable to her bank, or usable to buy her next wholesale order. For a rep whose income arrives monthly, that changes how her business runs rather than adding a feature.
>
> **No rep has ever been asked about it.** Add to the interview guide: *"How long do you wait to see money from a sale, and what would change if it were tomorrow?"*
>
> ⚠️ Gated on ProPay — it belongs with Tap-to-Pay, not with the works-on-download set.
| 🔵 **Low** | **Media Library** | Corporate's brand-control need, **not hers** |

> ### The finding that should govern all rep-facing work
>
> **She responds to earning and seeing. She does not respond to organising.**
>
> Compliance, media library, and inventory-*organisation* framing did not register. **Inventory that is simply already correct** is valuable to her; inventory *management* as a capability is not — Square already does that, free, and she has used it.
>
> **The sync is what she wants. The feature is not.**

## Her economics, in her words

**$60–100/month across 6–10 apps**, self-funded. Wayroo arrives at **$0 incremental** if she holds Revolution Pro, or **$5** if she does not — against a stack she is already paying for. **That is a 12–20x substitution argument**, and it is the strongest rep-facing economic case available.

⚠️ **The $5 version is not the weaker pitch.** A rep spending $60–100/month who has *used* the product is being offered a substitution below every alternative in the category. It just has a number in it. **Name the number early** — vagueness confirms suspicion in a population that has been sold to a lot.

# Three Variants — and One Non-Customer

The archetype is not homogeneous. **Full messaging treatment in [`06-persona-positioning.md`](06-persona-positioning.md); this is who they are.**

| | **R1 — In-Person Seller** | **R2 — Team Leader** | **R3 — Catalog-Only** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sells** | Her own stock, in person | Her own stock **+ builds a team** | From the catalog; carries little stock |
| **Measured on** | Personal volume | **Team volume** | Commission |
| **Hero feature** | **Tap-to-Pay** | **Downline on her phone** | Payment links |
| **Gets value at an un-onboarded DSO?** | Partial — inventory only | ✅ **Fully** | ❌ **Barely** |
| **Evidence** | **HIGH** — 2 accounts, observed | **MEDIUM-HIGH** — 2 accounts, 2 methods | 🔴 **LOW — never observed** |

> ### 📊 Corroborated at a second account — Paparazzi Premiere survey, 2026-08-30
>
> **Adoption rises monotonically with rank: Director 100% (18/18) · Star Consultant 80% (20/25) · Consultant 50%.** Higher rank means a larger downline and higher personal volume.
>
> **This is R2 arrived at from a different account by a different method** — a survey rather than convention-floor observation. Two readings, both pointing the same way: higher-volume sellers have more reason to push through setup, *and* the product delivers more to someone with a team.
>
> ⚠️ **The inverse is the warning.** Adoption is lowest at the ranks where onboarding friction bites hardest — and **a cold Revolution placement reaches the whole rank distribution**, not just Directors.

> ### R2 is the discovery of the JE launch
>
> Team leaders were recorded pre-launch as a **VP Sales** interest — dashboards were "Hope's feature." The convention showed the same appetite at rep level, **and specifically for downline data.**
>
> **She is the most valuable rep in the motion**, for three compounding reasons: her hero feature works with **zero DSO involvement**, she is the field's most credible voice, and direct selling gives her a downline with financial incentive to follow her. [`10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md`](10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md) §4 builds its entire growth loop on her.

> ### R3 is the largest hole in the customer evidence
>
> Never observed. **And the Aice pairing rests on her** — the argument for launching both products together is that Aice covers the catalog-only field during the ProPay wait. **If Aice's free tier is drawn too thin, the pair does not cover her and the retention bridge does not hold.**
>
> **Pilot slot 2 exists to find her.** See [`03-alpha-test-results.md`](03-alpha-test-results.md).

## R4 — The Hobbyist: not a customer

Signed up for the rep discount on personal purchases. **Does not sell and does not intend to.**

> *"80% of our people are just the hobbyists that buy for themselves. They want the 25% off their customer."* — Brian B, CEO, Color Street, volunteered unprompted against his own interest

**She can download, hold Revolution Pro, look like an adopter, and generate $0 forever.** Under a payments-only revenue model she is not a slow adopter — she is outside the model by construction. **Named here so she is subtracted from denominators rather than discovered in a forecast.**

# Adoption Barriers

| Barrier | Severity | What answers it |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **"Not another app to set up"** | 🔴 **Highest — now evidenced** | **Her inventory is already in it.** The only answer, and it must be true on first open. **📊 Confirmed 2026-08-30: 6 of 7 Paparazzi non-adopters cited "getting started seemed too complicated" — and they had attended a training workshop.** If onboarding defeats a rep who showed up to learn, it defeats a cold rep outright. |
| **"Square is free and works"** | 🔴 High | Square knows nothing about her company. **Never argue price here** — Square is $0 too. |
| **Been sold to before** | 🟠 Med | Name the price immediately. Enthusiasm reads as a pitch. |
| **The app says Wayroo, her company doesn't** | 🟠 Med | **Data authenticates, not branding.** Untested — and the 3 branded pilots will not test it. |
| **Selling tools locked** | 🟡 **Lower than assumed** | She still gets inventory + dashboards on download. **Milder than previously believed.** |
| **$5 for non-Pro reps** | 🟠 Med | Against $60–100/month. Frame as substitution, never as a new bill. |

# Evidence Grade

| Claim | Confidence | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Payment friction is the felt pain | **HIGH** | Observed, 764 reps; cross-validated at Color Street |
| $60–100/month across 6–10 apps | **MEDIUM-HIGH** | One account, self-reported, specific and named |
| Earning/seeing beats organising | **HIGH** | Directly observed at scale |
| Media library is a DSO need, not hers | **HIGH** | Same room, same weekend, opposite reactions |
| R2 exists as a distinct persona | **MEDIUM** | One account, observed |
| SMS is a real gap | **MEDIUM** | Repeated and unprompted, one account |
| R3 behaves as described | 🔴 **LOW** | **Never observed** |
| Hobbyist ratio | **LOW** as a number · **HIGH** that it exists | One CEO's self-report |

> ### 🔴 One convention and one unclosed deal.
>
> JE's 764 reps are **0.34% of the 75,000 addressable population**, and Color Street never became a customer. **This archetype is the best-evidenced customer document in the library and it is still built on two accounts.** The pilot's three slots are the correction.
