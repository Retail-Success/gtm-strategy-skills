**Last updated:** May 10, 2026
**Derives from:** `outputs/12-sales-deck.md`, all `outputs/06-*` Phase 6 work
**Status:** Master v1 (Economic Buyer / ROI-focused) + Field Champion variant (Operational / Rep Experience-focused). Update after Phase 6 Task 5 messaging testing.

| Version | When | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Master (Economic Buyer / ROI)** | After first discovery call with CEO / Founder / VP Sales | Internal budget conversation; CFO/CEO alignment | Get approval for the deal |
| **Field Champion (Operational / Rep Experience)** | After first conversation with Field Ops Director / Distributor Relations leader | Internal field/operations alignment | Build internal momentum from the rep experience angle |

### Content

**Headline:**
> **We replace your reps' multi-tool patchwork (Square + Excel + Venmo + Shopify) with one native platform built for direct selling — capturing every customer to your back office, dropping wholesale processing fees from ~3% to 1%, and structurally passing all three FTC tests.**

**The Problem:**
> Your reps run their business across 4–6 disconnected tools — Square for in-person payments, Excel for inventory, Venmo for cash sales, PayPal and Shopify for online, Facebook for marketing. You absorb 3% on every Shopify transaction and the full chargeback liability across every rep. Every Venmo and cash sale is a customer your CRM will never reach, accumulating FTC compliance exposure with every untracked transaction.

**The Solution:**
> Wayroo + Freedom is the native end-to-end commerce platform for direct selling. Freedom is the corporate back office (comp engine, genealogy, replicated sites). Wayroo is the rep business operating system — every rep gets her own ERP-grade platform (POS, inventory, payments, customer records, shipping, storefront) through her own ProPay merchant account, all natively integrated with your back office, with no Shopify dependency.

**Key Benefits:**
- **Replace 4–6 rep tools with one app.** Each rep runs POS, inventory, customer records, branded invoices, and shipping from one platform — built for direct selling, not retrofitted from retail.
- **Drop your processing cost.** Per-rep ProPay merchant accounts shift chargeback liability off your single Shopify merchant. Spendback drops wholesale processing fees from ~3% to 1% (three accounts validated; Paparazzi quantified the savings).
- **Capture every customer your reps sell to.** Customer records auto-create in your Freedom back office — not synced, auto-created. Unlock retargeting, loyalty, and win-back to a marketing audience you previously couldn't reach.
- **Pass FTC contractor classification structurally.** Per-rep merchant model satisfies the three-pillar test (rep owns merchant + chargeback + transaction). 1099 from ProPay automatically. Protected from rep reclassification (existential cost exposure for any sizeable DSO).

**Proof:**
> **4,000 Paparazzi reps generated $31M in 2025 — 60%+ year-over-year growth — on Wayroo.**

>

**Pricing Summary:**
> $1/active rep/month (pass-through to your existing eSuite fee structure) + transaction-fee revenue share. SaaS license fee phases out as your processing volume scales — the more your reps sell, the cheaper Wayroo gets. Detailed deal economics modeling (Spendback savings projection, real-seller cohort sizing) provided in proposal.

## Segment-Specific Application Notes

The two versions above are master / Field Champion variants. The content is intentionally segment-agnostic. For high-priority prospects, customize three elements:

### For Cash & Carry DSO prospects (Color Street pattern)

**Master one-pager adjustments:**
- Replace "3% Shopify rate" with prospect-specific number if you have it
- Add to Key Benefits: *"Solve catalog rotation with sell-on-the-fly + vintage inventory generic SKUs — no historical inventory backfill required."*
- Replace generic Proof quote with C&C-specific: *"Even that would be helpful to them, right? At least they're scanning it, they have track and it's generating an invoice."* — CEO, on vintage inventory generic SKUs

**Field Champion one-pager adjustments:**
- Emphasize vendor event POS more (Bluetooth scanning, fast checkout, shipping labels from event table)
- Add LuLaRoe lineage explicitly if prospect has ex-LuLaRoe rep population

### For Hybrid DSO prospects (Jordan Essentials pattern, existing Freedom client)

**Master one-pager adjustments:**
- Headline reframe: *"Reverse a 6-month rep sales decline at your next convention. Wayroo overlays your existing Freedom back office to give every rep a real platform, capture every customer to your back office, and drop your wholesale processing fee from ~2.55% to 1%."*
- Replace "no Shopify dependency" with "no new back-office vendor"
- Add Convention forcing function: *"For DSOs with an upcoming stylist convention or annual event, Wayroo is the announcement that turns a status update into a re-engagement event."*
- De-emphasize FTC compliance (Jordan Essentials never raised it; pattern confirmed it's a C&C-majority pitch)

**Field Champion one-pager adjustments:**
- Lead with the channel-shift bet: *"For DSOs whose minority C&C cohort wants to grow into a real channel, POS + Media Library is the catalyst."*
- Emphasize Spendback for the non-C&C majority who place wholesale orders

## Production Specifications

**Format:**
- Single-page PDF, designed in Figma or Canva
- Brand colors per `my-gtm-context.md` Section 10 (ByDesign blue + clean black/white)
- Typography: clean sans-serif (Inter / Manrope / Plus Jakarta Sans)
- Logo: Wayroo + Freedom logos top-left; ByDesign Technologies attribution bottom

**Visual hierarchy (per the layout diagram above):**
- Headline = largest type, top of page after logo bar
- Section headers (THE PROBLEM, KEY BENEFITS, etc.) = small caps or bold sans-serif, 1.5x body type
- Body = readable at 12-14pt printed
- Proof stat ($31M) = visually emphasized — large display type
- CTA = button-style treatment, accent color

**Distribution:**
- Hosted as `/one-pager.pdf` at a tracked URL (UTM-tagged for source attribution)
- Sent as PDF attachment in follow-up emails after first discovery call
- Embedded in sales deck appendix as a "share with your team" page
- Print-ready file maintained for events / in-person handoffs

---

## What's Not in This One-Pager (Out of Scope)

- **Detailed feature lists.** The one-pager surfaces benefits + outcomes, not feature catalogs. Feature deep-dives belong in demos and the sales deck.
- **Detailed pricing.** High-level pricing summary only. Per-prospect deal economics live in the proposal.
- **Per-competitor battlecards.** Brief positioning vs. alternatives is implicit; full competitive comparison lives in a separate battlecard deliverable.
- **Track 2 (direct-to-rep) version.** This one-pager targets DSO corporate champions. A separate Track 2 rep-facing one-pager would have different language, different proof points, and different CTA — not yet developed.
