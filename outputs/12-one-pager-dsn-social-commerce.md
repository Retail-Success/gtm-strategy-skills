### What Wayroo Does

> Wayroo is the rep-facing mobile app that connects to your back office. It gives every rep one place to sell — a personal storefront, branded invoices with payment links, and card payments taken on their own phone — with their wholesale order and inventory **already loaded when they open it.**

### Four Outcomes

- **Capture the customers your reps have been selling to for years.** When a rep creates a customer in Wayroo, the record auto-creates in your back office — not synced on a schedule, created. A field-level customer base that was invisible becomes a marketing audience you can reach directly. *(Named unprompted as a "huge benefit" by executives at two separate DSOs.)*

- **Close the sale in the channel where it happened.** Three selling surfaces in one app — personal storefront, invoice with an embedded payment link for remote and post-event orders, and in-person card payments taken on the rep's phone with no reader hardware. Nothing routes through Venmo, so nothing leaves your system.

- **Pay reps the next day, into their own account.** Earnings land on a branded payout card the following day, spendable anywhere or usable to fund their next wholesale order. Each rep transacts through their own merchant account — which also moves chargeback and dispute liability off corporate.

- **Get the audit trail as a byproduct of normal selling.** Every transaction carries customer, amount, date, and location. That is the retail-sales documentation the FTC asks for and the earnings record 1099s and state sales-tax filings depend on — captured without asking a single rep to do paperwork.

# PAGE 2 — YOUR PITCH CARD (do not print, do not hand out)

## The 30-second open

> "Every company here can tell me what their field posts. Almost none can tell me what their field *sold* — because it settled in Venmo. We're the layer where the sale actually closes, and the customer record lands back in your back office."

Then stop and ask a question. **Do not describe the product until they've told you which version of the problem they have.**

## Three qualifying questions, in order

| # | Question | What the answer tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"What share of your reps carry personal inventory and sell it themselves?"** | Sizes the commerce fit. **Treat the answer as a hypothesis, not a fact** — DSO self-reported C&C share has been wrong in both directions at every account where we could check it. |
| 2 | **"When a rep sells face-to-face or in a DM, does that customer ever show up in your system?"** | The real opener. The answer is almost always *no*, and they rarely enjoy saying it out loud. |
| 3 | **"Who's your back office on?"** | Routes the whole conversation — see the two rooms below. |

## You are standing in two different rooms

| | **They run ByDesign** | **They run Exigo / something else** |
|---|---|---|
| **The ask** | Not a sale. *"Your reps can have this now."* | A platform conversation, at normal length |
| **Lead with** | It's already included with Revolution Pro — and you get field visibility you've never had | The customer-capture gap, then the platform |
| **The one step** | Enabling payments so the selling tools switch on | Scoping call |
| **Do not** | Quote a price. There isn't one to quote. | Promise integration timelines |

> **The strongest thing you can say to a non-client in this room:** *"An existing platform customer's reps get this at no extra charge. You'd get exactly the same the day you're on the platform."* That converts "why is it free for them" into a reason to buy the platform.

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"We already give our reps an app."** | "Does it know what they ordered from you? Wayroo opens with their wholesale order and inventory already in it, because it's reading your back office. That's the part a bolt-on can't do." |
| **"Square does this and it's free."** | "Square's genuinely good at POS. It also knows nothing about your company — no wholesale order, no inventory from corporate, and the customer never reaches your system. The difference isn't the features, it's the connection." |
| **"Another app is the last thing our field needs."** | "Agreed — which is why the argument is subtraction. Your reps are paying $60–100 a month out of pocket across six to ten tools. This replaces a stack, it doesn't join it." |
| **"What does this cost us?"** | *(ByDesign client)* "Nothing incremental. It's included with the Revolution Pro subscription your reps already hold." *(Non-client)* "It's part of the platform — let's scope it properly rather than me guessing a number on a trade-show floor." |
| **"How long does this take to roll out?"** | "Jordan Essentials went from first conversation to a live field in about eleven weeks, and 764 reps were on it within days of their convention. The forcing function was their own event." |
| **"Is this an MLM compliance product?"** | "It's a selling product that happens to produce the record. Reps adopt it because they get paid next day and can take a card at a vendor table — the audit trail is a byproduct. Nobody adopts software to be compliant." |

## The event mechanic

Direct selling conventions are the highest-leverage close in this business — **Jordan Essentials adopted at 29% of its active field in two to three days because the launch happened on a convention stage.** Ask every executive you meet: **"When's your next convention?"** A date on the calendar converts a quarterly-pace pipeline into an event-pace one, and it is the single best qualifier on the floor.

## 🔴 Claim boundaries — do not cross these on the floor

| 🚫 Never say | ✅ Why / say instead |
|---|---|
| **"Free"** | Say **"included."** *Free* invites "what's the catch" and reads as a trial. |
| **"764 and still growing"** | **No post-launch adoption data exists.** The supportable claim is "764 reps adopted within days." Full stop. |
| **"Wayroo reversed their sales decline"** | No pre-launch baseline was ever captured. **Unsupported — do not say it.** |
| **"Paparazzi's whole field runs on Wayroo"** | 4,000 adopters sit inside a ~20,000-rep field. *"4,000 reps generated $31M"* is true and stronger per rep. **Never imply full coverage.** |
| Feature-parity claims vs. **Hustle** or **Boards** | Neither has a feature-level teardown. We can say Hustle charges $5 per monthly-active rep; we cannot compare feature sets. |
| Anything about **Aice** | The acquisition is unsigned. **Do not mention the partnership, and do not run anti-Aice positioning.** |
| A **per-rep price** | $3, $5, and $1 all circulate in internal documents and do not reconcile. **Quote nothing until Finance resolves it.** |
| A **Tap-to-Pay ship date** | It is in trial at Jordan Essentials, not generally shipped. Describe the capability, never a date. |
| "Empowering" · "revolutionary" · "game-changing" | House voice is outcome-first with specific numbers. No hype words. |
