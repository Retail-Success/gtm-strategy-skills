**The ask:** *"We're building the app your company just rolled out and we want to hear how you actually run your business. Twenty minutes, no pitch, and I'll share what we learn."*

**Lead with research, not sales.** People help with research. Offer findings back. Do not offer a gift card to a rep — it reframes the conversation as a transaction and this population sells for a living.

# The Interview Guide

**Rules.** Ask about **last week**, not about opinions. Never describe the product before the problem questions. When they mention a tool, ask what it costs. **Shut up after a question** — the pause is where the real answer lives.

### Context (5 min)
1. *"How long have you been selling with [DSO]? Is this full-time or alongside something else?"*
2. *"Walk me through a normal week."*
3. *"Do you carry your own inventory, sell from the catalog, or both?"* → **classifies R1/R2/R3**
4. *"Do you have a team under you?"* → **R2 flag**

### Problem (15 min — the core)
5. 🔴 *"Walk me through your last vendor event. What did you carry, how did people pay, what did you do that night?"*
6. 🔴 *"When was the last time you lost a sale? What happened?"* → **the P1 test**
7. *"What happens after a wholesale order arrives?"* → **the P3 test**
8. *"How do you know what you have left?"*
9. *"What apps do you pay for out of your own pocket? Roughly what does that come to each month?"* → **the $60–100 test**
10. *"What do you check on your phone versus what makes you open a laptop?"*
11. *"If you could delete one task from your week, which?"*

### Impact (5 min)
12. *"What does that cost you — in hours, or in sales?"*
13. *"What have you tried that didn't work?"*

### Solution & language (5 min)
14. *"If you were describing [the app] to someone in your downline, what would you say it does?"* → 🔴 **the comprehension test — their words become our copy**
15. *"What would make it a no-brainer?"*

### For non-adopters only
16. 🔴 *"You saw this at convention and didn't download it. What went through your mind?"*
17. *"What would have changed that?"*

### For the locked-state test
18. *[Show the screen.]* *"What would you do next?"*
> ✅ **Pass:** *"I'd tell my company."* 🔴 **Fail:** *"How much is it?"* or *"So I can't use it."*

# Synthesis Framework — after all interviews

| Dimension | What to extract |
|---|---|
| **Pain frequency** | How many of N raised each problem **unprompted** |
| 🔴 **Language patterns** | Their exact words. **This is the highest-value output** — it becomes the copy. |
| **Persona differences** | Does R3 describe a different world? **The question these interviews exist to answer.** |
| **Comprehension** | Can they explain the product? If not, the positioning fails at the first hop. |
| **Non-adopter reasons** | The group nobody has heard from |
| **Surprises** | Things nobody asked about that came up twice |
