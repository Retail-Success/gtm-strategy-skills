# Phase 12 — ABM Program (Pragmatic Pilot)

**Phase:** 12 — Executing Sales, Task 8
**Purpose:** A focused, resource-realistic Account-Based Marketing pilot to accelerate the highest-leverage Wayroo + Freedom deals. ABM flips the funnel — instead of casting wide and qualifying down, start with specific named accounts and build personalized campaigns to engage them. For Wayroo's stage (1-person GTM team, 1-person sales rep, $36K/year marketing budget per `my-gtm-context.md`), this means **3–5 accounts done well**, not 50 accounts done thinly.
**Last updated:** May 11, 2026
**Derives from:** `outputs/12-dream-client-list.md`, `outputs/12-outbound-campaign.md`, `outputs/12-sales-deck.md`, `outputs/12-video-selling.md`, all `outputs/06-*` Phase 6 work
**Status:** v1 pilot program. 5-account Tier 1 pilot defined. Tier 2 deferred until Tier 1 proves ROI. Update at 90-day decision point.

## ABM Tier Design (Wayroo-Specific)

The GTM Strategist methodology defines three ABM tiers. For Wayroo's pilot scope, only Tier 1 is in scope; Tiers 2 and 3 are noted as future expansion.

| Tier | Methodology Standard | Wayroo v01 Pilot | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Full custom campaigns** | 5–10 accounts; personalized content, direct outreach, tailored ads | **3–5 accounts** (resource-constrained scope) | ✅ v01 pilot |
| **Tier 2 — Semi-personalized** | 10–25 accounts; industry-specific content, targeted ads, multi-thread outreach | Defer | ⏸ Day-90 decision |
| **Tier 3 — Programmatic** | 25–100 accounts; automated personalization, retargeting, templated sequences | Defer; Wayroo's volume can be handled by Outbound Campaign (Task 4) Tier B/C without ABM-specific infrastructure | ⏸ Future |

**Why Tier 2 + 3 are deferred:** Wayroo's outbound campaign (Task 4) already covers semi-personalized (Tier A treatment) and template-based (Tier B/C) outreach at the team's current capacity. Adding ABM Tier 2 + 3 without proving Tier 1 ROI would dilute already-thin resources.

## Per-Tier 1 Account Playbook

For each Tier 1 account, execute these five workstreams in parallel over the 90-day pilot.

### Workstream 1 — Research & Buying Committee Mapping

Per the GTM Strategist methodology, map the full buying committee for each account. Capture in CRM.

| Per-Account Output | Detail |
|---|---|
| Decision Maker (Economic Buyer) | Name, role, LinkedIn URL, email, last touchpoint, what they care about |
| Influencer (Technical Gatekeeper, Field Champion, etc.) | Name, role, LinkedIn URL, email |
| Champion (existing or potential internal advocate) | Name, role, why they'd champion |
| Blocker (potential or known) | Name, role, what they'd object to, mitigation |
| Account-specific pain pattern | What's unique about this account's situation |
| Account-specific trigger event | What changes in next 90 days (convention, launch, comp plan revision, leadership change) |

**Tools:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator (free with Daniel's existing seat); Clay or ZoomInfo for org-chart enrichment (Clay $149/mo if budget allows); Salesforce as system of record.

**Owner:** Sam Atieh (research + committee mapping) + Daniel/Cassie (relationship intelligence)

### Workstream 2 — Personalized Content

For each Tier 1 account, build at least one custom asset that's account-specific (not just a template with their logo swapped in).

**Per-account content options:**

| Asset | When to Use | Example for Color Street |
|---|---|---|
| **Personalized landing page** (`wayroo.com/colorstreet` or hidden URL) | Highest-impact ABM asset; sharable with internal buying committee | Headline: *"Color Street + Wayroo: Your reunion launch plan"*. Content: deal economics dashboard (4K real-seller cohort math), Path B integration architecture diagram, Spendback savings projection, August 7–9 reunion launch timeline, custom video from Autumn |
| **Account-specific industry report** | When buyer cares about strategic context | *"The state of cash-and-carry direct selling in 2026 — what 20K-stylist DSOs are doing differently"* with Color Street positioned implicitly throughout |
| **Custom deal economics model** | When CFO/Founder is in the buying committee | Per-account model showing Spendback savings, eSuite pass-through math, real-seller cohort sizing, Paparazzi MAU benchmark comparison |
| **Co-branded convention announcement playbook** | Hybrid DSO with convention forcing function | *"Jordan Essentials × Wayroo: Convention Announcement Playbook"* — joint asset Nancy + Hope can use internally to socialize the launch |

### Workstream 3 — Multi-Channel Engagement

Coordinate touchpoints across channels for each Tier 1 account. Per-account orchestration is the ABM differentiator vs. broad outbound.

| Channel | Tier 1 Application |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Coordinated send from Daniel + Sam + Autumn at different cadence touchpoints; references the personalized landing page and account-specific content |
| **LinkedIn** | Targeted ads to the buying committee at the account (using LinkedIn's account-targeting capability — $500–1,500/month per account for visibility); ByDesign team engaging with account's leadership LinkedIn content; sponsored InMails to specific buyers |
| **Video** | Personalized Loom videos per buying committee member (per `outputs/12-video-selling.md`) |
| **Direct mail (Tier 1 only)** | High-touch gift to top decision-makers — examples: a personalized hardcover copy of "Go-To-Market Strategist" by Maja Voje (since Wayroo's positioning is built on it); handwritten note from Sam Atieh; account-specific case study printed and bound |
| **Events** | DSA event in-person engagement; Sam/Daniel attend events where Tier 1 accounts will be present; pre-event LinkedIn outreach to confirm meetings on-site |
| **Webinars (joint with partners)** | Co-host webinars with Brett Duncan or other Tier 1 consultant partners; invite Tier 1 accounts specifically; topic tailored to their pain pattern |

**Budget guideline:** $1.5–3K per Tier 1 account for direct mail + LinkedIn ads + event presence.

### Workstream 4 — Sales-Marketing Alignment

ABM works only when sales and marketing operate as one team on each account. Define handoff points explicitly.

| Handoff Point | Trigger | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| Account selected for Tier 1 | Sam + Daniel agree this account qualifies | Account flagged in Salesforce; 90-day pilot timer starts |
| Marketing content live | Landing page published; first asset shared with committee | Daniel notified; outbound sequence pauses for 5 business days to let the asset land |
| Engagement signal | Buying committee member visits landing page / opens video / replies to email | Daniel notified within 24 hours; books follow-up call within 5 business days |
| Stalled signal | No engagement signal for 14+ days | Sam + Daniel review; either escalate (stuck deal reactivation video per Task 7) or de-prioritize |
| Pilot close decision | 90-day pilot mark | Sam + Daniel review pipeline impact; decide scale, iterate, or sunset |

**Owner:** Sam Atieh (program manager) + Daniel Lang (sales execution)

### Workstream 5 — Account-Specific Iteration

Every Tier 1 account should be reviewed weekly. ABM is high-touch; weekly recalibration is the cost of doing it well.

| Weekly Review Format | Output |
|---|---|
| 30-minute Sam + Daniel call per Tier 1 account | Engagement signals review; next-week action plan; budget check; objection patterns; sentiment shift |

## Metrics for ABM v01

Track per Tier 1 account, weekly.

| Metric | Target (per Tier 1 account, over 90 days) | What It Tells You |
|---|---|---|
| **Account engagement score** (interactions across channels) | 15+ interactions across full buying committee | Whether the personalized investment is landing |
| **Buying committee members engaged** | 3+ unique decision-makers at the account | Multi-thread depth (single-thread deals are fragile) |
| **Pipeline generated per Tier 1 account** | $50K+ ACV-equivalent moved through the funnel | Direct ROI signal |
| **Deal velocity** (time from ABM start to next major deal stage) | Acceleration vs. non-ABM baseline (4+ months historical) | Whether ABM is shortening cycle |
| **Win rate ABM accounts vs. non-ABM** | 40%+ win rate (vs. ~30% baseline) | Quality of qualification + execution |
| **Cost per Tier 1 opportunity** | $5–10K total per account (content + media + time) | ROI denominator |

**90-day ABM v01 ceiling targets:**
- 3 Tier 1 accounts engaged
- Color Street and Jordan Essentials both progress to proposal or closed-won
- 1 cold-start account moves from cold → first meeting → active discovery
- Total program cost: $15–25K (content production + LinkedIn ads + direct mail + Sam's time)
- 1+ deal closes attributable to ABM acceleration (likely Color Street given current stage)

## 90-Day Pilot Structure

### Days 1–14 — Foundation

- Sam + Daniel + Cassie + Autumn select the 3 Tier 1 accounts
- Map full buying committee per account
- Identify per-account triggers (convention dates, decision-maker changes, etc.)
- Draft per-account content plan (which assets to build)
- Configure Salesforce ABM tracking (account-level engagement scoring)
- Set up LinkedIn account-targeted ads (if budget allows)

### Days 15–45 — Content Production + Launch

- Build personalized landing pages for each Tier 1 account
- Produce account-specific content (deal economics models, personalized videos, custom reports)
- Direct mail send (if applicable per account context)
- LinkedIn ads live for each account
- Coordinated team outreach (Sam + Daniel + Autumn) per account playbook

### Days 46–75 — Engagement + Sales Acceleration

- Weekly review per Tier 1 account
- Real-time follow-up on engagement signals (visit landing page → call within 5 days)
- Sales meetings booked with full buying committee per account
- Refresh content where engagement stalled
- Capture account-specific learnings (what's working, what isn't)

### Days 76–90 — Pilot Close + Decision Point

- Final 14 days: aggressive close push on accelerated accounts
- Compile pilot performance report: engagement, pipeline, velocity, win rate, cost per opportunity
- Sam + Daniel decide: scale to Tier 2, iterate Tier 1, or sunset

### Day-90 Decision Logic

| Pilot Outcome | Decision |
|---|---|
| 2+ Tier 1 accounts close + ROI > 3x cost | **Scale** — add Tier 2 (10–15 semi-personalized accounts); add 2 more Tier 1 |
| 1 Tier 1 account closes + 1 in active proposal | **Iterate** — continue Tier 1; refine content + multi-channel orchestration; don't expand yet |
| 0 closes but engagement signals strong | **Iterate with caution** — extend pilot 30 days; review whether deal velocity actually improved |
| 0 closes + weak engagement | **Sunset** — ABM isn't right for Wayroo's current stage; redirect resources to outbound + partnership channels |

| Tier 1 pilot becomes a time-sink that distracts from broader outbound | Hard cap: Sam max 12 hrs/week ABM; Daniel max 6 hrs/week ABM; protect outbound velocity |
