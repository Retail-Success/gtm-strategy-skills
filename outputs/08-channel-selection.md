## The Selection Rule

The methodology says pick 2–3 channels and dominate them rather than being mediocre across five. With 20 hrs/week and one sales rep, that is not a preference — it is the only survivable option.

One additional filter applies here that most GTM plans don't face: **the channel has to be able to produce revenue inside 4.7 months, or be cheap enough that its 2027 payoff justifies the 2026 cost.** Channels that are neither get cut regardless of how attractive they look.

## Selected Channels

### Channel 1 — Installed Base (Direct Account Channel)

**This is the 2026 revenue channel. Everything else in this document is 2027 investment.**

A note on classification: an installed base is usually treated as a segment rather than a channel. Treating it as a channel here is deliberate — it has a distinct reach mechanism (account team relationships plus Freedom back-office data), a distinct owner, a distinct cadence, and a measurable funnel. It behaves like a channel in every way that matters operationally.

**Evidence it works:** Jordan Essentials. Existing Freedom client → Wayroo upsell → closed in ~11 weeks → 700+ downloads across a ~2,500 rep base in 48 hours → founder pulled her paid start forward to September. One account, but it traversed the entire funnel inside the time available.

**The unfair advantage no other channel has:** you already hold the qualification data. Freedom back-office reports show which clients have declining rep sales, declining engagement, and what their real C&C share is. **Every other channel requires you to ask a prospect to self-report. Here you can measure.**

That matters more than it sounds, because the Jordan Essentials launch proved self-reporting is unreliable — the ~20% C&C figure was confirmed understated at an account where ByDesign holds the data. If corporate leadership doesn't know their own numbers, a channel where you can derive them instead of asking is structurally superior.

### Channel 2 — Partner / Consultant Network

**The 2027 engine, and the largest correctable inefficiency in the current motion.**

**Evidence:** 2025 partner channel converted at **71.4% lead-to-opportunity** against Google Ads at 2.5% — roughly 29x. It produced $159,300 from 5 opportunities.

And it supplied **5 of 67 opportunities in 2025 — 7.5% of total volume.**

> The best-converting channel in the portfolio receives almost none of the input. This is not a performance problem; it is a supply problem, and supply problems are fixed with structure rather than money.

**Why it's cheap:** `outputs/12-partnership-program.md` is already fully specified — compensation, contracts, operating model, one-pager copy, 90-day pilot plan. It has never been launched. The work is execution, not design. Compensation is performance-based, so there is **no fixed spend and no downside risk**: $500 per qualified intro, 15% of first-year value on close.

**Sequencing note:** industry events (DSA, ANMP, DSN) are reclassified from a lead channel to a **partner-recruitment input**. They convert poorly and measure badly as a direct lead source, but they are the highest-density venue for meeting the consultants this channel runs on.

### Channel 3 — Intent-Based Inbound (Capterra + Website Organic)

**Bundled deliberately.** These are two surfaces of one motion: buyers already searching for a solution. They share the same asset requirements (case studies, positioning copy), the same owner, and near-identical time cost. Treating them as separate channels would breach the 3-channel limit for no operational gain.

**Evidence — this is where the revenue actually comes from:**

| Surface | 2025 Opps | Close Rate | 2025 Closed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website organic | 34 | ~17% | $570,910 |
| Google Natural Search | 16 | ~56% | $900,000 |
| Capterra | 7 | ~29% | $213,275 |
| **Total** | **57 (85% of all opps)** | | **$1,684,185 (91% of revenue)** |

**The strategic point:** this channel produces the overwhelming majority of revenue and receives almost no deliberate investment. It is not a growth bet — it is **an existing asset with an unattended maintenance requirement.**

**What "feeding it" means here — and what it does not.** It does not mean a content program; that violates the OPE constraint and lands in 2027 anyway. It means three cheap, bounded things:

1. **Publish the case studies** (Action 3) — Jordan Essentials adoption story and Paparazzi. These are the highest-converting page assets a B2B software buyer encounters, and both Capterra and organic search feed prospects directly into them.
2. **Reallocate the dead Google Ads budget into Capterra** (Action 4).
3. **Rewrite the Wayroo homepage headline.** `my-gtm-context.md` §10 already assesses the current line — *"One Platform That Connects Corporate Strategy to Field Action"* — as "repetitive, feature-descriptive, not outcome-first." One page of copy, roughly a half-day, sitting on the highest-traffic surface you own.

## Channel Review Discipline

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Monthly review** | First Monday. Opportunities sourced, cost per opportunity, conversion by stage. |
| **No new channels before January** | Three is the cap. Adding a fourth in-window means all four get under-served. |
| **Kill threshold** | Any channel below 5% lead-to-opp for two consecutive months goes to review — the standard Google Ads failed. |
| **Attribution hygiene** | Salesforce origin field must distinguish partner-sourced from inbound before Wk 2, or the 2027 budget decision rests on the same ambiguous data that produced the 71.4% close-rate-vs-lead-to-opp confusion. |
