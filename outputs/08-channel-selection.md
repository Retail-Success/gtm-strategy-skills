# Selected

## 1. 🔴 Revolution In-Product — the primary channel

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | **75,000 Revolution Pro subscribers are the audience, and Pro is the gate on Wayroo** — every viewer is eligible by definition |
| **Why it wins** | No permission, no media spend, **deterministic targeting from back-office data**, direct attribution |
| **Cadence** | Persistent placements, not campaigns |
| **Owner** | Sam (creative) + Product (surfaces) |
| **90-day goal** | **A measured impression → download → transacting rate.** The number the whole plan lacks. |
| **Monthly cost** | **~$0** |

**This is not really a marketing channel. It is a product surface inside software ByDesign owns** — and it should be designed like one. Full design: [`08-channel-strategy.md`](08-channel-strategy.md).

## 2. Field Leaders — the multiplier

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | Downline dashboards were a top-tier JE reaction · **Paparazzi adoption rises with rank — Director 100%, Consultant 50%** |
| **Why it wins** | **Direct selling is a pre-built referral network** with financial incentives already wired in. No other software category gets one free. |
| **Mechanic** | Activate leaders first; surface **"% of your downline transacting"** |
| **Owner** | Autumn + Cassie |
| **90-day goal** | Leaders transacting before their accounts launch |
| **Cost** | Time only |

**Already operationalised** in [`10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md`](10-transacting-adoption-playbook.md) §4. **Its strength is that it works at accounts where ProPay is still pending** — the placement can only sell what the account can deliver; a team leader's downline dashboard works everywhere.

## 3. Conventions — punctual, not continuous

**The only channel with a proven adoption number: 764 reps in 2–3 days at JE.** And the Premiere survey shows the live training format is the most praised element the business has, with repeat attendees returning for three years.

**Not a scalable channel** — it depends on someone else's calendar. **But it is the highest-conversion moment available, and it should be captured on video** (see [`07-demo-script.md`](07-demo-script.md)).

# Deprioritised

| Channel | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Paid social / search** | ⚠️ **Spending money to reach people we can already reach for free, inside our own software.** Revisit only if in-product saturates. |
| **SEO / content** | Reps do not search for this. 4–8 month payback against a channel that works today. |
| **LinkedIn** | **The rep persona is not there.** Relevant only if Track 1 new-logo marketing restarts. |
| **Newsletter to reps** | Email addresses sit with the DSO; in-product is more direct. See [`08-deferred-tactics.md`](08-deferred-tactics.md). |
| **Product Hunt / marketplaces** | Wrong audience entirely — tech early adopters, not direct-selling reps |
| **Community (Reddit, Facebook groups)** | Reps do congregate in **company-run** Facebook groups. **Posting there without the DSO is exactly the surprise the notify-don't-ask posture avoids** — and the field-leader loop reaches the same people without the grievance. |

> ### The honest note on one-channel concentration
>
> **Depending on a single channel is normally a risk.** Here it is a consequence of owning the distribution surface — but it means **if the placement converts poorly, there is no second channel warmed up.**
>
> **The field-leader loop is the hedge**, and it is the reason it is listed as a selected channel rather than a tactic. It needs no placement, no ProPay, and no permission.
