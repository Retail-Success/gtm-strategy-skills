# The Filter

Three questions. **Anything scoring less than 3/3 gets parked, not done.**

1. **Does this move the 90-day goal —** *a measured transact rate from a rep reached through Revolution?*
2. **Is there evidence it works for a direct-selling rep specifically?** Not for SaaS. Not in general.
3. **Can it be executed well with Sam + part-time Autumn + part-time Cassie?**

> ### The tiebreaker, when all three are yes
>
> **Does it produce evidence, or does it produce an artifact?**
>
> This library has ~30 strategy documents and **one measured number** (764 downloads). The constraint is not thinking. **It is that almost nothing has been tested.** When two tasks both pass the filter, do the one that produces a number.

# Weekly Focus Check

Five minutes, Friday.

1. **What was my #1 priority this week? Did I do it?**
2. **What number moved?** — *If none, why not?*
3. **What did I write that nobody will read?**
4. **What did I learn from someone outside the company?** — *If nothing, that is the finding.*
5. **What is my #1 next week?**

> **Question 3 is the honest one.** Question 4 is the one that would have changed the last three months.
