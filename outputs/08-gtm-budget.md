> ### The cash budget is close to zero, and that is the actual finding
>
> **No media spend. No sales cycle. No new contracts. No events we are paying for.** The channel is a product surface inside software ByDesign already owns, reaching an audience that is already a customer.
>
> **So the constraint was never money. It is engineering capacity and attention** — and a budget that only counts cash would show almost nothing while missing the entire binding constraint.

# The Forgone-Revenue Line

**Nobody writes a cheque, so this does not appear in a cash budget — and it is the largest number in the plan.**

| Item | Forgone |
|---|---|
| **3 × white-label at $25,000** | **~$75,000** |
| **Wayroo Essentials free to 75,000 reps** | The whole rep-facing SaaS line, permanently |
| **Not charging Hustle's $5/monthly-active-rep** | ⚠️ **Paparazzi alone: ~20,000 reps.** At Hustle's rate that is a very large annual number we chose not to pursue. |

**All three are deliberate.** Free removes the cost objection, transaction pricing stops DSOs rationing access, and branded pilots buy reference accounts the motion has never had.

> **But they should be recorded as spend, because that is what they are.** A plan whose cash budget is $10K and whose forgone revenue is six or seven figures is not a cheap plan — **it is a plan that pays in margin instead of cash**, and the two decisions deserve the same scrutiny.

> ### LTV:CAC above 50:1 is not a sign of health — it is a sign the ratio is the wrong tool
>
> With CAC near zero, **LTV:CAC tells you nothing about whether this works.** It will look spectacular whether the motion succeeds or fails.
>
> 🔴 **The metric that actually matters is transact rate**, and it has never been measured. **Judge this budget on whether it produces that number**, not on its efficiency ratios.

# Budget Rules

1. 🚫 **No paid media until in-product is saturated and measured.** Paying to reach people we already reach free is the clearest waste available.
2. **Cap unproven channels at 10% of GTM time.** The in-product channel is unproven too — but it is nearly free, so the cap applies to *alternatives* to it.
3. **Convention travel only when a client's calendar creates the moment.** Do not manufacture events.
4. 🔴 **Treat the engineering ask as the budget.** If it does not get capacity, no amount of cash substitutes.
5. **Review monthly against transact rate**, not against spend efficiency.
