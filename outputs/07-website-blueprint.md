> ### Task 1 reframed, because there is no web funnel
>
> The standard blueprint assumes strangers arrive on a homepage. **This motion has no strangers.** Reps are reached inside Revolution — logged in, identified, and pre-qualified. **A marketing homepage would serve almost nobody in this funnel.**
>
> **But there is one page every single rep sees, and it has never been audited:**
>
> ### 🔴 The app store listing.
>
> It sits between the placement click and the download. **It is the last thing a rep reads before deciding**, and it is the only surface in this funnel that behaves like a landing page.
>
> Given that **6 of 7 Paparazzi non-adopters stalled on "getting started seemed too complicated,"** what this page promises about effort matters more than what it promises about features.

# The App Store Listing

## Title & subtitle

| Field | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **App name** | **Wayroo** *(branded pilots: their name, e.g. Premiere)* |
| **Subtitle** (30 chars) | 🔴 **"Your inventory's already in it"** — the data claim, and it fits |
| Alternative | *"The app that comes with your back office"* — clearer, longer, weaker as a subtitle |

⚠️ **Do not lead the subtitle with "free."** It invites *"what's the catch?"* and reads as a trial — the same reason the messaging house uses *included* throughout.

## First three lines of the description

**These are the only lines shown before "more" on both stores.** They carry the whole page.

> **Your wholesale order is already in it. Nothing to set up.**
>
> **Wayroo is the selling app that comes with your back office** — your inventory, your customers, and your numbers are already there, because it runs on the same system your company does.
>
> **Take card payments on your phone. Send an invoice. Ring up an event.**

**Line 1 is the objection-killer**, and it is the one with evidence behind it. Line 2 is the category. Line 3 is the hook.

## Full description structure

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Opening** | The three lines above |
| **"Nothing to set up"** | Wholesale orders arrive loaded · no spreadsheet, no SKU entry · mark received as boxes land |
| **"Get paid however she wants to pay"** | Tap-to-Pay · invoicing with payment links · event checkout with barcode scanning |
| **"Your numbers on your phone"** | Sales, downline, performance — live from the back office, no second login |
| **"Included with Revolution Pro"** | ⚠️ **State the Pro relationship plainly.** The listing reaches non-Pro reps too — per Pillar 3a/3b, never claim unqualified inclusion on an open surface. |
| **Support** | Where to get help |

## Screenshots — the highest-leverage element

**Most reps decide on screenshots, not description.** Order matters:

| # | Screenshot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 🔴 **A populated inventory screen with real product images** | **USP 1, proved before download.** Not a feature list, not a hero graphic. |
| **2** | Tap-to-Pay in use | The hook |
| **3** | The dashboard with real numbers | R2, and it works with no DSO involvement |
| **4** | An invoice a customer receives | The professionalism job |
| **5** | Event checkout | R1 |

> **Screenshot 1 is the whole page.** A rep who sees a stocked inventory screen understands the product's claim without reading a word — and it directly answers the reason non-adopters gave for not starting.

# Open Surfaces — deliberately minimal

**Only for traffic that arrives without a known Pro status** — an Aice placement, a shared link, a convention QR code.

| Surface | Build? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **App store listing** | ✅ **Yes — audit now** | The real page |
| **A single landing page** | 🟡 **One, if any** | Only if links need a destination. **Use the open-surface UVP** — *"Everything you sell, from one app — with your inventory already in it"* — and **state the Pro relationship plainly rather than claiming inclusion.** |
| **Help centre** | ✅ Existing standards | Reps land here after adoption |
| Marketing homepage | 🚫 **No** | No audience arrives this way |
| Pricing page | 🚫 **No** | There is no price to display |
| Blog / SEO | 🚫 **No** | Not an acquisition channel here |

# Audit Checklist — do this before the placement ships

The listing exists today and nobody has reviewed it against the current positioning.

- [ ] 🔴 **Does screenshot 1 show populated inventory with real product images?**
- [ ] Do the first three lines lead with *nothing to set up*?
- [ ] Is *free* used anywhere it should say *included*?
- [ ] Is the Revolution Pro relationship stated plainly, without over-claiming inclusion?
- [ ] Any paywall grammar — *upgrade*, *premium*, *unlock*?
- [ ] Is **Reply to Buy** promoted? 🚫 **Remove it** — 3 use it, 24 struggle with it
- [ ] Do branded pilot listings follow the same structure?
- [ ] Are screenshots current, or from an older build?

# What Is Explicitly Not Needed

Marketing homepage · pricing page · blog · SEO strategy · cookie banner · hero video · testimonials wall.

**All assume an anonymous visitor arriving from search or social.** This funnel has neither. **Building them would produce a website nobody in the motion visits** — and the attention is better spent on the one page every rep actually reads.
