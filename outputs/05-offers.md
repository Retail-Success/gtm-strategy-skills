> **Three audiences, three offers.** The rep with Revolution Pro, the rep without it, and the DSO. **Getting the first two mixed up is the fastest way to lose the audience** — telling a non-Pro rep it is free and then switching her store off is the failure mode the messaging house exists to prevent.

> ## **Wayroo. It's already yours.**
>
> The selling app that comes with your back office — **included with the Revolution Pro you already pay for.**
>
> - **Your inventory is already in it.** Wholesale orders arrive loaded. No spreadsheet, no SKU entry.
> - **Take card payments on your phone.** No reader, no hardware.
> - **Send an invoice** with a payment link, from anywhere.
> - **Your numbers and your team's, on your phone** — no laptop, no second login.
> - **Ring up an event** with barcode scanning; inventory updates itself.
>
> **Nothing to buy. Nothing to set up. Open it and your business is already in there.**
>
> **[ Open Wayroo ]**

**For:** any rep on Revolution Pro. **Price:** $0 incremental — **and "included" is unqualified truth for this audience.**

⚠️ **Never show this version to a rep without Pro.**

# Offer 2 — The Rep Without Revolution Pro *(JE's 510, and the equivalent everywhere)*

> ## **$5 a month. Here's exactly what it replaces.**
>
> Wayroo comes with **Revolution Pro — $5/month**, and you can turn it on yourself in about a minute.
>
> **What you're paying for six to ten apps today:** Square, a spreadsheet, PayPal or Venmo, a separate invoicing tool. **Most reps tell us that runs $60–100 a month.**
>
> **What $5 gets you:** card payments on your phone · invoicing with payment links · event checkout · your inventory, already loaded from your wholesale orders · your sales and your team's, on your phone.
>
> **[ Turn on Revolution Pro — $5/month ]**

**For:** reps who want Wayroo and lack Pro. **Price:** $5/mo.

> ### This is the stronger economic argument, not the weaker one.
> A rep spending $60–100/month is being offered a **12–20x substitution below every alternative in the category.** It just has a number in it — **so name the number early.** Vagueness confirms the suspicion of a rep who has been sold to before.

**Risk reversal:** *"Turn it on yourself, cancel any time."* **Self-serve is the risk reversal** — it removes the "wait on my upline" stall.

>

>
> **What's not switched on is card payments.** That needs **ProPay approval** — underwriting paperwork with our processor, not a purchase and not a project.
>
> **[N] of your reps have already asked for it.**
>

**For:** CEO/Founder, VP Sales, Finance/Ops. **Price:** $0. **The ask is paperwork.**

# Offer 4 — The Founding Pilot *(3 DSOs only)*

> ## **Founding Partner Pilot — three companies.**
>
> Everything above, plus:
>
> ### **Custom branding, free — a $25,000 build.**
> Your name, your look, your field's app.
>
> **What we need:** ProPay approval, a launch moment, and permission to talk about how it went.
>
> **First three partners. Then it closes.**

**Two rules, and they are what protect the price:**

**1. Always say the $25,000.** A gift with a number is a discount. **A gift without one becomes the new price.**

**2. Bound it — "the first three."** Scarcity is what stops this becoming the standard offer, and it is also what makes saying yes quickly worth something. **Never call it "pilot pricing."** It is a founding-partner arrangement with a closed door.

# Offer 5 — Rung 2 🔴 Not ready

> ## **Wayroo Pro + Aice Pro — [unpriced]**

**Blocked on three decisions**, and they are the same decision: **what goes in it** (SMS is the strongest candidate — most-requested, non-transactional, and reps pay $10–30/mo for it today) · **the Aice free/paid line** · **the price** (band suggested by the market: $10–15 on top of the $5).

🔴 **Design rule that binds absolutely: nothing transactional.** Tap-to-Pay, invoicing, POS, storefront and Dropship can never move behind this tier. **Suppressing a transacting rep costs ~$216/year against an upgrade that will not approach it.**

# Anchoring & Structure

| Principle | Applied |
|---|---|
| **Anchoring** | 🔴 **Against the rep's own $60–100 stack, never against Square.** Square is $0 — anchoring on price there loses the room. |
| **The $25,000 anchor** | Makes free read as a concession rather than a giveaway |
| **Decoy** | ❌ **Not used.** With two rep tiers ($0 and $5) there is no decoy to build, and manufacturing one would be dishonest. |
| **Charm pricing** | ❌ Not used. $5 is already trivially small; $4.99 would look like a tactic. |
| **Risk reversal** | Self-serve activation and cancellation |
| **Urgency** | **Only in offer 4**, and it is real — three slots. **Do not manufacture it elsewhere.** |

---

# What Not to Say

| 🚫 Never | Why |
|---|---|
| *"Free"* to a non-Pro rep | **It isn't, and the store shutoff proves it** |
| *"Cheaper than Square"* | Subscription: both $0. **Processing: confirmed 2026-09-02 as only slightly below Square** — not enough to move anyone. **Say "about the same rate, and it does what Square can't."** |
| *"Replaces all your apps"* | **Canva and SMS survive.** Overclaiming loses the skeptical rep. |
| *"Upgrade / Premium / Unlock"* | Paywall grammar — banned in rep-facing copy |
| *"Limited-time"* on the free tier | It isn't limited, and saying so makes it sound like a trial |
