## The frame

> **Most rep apps help the rep *sell*. Wayroo helps the rep *run the business*, and it's connected to the company's back office.**

| Category | What it answers for the rep | Who's here |
|---|---|---|
| **Content and creation** | "What do I post?" | **Aice** *(partner / acquisition target)* |
| **Sales enablement and coaching** | "What should I do next, and what do I share?" | **Hussle**, **NowSite** *(partner)*, much of **Fluid** |
| **Commerce front end** | "Where does my customer check out?" | **Fluid** (storefronts, checkout, subscriptions) |
| **Back office extended to the field** | "How do I take the payment, track my stock, invoice, see my numbers and get paid?" | **Wayroo** |

That's the frame you described. The research supports it for Hussle, NowSite and Aice. **For Fluid it's only partly true, and that matters** (see below).

---

## 🔴 Three findings that change what the pitch can say

### 1. Fluid is a commerce competitor, not just a sales-coaching tool

Fluid calls itself *"the complete AI platform for your business"* and *"bring commerce, payments, customers, and your field together."* Its product includes **branded storefronts and checkouts, rep-specific selling links and personal storefronts, payments and subscription billing, CRM, analytics, compliance monitoring**, and an AI assistant ("Mist"). It **integrates with Exigo, ByDesign and Pillars.**

- **Overlap with Wayroo:** rep storefronts, payments, CRM and dashboards. It's the closest competitor on the list.
- **Where we found no evidence of Fluid (verify before claiming):** Tap-to-Pay or in-person POS, rep inventory synced from the wholesale order, vendor-event selling with barcode scanning, invoices with payment links, or **per-rep merchant accounts**. Fluid's payments look like company-level checkout, not "each rep is her own merchant."
- **Fluid is already inside our pipeline.** Third-party coverage of its raise lists **Color Street** as deploying Fluid distributor microsites, and Color Street is our live Wayroo-only prospect. Also listed: Thermomix and ASEA.
- **Funding:** $15M raised (announced March 2026), with $3M ARR reached in six months.
- **It has bought a rep app before:** in July 2024 Fluid acquired **NOW Tech**, a direct-selling mobile app company.

### 2. Fluid says it's free, so "they charge reps a monthly fee" doesn't hold for Fluid

Fluid **made We-Commerce free** after reaching $3M ARR. Who pays what isn't public: it may charge DSOs for other modules or earn on payments. But **you can't tell a prospect "Fluid charges your reps a monthly fee"** without proof. A DSO evaluating Fluid would catch that immediately.

Who actually pays, as far as we can verify:

| App | Who pays | What we know |
|---|---|---|
| **Hussle** | **The DSO** | "Usage-based agreements rather than seat-based licensing." Paparazzi pays **~$5 per monthly active rep** (internal figure, `my-gtm-context.md` §6). |
| **Fluid** | **Unclear** | "Made We-Commerce free." Monetization not public. |
| **NowSite** | **The rep** | App Store listing is "free," but reviews mention recurring charges (~$20) and refund complaints. Rep-paid subscription. |
| **Aice** | Rep or DSO | Free tier plus paid pro tier (internal). |
| **Wayroo** | **Nobody, until the rep sells** | ByDesign earns ~1.5% of rep payments. Core app and selling tools stay free. |

**The defensible version of the pitch line:** *"Most rep apps charge somebody a fee whether or not your reps sell. Wayroo only makes money when your rep starts making money."* That's true across the whole table, including Fluid, since its free tier exists to sell something else.

### 3. "Hustle" is actually **Hussle** (hussle.tech)

Hussle's customer logos include **Paparazzi**, Amare, Nature's Sunshine, Shaklee, USANA and Arbonne, and it claims 2,000,000 users. That's the same company as the "Hustle" in our files. **Fix the spelling in every customer-facing asset.** Misspelling a competitor's name in front of a DSO that uses it costs credibility.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Positioning** | "The complete AI platform for your business." *We-Commerce*: relationship-based selling for direct sales. |
| **Category** | Commerce front end + enablement + AI |

| **Where Wayroo is different (verify first)** | In-person selling (Tap-to-Pay, vendor-event POS, barcode scanning), **rep inventory synced from the wholesale order**, invoices with payment links, **per-rep merchant accounts** (chargebacks sit with the rep), and the rep's own sales, team and rank numbers from the back office |

## Positioning map

Two axes that matter to a DSO: **what the app does for the rep** (helps her sell vs. runs her business), and **how connected it is to the company** (standalone vs. connected to the back office).

```
                      CONNECTED TO THE DSO BACK OFFICE
                                    ▲
                                    │
                 Fluid ●            │            ● Wayroo
     (storefronts, checkout,        │   (payments, inventory, invoicing,
      content, AI; integrates       │    POS, rep's own numbers;
      with commission engines)      │    extends the back office)
                                    │
                Hussle ●            │
     (content, LMS, tasks;          │
      100+ integrations)            │
  HELPS THE REP SELL ◄──────────────┼──────────────► RUNS THE REP'S BUSINESS
                                    │
       Aice ●                       │
   (content creation,               │
    community)                      │          (Square, Venmo, spreadsheets
                NowSite ●           │           sit down here: run pieces
   (social leads, sales sites)      │           of the business, connected
                                    │           to nothing)
                                    ▼
                               STANDALONE
```

**The white space Wayroo owns:** top right. It runs the rep's business *and* is connected to the company. Fluid is the only competitor moving toward that corner. Square and Venmo run pieces of the business but connect to nothing.

## Gap analysis: what nobody else does

Based on public information. **Verify each against Fluid before saying it out loud.**

| Capability | Wayroo | Fluid | Hussle | NowSite | Aice |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tap-to-Pay / in-person card payments | ✅ | Not found | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Vendor-event POS with inventory countdown | ✅ | Not found | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Rep inventory loaded from her wholesale order | ✅ | Not found | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Invoice with payment link | ✅ | Not found | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Per-rep merchant account (chargebacks on rep) | ✅ | Not found | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Rep personal storefront | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | Sales sites | ❌ |
| Rep's sales / team / rank numbers from back office | ✅ | Dashboards (scope unclear) | Reports | ❌ | ❌ |
| Content library / media | ✅ (partial) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Training / LMS | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | AI plans | ✅ |
| AI assistant | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Free to the rep | ✅ | ✅ (We-Commerce) | DSO pays | ❌ Rep pays | Free tier |

**Read it honestly.** Wayroo wins on running the business in person and on the transaction. It loses on training, AI and content depth. That's why it partners with Aice and NowSite rather than competing on those.

## What this means for the beta pitch (beat by beat)

| Beat | Change |
|---|---|
| **1. Why I'm calling** | No change |
| **2. Their reps' world** | Add the category line: *"Most rep apps help your reps sell: content, posts, training. Wayroo is different. It's where she actually runs the business."* |
| **3. What it costs them** | 🔴 **Soften the fee line.** Change *"the first thing most rep apps ask for is a monthly fee"* to *"a lot of tools charge a monthly fee whether or not she's sold anything."* Don't make it about any specific competitor, since NowSite (a partner) is rep-paid and Fluid says it's free. |
| **4. What Wayroo does** | Lead with what Fluid and Hussle don't do: **Tap-to-Pay, vendor-event selling, inventory from her wholesale order, invoices with payment links.** Put the storefront last, because Fluid has one too. |
| **5. What you get** | Add for anyone evaluating Fluid: *"every rep is her own merchant, so chargebacks are hers, not yours."* |
| **6. Proof** | No change |
| **7. How we make money** | Keep *"we only make money when your rep starts making money."* It's true against every competitor. Drop *"most rep apps charge your reps a monthly fee"* unless they're comparing a rep-paid app. |
| **8. The ask** | Add: *"Already using Hussle, NowSite or a content tool? Keep it. Wayroo sits next to it and handles the selling."* That makes Wayroo additive, not a rip-and-replace. |

### New objection responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| **"We're looking at Fluid."** | "Fluid is strong on storefronts, content and AI. The question is what happens at a vendor table, or when a rep needs to invoice a customer or knows what's in her inventory. That's where Wayroo lives. Ask them to show you Tap-to-Pay and per-rep merchant accounts." *(Verify before saying they can't.)* |
| **"Fluid is free too."** | "So is Wayroo for beta partners. The difference is what each one is built to do. Fluid's free tier gets you their commerce front end. Ours gets your reps taking payments and running their business." |
| **"We already pay for Hussle."** | "Keep it if your team likes it. Hussle is great for content and training. It doesn't take payments or track inventory, and that's what Wayroo does." |

### Claim guardrails (add to the pitch)

| 🚫 Don't say | Why |
|---|---|
| "Fluid charges your reps a monthly fee" | Fluid says We-Commerce is free |
| "Fluid doesn't do payments" | It does: checkout, subscriptions, payment processing |
| "Fluid can't do Tap-to-Pay / inventory / per-rep merchant" | Not verified. Say "ask them to show you." |
| "Hustle" | It's **Hussle** |
| Anything negative about NowSite or Aice | Partners |
| NowSite's billing complaints | Partner, and it doesn't help us |
