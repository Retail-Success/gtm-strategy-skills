## Copy rules for every ad

- **Claims allowed** (`06-brand-architecture-2027.md`): one system from back office to field; reps take payment on their phone (Tap-to-Pay is claimable in copy; confirm it's on in production before any live demo); one record of every sale; comp-plan design from the team that runs the engine.
- **Don't claim:** Cart V3, Enrollment or Branding Studio (beta); AI custom development (no product); that Exigo lacks a rep app; a joint venture with Aice; prices (D8 is open); "free" (the offer shape is open).
- **No customer names in ads** until each customer approves. Jordan Essentials may be named to non-clients (context, 2026-10-05), but **not in paid ads without fresh sign-off**, and not with adoption numbers.
- **Voice:** confident, brief, outcome-first. No em dashes. None of: leverage, harness, unlock, robust, cutting-edge, empower.

## Test plan and kill rules

| Weeks | What changes | What stays fixed |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 (from 2027-02-12) | LinkedIn audiences: Tier 1 vs. Tier 2 | Copy W1 |
| 3–4 | LinkedIn copy: W1–W3 (plus A1–A2 if Aice signs off) | The winning audience |
| 5–7 | Google keyword pruning; Capterra bid | — |
| 2027-04-01 | Read everything; scale for the May launch or cut | — |

**Kill rules:**
- An ad set with **200+ clicks and no demo request** gets new copy or stops.
- A channel with **no qualified opportunity in 60 days** stops.
- **No more than 30% of spend** on an unproven channel.
