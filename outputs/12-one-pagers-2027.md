## One-pager A — ByDesign + Wayroo (DSOs that need a back office)

**Headline:** ByDesign runs the business. Wayroo runs the field.

**The problem:** Your home office runs on one system; your reps run on ten. Sales, customers and stock that live in Square, Venmo and Facebook never reach you, and each tool you add is another contract and integration to manage.

**The solution:** ByDesign runs commissions, orders, enrollment and inventory. Every rep gets Wayroo, the app connected to your back office from day one, with training and community from our partner Aice built in.

**Key benefits:**
- **See** every rep's sales and customers, not just what flows through your cart.
- **Equip** every rep with one app to sell, take payment and track inventory.
- **Pay** the plan you designed: comp-plan design and review on the engine that runs it.
- **Move** with a named migration lead and a plan built around your commission run.

**Proof:** [Paparazzi line, only with sign-off: "Reps on Wayroo, about a fifth of the field, sold $31M in 2025."] · Jordan Essentials, live on ByDesign and Wayroo since July 2026 · References: Healthy Home, J.Hilburn, Benevita.

**Pricing:** Platform licence with Wayroo included. Pricing in your proposal.

## One-pager B — Wayroo, powered by ByDesign (DSOs that keep their back office)

**Headline:** Your back office, in every rep's pocket.

**The problem:** Your reps run their business on their phones, mostly on apps you can't see. Sales happen off your rails, customer data stays with the apps, and new reps pay for tools before they've made a sale.

**The solution:** Wayroo connects to the back office you already run. Reps sell in person, online or by invoice, get paid on the spot, and see their sales and inventory in one app. Training, community and content from our partner Aice are inside.

**Key benefits:**
- **Keep** your back office. Wayroo connects to it.
- **Take** payment on the rep's phone with tap to pay, invoices and a storefront.
- **Track** inventory that loads from the wholesale order and updates as reps sell.
- **See** every sale and customer back at the home office.

**Proof:** [Paparazzi line, only with sign-off] · Jordan Essentials, live on Wayroo since July 2026 · [LivePURE, from Q2 2027, with permission].
