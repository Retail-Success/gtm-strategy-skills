# The Top 5 — highest severity × frequency × worst workaround

| # | Problem | Why it ranks | Wayroo's answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 🔴 **Loses the sale when the customer has no cash** | Hair on fire, every event, workaround is *lose the money* | **Tap-to-Pay** | **Strongest observed reaction of the entire JE launch** |
| **2** | 🔴 **$60–100/month for 6–10 disconnected apps** | Recurring, self-funded, and she can name the number | **Included at $0** | JE, named tools, self-reported |
| **3** | 🔴 **Manual SKU entry and inventory reconciliation** | Hair on fire, every order, workaround is a spreadsheet she abandons | **Pre-loaded from the wholesale order** | Cross-validated, 4 accounts |
| **4** | **Unprofessional payment and no customer receipt** | Emotional + social + repeat-purchase loss | **Branded invoices with payment links** | JE top-tier reaction |
| **5** | **No visibility into her own or her team's numbers** | Weekly, workaround is a desktop login | **Dashboard widgets — and they work with zero DSO involvement** | JE top-tier reaction |

> ### The pattern that should govern everything rep-facing
>
> **Every top-5 problem is about earning or seeing. None is about organising, filing, or complying.**
>
> Observed directly: compliance, media library, and inventory-*organisation* framing did not register at rep level. **Inventory that is simply already correct is valuable; inventory management as a capability is not** — Square does that, free, and she has used it.

# Problems Wayroo Does NOT Solve

Naming these prevents overclaiming, and two of them are live product gaps.

| Problem | Status |
|---|---|
| 🔴 **No way to message customers (SMS)** | **Real gap.** Repeated, unprompted rep requests at JE for Project Broadcast. She pays for it separately today. |
| **Content and graphics (Canva)** | Not addressed. Part of the $60–100 that survives. |
| **Training, team chat, community** | **Aice's half of the pair** — which is why the catalog-only rep needs both |
| **Recruiting and downline building** | Out of scope |
| **Finding customers** | Out of scope. **Wayroo helps her sell to people she already found.** |

> ⚠️ **Do not claim Wayroo replaces the whole $60–100 stack.** It replaces the selling and record-keeping half. **The honest claim is a large dent, not a clean sweep** — and the honest version survives contact with a rep who still has a Canva subscription.

# Validation Questions

For the next 5–10 rep conversations. **Ask about last week, not about opinions.**

1. *"Walk me through your last vendor event. What did you carry, how did people pay you, and what did you do that night?"*
2. *"What apps do you pay for out of your own pocket? Roughly what does that come to?"*
3. *"When was the last time you lost a sale, and what happened?"*
4. *"After a wholesale order arrives, what do you actually do with it?"*
5. *"How do you know what you have left?"*
6. *"What do you check on your phone versus what makes you open a laptop?"*
7. *"If you could delete one task from your week, which one?"*

> **Question 3 is the one to listen hardest to.** If the answer is a payment problem, the Tap-to-Pay hook is validated outside JE. If it is something else, the lead needs to change.
