## UVP vs. USP — The Distinction

| | UVP (Unique Value Proposition) | USP (Unique Selling Point) |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | The value promise to customers | The specific feature/attribute unique to Wayroo |
| **Audience** | Customer-facing (marketing, website, pitch) | Internal + sales (why we win deals) |
| **Example (Wayroo)** | "Replace Square + Venmo + Excel + Shopify with one native platform built for direct selling..." | "Per-rep ProPay merchant accounts with two-tier underwriting" |
| **Test** | Does the customer say "I want that"? | Can a competitor truthfully claim this? |

## Unique Value Propositions

### Master UVP — DSO Leader Audience

> **Replace Square + Venmo + Excel + Shopify with one native platform built for direct selling — without ripping out your back office, without absorbing 3% on every transaction, and without adding a vendor your reps will abandon in two events.**

**UVP formula breakdown:**
- **End result:** Replace the fragmented patchwork with one platform
- **Qualifier:** Native + built for direct selling (vs. retrofitted general-purpose tools)
- **Without main fears:** (1) rip-and-replace back office, (2) Shopify TCO at scale, (3) rep abandonment risk

### Master UVP — Rep Audience (Track 1 End User)

> **One app to run your business — sell anywhere, get paid through your own merchant account, manage inventory, file your taxes — without juggling Square, Excel, Venmo, and PayPal.**

### C&C Variant UVP

> **Turn every stylist into a real sub-business owner — branded storefront, mobile POS, her own ProPay merchant account, automatic 1099 from her processor — all in one app, all visible to corporate, all under your brand. Without ripping out the back office or absorbing 3% on every cash-and-carry transaction.**

### Hybrid Variant UVP

> **Reverse a 6-month rep sales decline at your next convention. Overlay your existing Freedom back office with the rep app that grows your cash-and-carry channel, captures every customer your reps sell to, and drops your wholesale processing fee from 2.55%+ to 1% — built on the platform you're already running.**

### UVP Quality Checks

| Test | Master DSO | Master Rep | C&C Variant | Hybrid Variant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Specific** (numbers, timeframes, concrete outcomes) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ (6-month decline, 2.55→1%) |
| **Desirable** (customer wants this outcome) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Credible** (evidenced/demonstrable) | ✅ (Paparazzi $31M) | ✅ | ✅ (Paparazzi + per-rep merchant fact) | ✅ (Paparazzi recovery + Spendback math) |
| **Unique** (differentiated from alternatives) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

### Confirmed Truly Unique USPs (Defensible in Any Competitive Conversation)

1. **Per-rep ProPay merchant accounts with two-tier underwriting**
2. **Three rep selling channels in one app** (storefront + invoicing + vendor POS)
3. **Sell-on-the-fly + vintage inventory generic SKUs** (catalog-rotation deal-unblocker)
4. **Spendback** (1% wholesale processing vs. 3% Shopify rate)
5. **Dropship with individual rep merchant accounts** (first-mover, no competitor equivalent)
6. **Three-pillar FTC compliance pass-through** (retail docs + contractor classification + tax)
7. **Path B integration architecture** (commission engine direct; for DSOs on Shopify + custom commission)
8. **LuLaRoe lineage credibility**
9. **ERP-tier rep scope** (pending Exigo research to fully confirm)
10. **Three-way revenue alignment** (transaction-fee model vs. subscription)
11. **Paparazzi $31M / 60%+ growth proof point**

### USPs That Need Sharper Articulation (Not Truly Unique Standalone)

- **Customer record auto-creation flowing to DSO back office** — partially shared with competitors; Wayroo's *automatic, non-sync* mechanism is the differentiator. Frame as "auto-creation, not manual sync" to claim uniqueness.
- **ProPay DSO-native underwriting** — shared category (Exigo, Aice, Rallyware all DSO-focused). The specific Wayroo combination (ProPay + native end-to-end + per-rep merchant) is unique; the underwriting alone isn't.

### C&C Variant — USP Emphasis

For C&C DSOs, lead with these USPs in this order:

1. **Three rep selling channels + sell-on-the-fly + vintage inventory** (POS at vendor events is the defining use case)
2. **Per-rep ProPay merchant accounts + chargeback shift** (CEO-level financial argument)
3. **Customer record auto-creation flowing to DSO back office** (Edmond's "we don't own those customers")
4. **Spendback** (CFO-level cost reduction; three-account validated)
5. **LuLaRoe lineage** (rep adoption credibility)
6. **Three-pillar FTC compliance** (structural close, not opener)

### Hybrid Variant — USP Emphasis

For Hybrid DSOs (existing Freedom client + minority C&C), lead with these USPs:

1. **Native Wayroo ↔ Freedom integration** (existing-client motion; no new vendor evaluation)
2. **Customer record auto-creation** (Nancy "huge benefit to the DSO")
3. **Spendback** (CFO-level cost reduction; Nancy "big benefit")
4. **Wayroo widgets pulling Freedom report data** (Hope "important"; VP Sales operational)
5. **Paparazzi proof point** (the recovery outcome metric)
6. **Three rep selling channels + Media Library bundle** (channel-shift catalyst for growing C&C share)
7. **Dropship** (Phase 2 hero feature for non-C&C majority)

## When To Win, When To Avoid

| Scenario | Outcome | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-market C&C DSO on Square + Venmo + Excel + Shopify | **Win** | Wayroo replaces the entire patchwork; per-rep ProPay merchant + Spendback economics + Path B integration; LuLaRoe credibility lands with ex-LuLaRoe reps |
| Existing Freedom client with 10–30% C&C + sales decline + upcoming convention | **Win** | Hybrid variant motion; native integration + customer auto-creation + Spendback all land; recovery + announcement story aligns with buyer thesis |
| New-logo enterprise DSO ($1B+) already on Rallyware for sales enablement | **Avoid head-to-head; pursue coexistence** | Rallyware is different category (enablement) + enterprise tier. Wayroo can co-exist as commerce layer; not a displacement win. |
| Standard DSO already on Aice + Shopify | **Win on cost arguments; lose on AI/UX** | TCO argument is structurally winnable (3% × everything vs. Wayroo per-rep model + Spendback); rep experience comparison favors Wayroo (no Shopify split); AI/UX comparison currently favors Aice |
| Sub-200-rep DSO | **Avoid** | Below ICP economic threshold; deal economics don't justify Wayroo's onboarding + support investment |
| DSO on Shopify with no back-office change appetite | **Lose** unless TCO math overwhelms preference | Shopify's switching cost is high; need a champion willing to fight the TCO battle internally |
