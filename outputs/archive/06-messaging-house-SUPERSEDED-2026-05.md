## 1. Elevator Pitches

Three versions, each for a different context:

### Cocktail Party (Casual, Non-Expert)

> We make a mobile app for direct sellers — the people who sell makeup, jewelry, or nail strips at vendor events and through Facebook. Their tools today are a mess (Square + Excel + Venmo + Facebook posts), and we replace the whole patchwork with one app that handles selling, payments, inventory, and taxes.

### Investor / Strategic (Outcome-Focused)

> Wayroo + Freedom is the native end-to-end commerce platform for direct selling — the only system where the corporate back office and the rep-facing mobile app are built natively together, with per-rep ProPay merchant accounts and structural FTC compliance built in. 4,000 reps on one live client (Paparazzi) generated $31M in 2025 at 60%+ year-over-year growth. We're going after mid-market DSOs (1K–30K reps) where reps are currently running their businesses on Square + Venmo + Excel — and where DSOs are absorbing 3% on every Shopify transaction.

### Prospect (Problem-Solution, Benefit-Led)

> Your reps are running their business across 4–6 disconnected tools — Square for payments at events, Excel for inventory, Venmo for the messy transactions, Facebook for marketing — and you can't see any of it. We replace the whole patchwork with one app built for direct selling: POS, inventory, payments, customer records, branded invoices, shipping, all in one platform with each rep operating as their own merchant account. Your reps spend more time selling, you finally see your field-level customer data, and you stop absorbing 3% on every transaction.

**All three pass the "so what?" test:**
- Cocktail party → concrete (named products, named tools)
- Investor → quantified (Paparazzi $31M, market size)
- Prospect → outcome-led (selling time, customer visibility, processing cost)

## 2. Core Messaging Pillars (3 Maximum)

Three pillars. Each represents one major theme of value. Three is the limit — more than three and nothing sticks.

### Pillar 1: One App to Run Your Reps' Business — Built for Direct Selling

**Headline:** *"Your reps are running their business across 4–6 disconnected tools. Wayroo replaces them with one — built for direct selling, not retrofitted from retail."*

**Supporting points:**
- **Three rep selling channels in one app** — branded ecommerce storefront + invoicing with payment links + vendor event POS (Bluetooth scanning, sell-on-the-fly, vintage inventory, shipping labels). No competing rep tool covers all three.
- **ERP-tier rep scope** — inventory + orders + payments + financial records + shipping + storefront, all in one platform. Reps run their actual business on Wayroo, not a sales tracking tool.
- **Per-rep ProPay merchant account** — each rep is a real independent business with their own merchant, their own chargeback liability, their own 1099. No single-vendor merchant model.
- **LuLaRoe + Paparazzi proof** — Wayroo built the LuLaRoe rep app (largest C&C deployment in MLM history). Paparazzi: 4,000 MAUs generated $31M in 2025 at 60%+ YoY growth. Rep adoption is not theoretical.

**Objection it addresses:**
- *"Our reps are used to Square."* → Square handles one channel (POS) and doesn't track inventory, generate branded invoices, create customer records, or hand off to shipping. Wayroo handles all of that, in one app.
- *"Rep adoption is always the problem."* → Paparazzi mandate proof (4K MAUs) + ex-LuLaRoe reps already know the platform + ERP-tier scope means reps run their business on it (not abandon it).

### Pillar 2: One Native Platform, No Stitching, No Shopify TCO

**Headline:** *"We're the only platform where the corporate back office and rep app are built natively together — no Shopify dependency, no third-party integration tax."*

**Supporting points:**
- **Native Wayroo ↔ Freedom integration** (for new-logo deals) — comp engine, genealogy, replicated sites, rep storefront, and rep app all in one platform. No integration overhead.
- **Path B integration** (for DSOs with existing custom back office) — Wayroo integrates with the commission engine, not Shopify. Bypasses Shopify's payment-method restrictions for Spendback. CTO-friendly architectural pattern.
- **Shopify's TCO is structurally inverted** — 3% × every rep transaction + single-merchant chargeback liability = unbounded cost at scale. Wayroo's per-rep ProPay merchant model + Spendback (1% on wholesale) inverts this.
- **Three-way revenue alignment** — Wayroo earns from transactions, not subscriptions. The vendor's commercial model aligns with rep transaction volume, the same metric DSO + rep optimize for. Cost shrinks at scale.

**Objection it addresses:**
- *"Shopify looks modern."* → Shopify is built for ecommerce, not direct selling. The "modern UX" has a 3% × every transaction hidden cost you're absorbing — and a single-merchant chargeback model that fails FTC contractor classification.
- *"We don't want to rip out our back office."* → We don't ask you to. For existing Freedom clients, Wayroo overlays. For DSOs with custom back office (Color Street model), we integrate at the commission engine layer — your back office stays intact.

### Pillar 3: See Every Customer Your Reps Sell To — Automatically

**Headline:** *"Every customer your reps sell to becomes a record in your back office. Automatically. Not synced. Auto-created."*

**Supporting points:**
- **Customer auto-creation** flowing to DSO Freedom back office (two-account validated — Nancy at Jordan Essentials "huge benefit"; Edmond at Color Street "we don't own those customers")
- **Marketing database unlock** — retargeting, loyalty, win-back, product launches to buyers your CRM previously could not reach
- **Brand integrity at rep scale** — replicated site auto-populates with corporate-approved images, descriptions, SKUs; structurally enforced
- **Three-pillar FTC compliance pass-through underneath** — retail-sales-documentation, contractor-classification three-pillar test (rep owns merchant + chargeback + transaction), tax (1099 from ProPay + sales tax records). Structural foundation. Not the lead pitch but the safety net beneath it.

**Objection it addresses:**
- *"We market through our reps."* → The rep distributes; the customer relationship belongs to you. Today, your CRM is empty for the customers your reps sold to. Wayroo fills it automatically — no manual rep work, no sync gymnastics.
- *"We don't need another compliance tool."* → Wayroo isn't a compliance tool. Compliance is structural — built into the per-rep merchant architecture. You get it for free because of how the platform is built, not because you bought a separate compliance product.

## 4. Tagline Options (5 Candidates)

Each is under 8 words, benefit-focused, distinct from competitors.

| # | Tagline | Frame | Recommended For |
|---|---------|-------|------------------|
| 1 | **More selling. Less software.** | Outcome-focused; works for DSO + rep | **★ Recommended overall** — captures the universal pain (fragmentation/admin time) in 4 words. Works for both audiences without modification. |
| 2 | The rep business operating system. | Category-defining; ERP-tier framing | Best for CTO-facing materials, technical buyer outreach |
| 3 | Where every rep runs a real business. | Sub-business owner framing | Best for Field Champion outreach; rep-facing brand materials |
| 4 | Built for direct selling. Native everywhere else. | Category differentiation vs. retrofits | Best as competitive battlecard line; pairs with Shopify/Square comparisons |
| 5 | One platform. From your back office to your reps' phones. | Native end-to-end claim | Best for Wayroo + Freedom together (new-logo motion); strong as website hero |

**Recommendation:** Lead with **"More selling. Less software."** as the primary Wayroo tagline. It's short, outcome-focused, works for both DSO leadership and rep audiences without changes, and directly addresses the cross-account validated "admin time vs. selling time" pain (4 accounts confirmed). It also implies the category claim ("less software" = consolidation) without spelling it out.

**Note on existing brand:** The current tagline in use ("The Future Starts Now" — video slide) is directionally bold but untethered to a specific outcome. Replacing it with "More selling. Less software." would anchor the brand to a concrete value claim.

## 8. Discovery Question Library (For Use by Sales + SDR)

The most strategically valuable discovery questions surfaced across the two May 6 calls. These become the standard discovery motion across all DSO conversations.

| Question | What It Reveals | Use When |
|---|---|---|
| *"What percentage of your active reps are actually selling to customers vs. just buying at the rep discount for personal use?"* | Seller-vs-hobbyist ratio; addressable Wayroo cohort sizing; pricing model calibration | Every DSO discovery call (open-ended qualifier) |
| *"Where are your rep sales and engagement trends today, and when is your next convention or annual event?"* | Decline-driven recovery motion (Hybrid signal) + convention forcing function | Existing Freedom clients; any DSO with rep performance pain |
| *"What percentage of your reps sell from personal inventory vs. fulfilling direct-to-customer catalog orders?"* | C&C / Hybrid / Non-C&C segmentation filter | First-touch qualification across all DSO outreach |
| *"How much time do your reps spend per week on admin tasks — spreadsheets, shipping, inventory counts — and how much of that generates any revenue?"* | Surfaces the selling-time vs. admin-time pain (4-account validated cornerstone) | Field Champion / VP Sales conversations |
| *"How do you currently document rep earnings for 1099 issuance when they sell at events or through Venmo?"* | Surfaces FTC + tax compliance gap | CFO / CEO conversations; late-stage close move |
| *"Do you know how many unique customers your reps sold to last quarter?"* | Surfaces customer data invisibility | Founder / Strategic EB pitch (Pillar 3 opener) |
| *"What does your current rep-facing tech stack look like — what does the rep log into, and what does that pull from?"* | Surfaces integration scope + reveals Shopify / custom back office context | CTO / Technical Gatekeeper conversations |
| *"If a top rep complained about inventory management at your next company event, what would you say?"* | Surfaces field-ops pain emotionally | Field Champion conversations |
| *"Walk me through what happens in the first 30 days after a new stylist signs up — do you feel that flow is working?"* | Surfaces operational debt / Phase 2 expansion lever (Brian B "joint process is a disaster" pattern) | CEO conversations; deal-expansion probing |
| *"What's your current processing rate on rep wholesale orders, and how much do you process per month?"* | Sets up Spendback CFO economic argument | Founder / CFO / Strategic EB pitch |
