## Key Strategic Insights

> ### 1. The DSO was never the customer. It was the gate — and the gate turned out to be optional.
> Reps are reachable without permission, and inventory plus dashboards work on download with zero DSO involvement. **Acquisition has no gatekeeper. What remains gated is revenue, not adoption.**

> ### 2. The two largest weaknesses in this business are engineering tickets.
> `transaction_completed` and the demand-counter tap. One gates the North Star; the other is now the only thing that opens a DSO conversation. **Both low-effort. Neither shipped.** Strategy is not the constraint.

> ### 3. Tap-to-Pay is the hook; pre-loaded inventory is the fit.
> Tap-to-Pay drew the strongest reaction of the JE launch. **But the pain that stops adoption is "another app I have to set up,"** and only populated first-run kills it. They are sequenced, not competing.

> ### 4. Paparazzi is the highest-yield target in the base, and it is in nobody's plan.
> ~16,000 un-adopted reps, ProPay already cleared, already paying Hustle $5/rep. **Revenue-ready with no cycle** — a better first move than a new pilot.

> ### 5. The library is ~30 documents deep on one measured number.
> The constraint is not thinking. **It is that almost nothing has been tested.**

## Ready for Phase 2?

> ### Yes — with one condition.

The foundation is sound: the customer is identified and observed, the problem is validated at scale, the position is structurally differentiated, and the channel exists. **Phase 2 (competitive intelligence) can proceed immediately** — and its cheapest task is a conversation with Jordan Essentials about why they left Boards.

**The condition:** the two events must be requested in week 1 regardless of which phase the work is in. They are not a Phase 4 deliverable that can wait its turn — **they gate whether any subsequent phase produces evidence or just artifacts.**

**What to revisit later, not now:** the OPE's endgame numbers assume the channel converts. If sprint 2 shows it does not, the Year-0 line needs re-forecasting — that is a day-60 decision, not a reason to hold Phase 2.
