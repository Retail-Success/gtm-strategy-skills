> ### 🔴 The one rule that governs this whole document
>
> **Reps are led with Tap-to-Pay. The home office is never led with Tap-to-Pay.**
>
> Tap-to-Pay is the strongest hook available with reps and the worst possible opener with the home office — because it implies *"you must complete ProPay onboarding,"* which converts a free, frictionless ask into a compliance project. **These audiences get different messages in a fixed sequence, not two versions of one message.**

---

# The Three Beats

Every asset below belongs to exactly one beat. Using the wrong beat's language is the most likely way this motion fails.

| Beat | Audience | Ask | Lead with | Never mention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Approval** | Home office | Permission + a comms channel | Costs nothing · pays margin · field visibility | **ProPay. Tap-to-Pay. Onboarding.** |
| **2 — Adoption** | Reps | Download and use it | **Tap-to-Pay** · your inventory's already there · no new bill | Compliance, corporate visibility |
| **3 — Unlock** | Home office | Complete ProPay onboarding | **"Your reps are asking"** + the demand count | Anything that sounds like a new sale |

**Beat 3 is rep-pulled, not vendor-pushed.** That is the entire design: ProPay onboarding is never requested cold, it arrives as a response to the home office's own field.

# 1. Elevator Pitches

## Rep — 15 seconds

> **Take card payments right on your phone, and your inventory's already in there.** It's the selling app that comes with your back office — no setup, no new bill, it's included in what you already pay for.

## Home office — 30 seconds (Beat 1)

> **Your reps are already buying tools — $60 to $100 a month, six to ten apps, none of which you can see.** Wayroo is the rep selling app included with Revolution Pro. Same data as your back office, so every rep sale becomes a record you can see. **It costs you nothing to approve, and it's the reason your reps subscribe.**

## Home office — 30 seconds (Beat 3, rep-pulled)

> **Forty-seven of your reps have asked for card payments in the app.** It's included at no cost — the only thing standing in the way is the merchant-account setup with our processor, which is a paperwork step on your side. **Once it's done, your field can take a card at any event.**

## Internal / leadership — 20 seconds

> We give the rep app away to reps whose companies already pay us, and we earn on the transactions they run through it. **The rep is the channel: they generate the demand that gets the home office to finish payment setup, which is what turns the revenue on.**

# 2. Core Messaging Pillars

Three. No more — nothing sticks past three.

## Pillar 1 — It's already yours *(the lead)*

> **Your inventory is already in it.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Proof** | Wholesale order and inventory arrive pre-loaded from the back office. Nothing to import, no spreadsheet, no SKU entry. |
| **Proof** | Sales flow back to the home office automatically — no reporting, no screenshots. |
| **Proof** | Same system that runs commissions and genealogy. |
| **Objection it answers** | *"Not another app I have to set up."* |
| **Audience** | Rep (lead) · Home office (support) |

**This is the pillar that carries the unbranded problem.** The app says Wayroo; the rep's company doesn't. Only the data proves the connection — so the data has to be the first thing they see.

## Pillar 2 — Take a card, anywhere *(the hook)*

> **Card payments on your phone. No reader, no hardware.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Proof** | Tap-to-Pay — the customer taps their card on the rep's phone |
| **Proof** | Invoicing with payment links for remote and post-event sales |
| **Proof** | POS with barcode scanning at vendor events; inventory decrements automatically |
| **Objection it answers** | *"I lose sales when someone doesn't have cash."* |
| **Audience** | **Rep only. Never the opener with the home office.** |

## Pillar 3 — Nothing extra to pay *(the closer)*

> **Included with Revolution Pro. You're already paying for it.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Proof** | No incremental charge; selling tools included, never upsold |
| **Proof** | Replaces tools the rep is currently buying — $60–100/month across 6–10 apps |
| **Proof** | Competitors charge per rep — **Hustle is $5 per active rep** |
| **Objection it answers** | *"What's the catch?"* |
| **Audience** | Rep · Home office |

> ⚠️ **Use pillar 3 against Hustle and Boards, never against Square.** Square's free tier is also $0 — leading on price there invites the obvious reply. **Against Square, pillar 1 is the entire argument.**

# 3. Audience-Specific Messaging

| Audience | Their priority | Lead with | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rep — sells in person** | Not losing a sale at the table | **Tap-to-Pay**, then pre-loaded inventory | Compliance, corporate visibility, anything about "the company can see" |
| **Rep — catalog/online** | Earning more, getting paid faster | Payment links and invoicing, then storefront | POS, barcode scanning, vendor-event framing |
| **Rep — skeptical / been burned** | Not being sold something | **Included. Already paid for. Nothing to buy.** | Enthusiasm, feature lists, urgency |
| **Founder / CEO** | Field productivity and retention | **Costs nothing, pays margin**, better-equipped field | **ProPay onboarding**, implementation, anything resembling a project |
| **VP Sales / Field Ops** | Visibility and coaching | Every rep sale becomes a record you can see | Payments mechanics, merchant accounts |
| **Finance / Ops** *(beat 3 only)* | Liability and effort | Rep-owned merchant accounts shift chargeback liability off corporate | Rep excitement, adoption numbers |

> **The skeptical-rep row matters more than it looks.** 510 JE reps are about to lose their stores. Enthusiasm is the wrong register for anyone who has just had a product change on them — lead with *what is certain and already paid for*, not with what is exciting.

# 4. The Locked State — Copy

Full rationale in [`06-positioning-master.md`](06-positioning-master.md). This is the copy.

## In-app

> ### Tap to Pay
> Take card payments right on your phone. No reader, no extra hardware.
>
> *[DSO name] is completing payment setup with our processor. It's a banking requirement — Tap to Pay is included in your app at no cost as soon as it's done.*
>
> **[ Tell [DSO name] I want this ]**
> *You and 46 other reps have asked.*

## Variants

**Invoicing:**
> Send a customer an invoice they can pay from their phone. *Included — waiting on [DSO]'s payment setup with our processor.*

**POS:**
> Ring up a sale at your table and let inventory update itself. *Included — waiting on [DSO]'s payment setup with our processor.*

## After the rep taps

> **Got it — we'll let [DSO] know.**
> You're the 47th rep to ask. We share the count with your home office so they know the field wants it.

**Honest, and it tells the rep exactly what their action does.** No fake progress bars, no "coming soon," no date we can't commit to.

## Words that turn this into a paywall

| 🚫 Never | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|
| Locked · Unavailable | **Payment setup pending** |
| Upgrade · Unlock · Get access | **Included — switching on soon** |
| Premium · Pro · Plus | *(no tier language at all)* |
| "Contact corporate to purchase" | **"[DSO] is completing payment setup"** |
| "Not included in your plan" | **"Included in your app at no cost"** |
| 🔒 padlock icon | A status dot, or nothing |

# 5. The Home-Office Economic Line

## The line

> ### **Wayroo is the reason your reps buy Revolution Pro — and you make margin on every one of them.**

## How to deliver it

> *"Every rep on Revolution Pro is paying for it — you or them. You set the rep price, you keep the spread. Wayroo is what makes that subscription worth having: your reps get card payments, a storefront, and inventory that fills itself, and it costs you nothing to approve. Jordan Essentials does exactly this."*

## Why it beats the old line

| Old | New |
|---|---|
| "eSuite pass-through is cost-neutral" | **"You make margin on every rep who subscribes"** |
| Frames revenue as an absence of cost | Frames it as revenue |
| Approver has no reason to drive adoption | **Approver has a financial reason to drive adoption** |

> ⚠️ **Pitch the mechanic, not the numbers.** The **$2/rep at 67% margin** figures are unverified against JE's actual billing. Say *"you set the rep price and keep the spread"* until Decision #1 in [`05-aice-bundle-and-free-tier-pricing.md`](05-aice-bundle-and-free-tier-pricing.md) closes.

# 6. The 510 — Store Shutoff Messaging

**Not in the original brief; included because it is live, it is a positioning problem, and it lands on the flagship account.** Drop it if it is owned elsewhere.

**The situation:** 510 of 764 JE Wayroo adopters are not on Revolution Pro. Their stores get turned off. They are prompted to pay the $5 fee, which restores Revolution Pro + Wayroo + selling tools.

> ### The risk in one sentence: these reps adopted something that worked, and it is about to stop working. Handled as an outage, it is a bait-and-switch. Handled as an upgrade, it prices the entire attach motion.

## Rules

1. **Tell them before it happens, not after.** A rep who discovers it is a churned rep.
2. **Never frame it as losing something.** Frame it as what the subscription *includes.*
3. **Name the price immediately.** $5 is small; hiding it makes it feel large.
4. **Say they can pay it themselves.** JE reps can self-pay in the back office — that is agency, and it removes the "wait on my upline" stall.
5. **Lead with what they already experienced.** These reps have used the product. That is the strongest asset in the message.

## Draft — pre-shutoff

> **Subject: Keeping your Wayroo store open**
>
> You've been using Wayroo since convention — thanks for jumping in.
>
> Your store runs on **Jordan Central Pro**, which is $5/month. You're not on it yet, so your store will switch off on [date].
>
> **Getting on Pro keeps everything you're already using** — your store, your inventory, your customers — and adds card payments on your phone, invoicing, and event checkout. You can turn it on yourself in your back office in about a minute.
>
> **[ Turn on Jordan Central Pro — $5/month ]**

**What it does:** no surprise, no loss framing, price stated plainly, self-serve action, and it leans on the product they have already used.

## Measure this

**The 510 conversion rate is the most informative number available this quarter.** It prices the Wayroo → Revolution Pro attach and tells you whether a post-adoption paywall converts or churns — before it runs at 54 other clients.

# 7. Taglines

| # | Candidate | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ✅ **"It's already yours."** | **Recommended.** Carries inclusion, pre-loaded data, and no-new-bill in three words. Answers *"what's the catch?"* before it's asked, and works on the locked state without modification. |
| 2 | "The app that comes with your back office" | Clearest, most explanatory. Better as a category descriptor than a tagline. |
| 3 | "Your inventory's already in it." | Sharpest single proof point. Strong for rep ads; too narrow to carry the brand. |
| 4 | "Take the card. Keep the sale." | Best pure-Tap-to-Pay hook. **Rep-only** — never home office. |
| 5 | "Sell like you're already set up. Because you are." | Warmest. Long, and slightly clever at the expense of clear. |

**Recommend #1** — *"It's already yours."* It does the heaviest lifting in the positioning: it makes free sound like ownership rather than a trial, and it is the one line that works identically on an unlocked feature and a pending one.

# 9. Words We Use / Words We Avoid

| ✅ We say | 🚫 We don't say | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Included** | Free | *Free* invites "what's the catch" and reads as trial. *Included* implies ownership and explains itself. |
| **Payment setup pending** | Locked · Unavailable | Pending is a process. Locked is a gate. |
| **Already in your app** | Available · Offered | Ownership, not availability |
| **Your home office** | Corporate · Admin | Reps say "home office" |
| **Card payments on your phone** | Mobile POS · Payment acceptance | Plain language beats category language with reps |
| **Comes with your back office** | Integrated with · Syncs to | Integration language is vendor-speak. *Comes with* is a fact about ownership. |
| **You set the rep price, you keep the spread** | Cost-neutral · Pass-through | Revenue framing, not cost framing |
| **Reps** | Users · Distributors · Consultants | *Users* is vendor-speak; the others vary by DSO. **Match the DSO's own word when known** — stylists at Color Street, consultants at JE. |
| — | Upgrade · Premium · Pro · Unlock | Paywall grammar. **Banned in all rep-facing copy.** |
| — | Empowering · Revolutionary · Game-changing | `my-gtm-context.md` §10 — outcome-first, no hype |
| — | "Powered by Wayroo" | The app is unbranded. Don't reintroduce branding through copy. |
