# Hybrid DSO Qualification Questions — Existing-Client Intake

**Purpose:** Qualify existing ByDesign Freedom clients against the Hybrid DSO working hypothesis (`outputs/02-hybrid-dso-icp-intelligence.md`). Identifies which of the 7 sales-team-flagged minority-C&C accounts are *Jordan Essentials twins* — i.e., the highest-leverage Hybrid candidates ready to run the same playbook.

**Use:** Single intake template applied to each of the 7 accounts. Answers populate the prioritization logic at the bottom. The 1–2 highest-scoring accounts become the next Hybrid validation calls.

**Owners:** Sales team to gather answers per account; Cassie Lewis + Autumn Fowers run the Wayroo upsell motion on qualified Hybrid candidates.

**Last updated:** May 9, 2026

---

## Instructions

Answer each question for *each of the 7 accounts*. Rough answers are fine — directional is more useful than precise. Estimates beat blanks.

---

## Category 1 — The Why-Now Trigger (Highest Priority)

These three questions decide whether the account is a Hybrid DSO upsell candidate or just a C&C-aware existing client. Without a why-now, there is no sales urgency.

**Q1.** What's the trigger that drove this account to consider Wayroo? Pick the closest match (or write in):
- (a) Declining rep sales over a recent multi-month window
- (b) Declining rep engagement / activation / MAU
- (c) Upcoming convention or annual event needing an announcement moment
- (d) Compliance pressure (FTC, tax, state-level)
- (e) Competitor (Aice, Rallyware, Fluid) approached them
- (f) New executive (VP Sales, Founder, Field Ops) drove the inquiry
- (g) ByDesign-led outbound — no specific prospect-side trigger
- (h) Other — write in

> *Why this matters:* Jordan Essentials' trigger was (a) + (b) + (c). Accounts that share that profile are the highest-leverage Hybrid candidates. Triggers (d) or (e) are different sales motions and should not be lumped into the Hybrid playbook.

**Q2.** If the trigger is sales/engagement decline, do they have rough numbers? Even directional is useful — *"Q1 sales down ~15% YoY"*, *"active rep count dropped from ~2,000 to ~1,500 over 6 months"*. If no numbers, just *"directional only — no metrics shared"*.

> *Why this matters:* Quantifying the decline gives the success metric for the post-launch review. Without it, we are selling against an unmeasurable baseline.

**Q3.** How urgent is the conversation? Pick one:
- (a) Active — they want to move now
- (b) Warm — interested, evaluating timeline
- (c) Cold — passive, no current motion
- (d) Stalled — was active, now quiet

---

## Category 2 — The Forcing Function

**Q4.** Do they have an upcoming convention, annual event, summit, or rep gathering in the next 6 months? If yes, what date and what scale (rep attendance estimate)?

> *Why this matters:* Jordan Essentials' convention (~July 22, 2026) compresses their close timeline to 11 weeks. Accounts with a near-term event are the priority Hybrid candidates — the event creates a natural deal-closing forcing function. Accounts with no event need a manufactured forcing function (much harder).

---

## Category 3 — DMU and Stakeholder Profile

**Q5.** Who's the primary contact, and what's their title? Is there a second decision-maker actively involved? Specifically:
- Is there a Founder / CEO involved?
- Is there a VP Sales / VP Field Ops involved?
- Is there a Tech / Ops Gatekeeper raising integration concerns?

> *Why this matters:* Jordan Essentials has two aligned Economic Buyers (Nancy Founder + Hope VP Sales) and no Tech Gatekeeper. That is a fast-moving DMU profile. Accounts with the same Founder + VP-Sales pairing should move at similar velocity. Single-contact accounts may move slower; accounts with a Tech Gatekeeper may stall on integration concerns.

**Q6.** Has the VP Sales / VP Field Ops shown interest in rep performance reporting / dashboards? Yes / No / Not yet discussed.

> *Why this matters:* Hope's "important" reaction to Wayroo widgets pulling Freedom report data is a Hybrid-specific resonance pattern for the operational EB. If a VP Sales has the same interest, the dashboard pillar lands; if not, the operational-EB pitch needs a different lead.

---

## Category 4 — Account Sizing and Spendback Economics

**Q7.** Approximate active rep count? Rough range is fine (e.g., 500–1,000, 5,000–10,000).

**Q8.** Approximate C&C cohort share? Even rough (e.g., "small minority — maybe 15%", "growing — maybe 25–30%").

**Q9.** Wholesale order volume — directional sense? Are reps placing wholesale orders monthly / quarterly / occasionally? Is the DSO currently absorbing 2.5%+ processing fees on those orders?

> *Why this matters:* Spendback's economics (DSO fee 2.55%+ → 1%) only become a "big benefit" at meaningful wholesale order volume. For a 500-rep account with quarterly wholesale orders, Spendback is a marginal cost play. For a 5,000-rep account with monthly orders, Spendback alone funds the deal. This determines how prominently to lead with Spendback in the pitch.

---

## Category 5 — Positioning Pre-Test (What's Already Landed)

**Q10.** Has anyone at the account already raised customer data ownership as a concern? ("We can't market to our reps' customers", "we don't see who they sell to.") Yes / No / Not yet discussed.

**Q11.** Has Spendback been mentioned to them yet? If yes, what was the reaction? (High / Medium / Low / Neutral)

**Q12.** What feature or value prop has resonated most strongly so far? One sentence. If nothing has been pitched yet, write "no pitch yet."

> *Why these matter (Q10–Q12):* Jordan Essentials surfaced three top resonance signals — customer auto-creation (Nancy "huge benefit"), Spendback (Nancy "big benefit"), rep dashboards (Hope "important"). Pre-existing resonance at any of the 7 accounts on these same features = strong Hybrid-pattern signal. Accounts not yet pitched should be tested with these specific value props in the next call.

---

## Category 6 — Stack and Integration

**Q13.** Is the account on Freedom only, or Freedom + another rep-side system (Shopify, Aice, custom)? Existing-client integration scope.

> *Why this matters:* A Freedom-only account is the simplest Wayroo overlay (Jordan Essentials profile). A Freedom + Shopify account adds integration complexity. A Freedom + Aice account would surface different competitive dynamics.

---

## Category 7 — Account Health

**Q14.** Account renewal status and recent expansion/churn signals?
- When does the current Freedom contract renew?
- Are they expanding (more users, more features, growing rep count) or stalling (flat growth, support escalations)?
- Any churn risk signals?

> *Why this matters:* A stalling existing-client account is a different sale than an expanding one. Stalling accounts are *more* receptive to Wayroo as a recovery story (this lines up with Jordan Essentials). Expanding accounts are receptive to Wayroo as a growth lever (different pitch). Churn-risk accounts may need Wayroo as a retention play before any upsell conversation.

---

## How to Prioritize the 7 Once Answers Come Back

Accounts that are **simultaneously**:

- **Trigger:** sales/engagement decline (Q1 = a or b)
- **Forcing function:** upcoming event in next 6 months (Q4 = yes)
- **DMU:** Founder + VP Sales pairing (Q5)
- **Sizing:** wholesale order volume large enough to make Spendback compelling (Q9)
- **Stack:** Freedom-only (Q13)

…are **Jordan Essentials twins** — the same Hybrid pattern, ready for the same playbook. Aim for 1–2 of these as the next Hybrid validation calls. Confirming a third Hybrid account on the same playbook is the threshold for promoting Hybrid DSO from working hypothesis to a named ICP segment in `my-gtm-context.md`.

Accounts with **partial alignment** (e.g., right trigger but no upcoming event, or right DMU but a Shopify dependency) are second-tier — pursue, but expect playbook variation, not pattern replication.

Accounts with **no clear trigger** are deprioritized for now. Without a why-now, the conversation is ByDesign-led prospecting against an existing relationship — slower, harder, lower hit rate.
