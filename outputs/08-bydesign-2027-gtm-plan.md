## Executive summary

1. **ByDesign's problem in 2026 was converting deals, not finding them.** Salesforce shows **85 opportunities created in 2026 and 2 won** (Feenix, OneKlic). 72 are still open or dormant. In 2025, 26 of 83 decided opportunities were won (31%). Buying more top-of-funnel traffic won't fix a 2-win year.
2. **Cold paid traffic brought volume without fit.** Meta paid social (~325 sessions, Aug–Nov 2026) produced 0 demo visits and 0 forms. ZoomInfo sequences brought the most traffic (910 sessions) but mostly identified companies outside direct selling.
3. **Intent and owned channels convert.** These were Capterra (20 opportunities in 2025–26, including Sciteria), organic search (35), AI assistants (6 opportunities, growing), the Freedom back-office ad (3 client leads from 32 visits) and partners (5 of 7 leads became opportunities in 2025).
4. **So 2027 is:** fewer, better-fit opportunities, worked to a close. Paid media is a small, measured test pointed at intent and at a named target list. Wayroo leads for new DSOs; comp plans are the low-cost door for startup DSOs.

## 6. Sales plan (Dan, Cassie, Sam assisting)

1. **Clean the pipeline by 2026-12-18 (Dan Lang).** Close out or re-date everything at 3% with a passed close date; put an amount on every open opportunity. Then forecast from the 5 at 50%+.
2. **Qualify harder.** A new DSO opportunity needs: a named back-office problem or renewal date, rep count, and a decision-maker met. Use `02-hybrid-dso-qualification-questions.md`.
3. **Split the work.** Dan: new DSOs (segment 4) and inbound. Cassie: client retention, add-ons and Wayroo activation (segments 1–2). Sam: Aice-led deals (segment 3) and LivePURE.
4. **Lead with a paid beta that credits back** for new Wayroo DSOs (LivePURE, Rexair first). The beta fee is credited against year-one fees when they go live and reach an agreed rep-activation level. Offer sheet due 2027-01-15 (Sam + Morgan).
5. **Comp plans as the startup door.** Best-performing email, plus 7 chatbot leads. Package a fixed-price comp-plan design that converts into Freedom.
6. **Reference program.** PlanNet asked for references. Formalize Healthy Home (Jen Boberman), J.Hilburn (Ravi Soni), Benevita, and Feenix once live (Cassie Lewis, 2027-01-15).
7. **Protect the base.** Save plays on declining Platinum and Gold clients (TrooLife, Pomifera, Lemongrass Spa first, per Miguel's report); Wayroo activation as a retention lever.

## 7. Funnel projection (Task 3) — the method, until the target exists

**No 2027 new-client revenue number is set.** The math, ready to fill in:

| Step | Formula | Inputs today |
|---|---|---|
| Deals needed | Target ÷ average first-year deal value | Open-pipeline average asking amount **$68.7K** (17 opportunities with amounts; not a won-deal average) |
| Opportunities needed | Deals ÷ close rate | Close rate on decided opportunities: **31% (2025)**, **17% (2026: 2 of 12)** |
| Qualified meetings | Opportunities ÷ meeting-to-opportunity rate | Not measured |
| Paid budget | Paid-sourced opportunities × allowed cost per opportunity | Spend not tracked |

**Illustration only** (the 2026 new-client target of $500K reused as a placeholder): about 8 deals. That takes 26 opportunities at 31%, or 47 at 17%. **2026 created 85.** The gap is conversion, which is why the plan spends on qualification and closing before traffic.

## 8. Budget (Task 8) — questions to answer first

Not much was spent on marketing in 2026 (Sam, 2026-11-27). The questions that set the 2027 number:

**What we spent and what it bought**
1. What did we spend in 2026, by channel: Meta, Google Ads (ByDesign and Wayroo), Capterra, the ZoomInfo licence, DSU, DSN, The Juice, swag, agencies? *(Sarah Percell, 2026-12-11)*
2. Which won and open deals trace to a paid source? Rexair (ad) and Sciteria (Capterra) are known.
3. What is our real average first-year deal value for a new DSO, and for a Wayroo pilot?

**What leadership will commit**
4. What 2027 new-client revenue will leadership commit to? *(Morgan Purvis, 2026-12-18)*
5. Is the ad budget a fixed sum, or a share of the pipeline it creates?
6. What cost per qualified opportunity is acceptable, given a deal around $69K and a 17–31% close rate?

**Capacity and partners**
7. Who runs paid (Drew?), how many hours a week, and is there agency money?
8. What does our own launch event cost (venue, travel, production, livestream), and does Aice co-fund it?
9. Will Aice co-fund joint marketing, and does its spend count toward ours?
10. Can sales handle more opportunities? With two sellers and 64 open, more leads may not mean more wins.

**Measurement**
11. Can we get spend and Google Ads data into the Revenue Tool by 2027-01-08, so cost per opportunity is visible from the first test?

**Structure once answered:**
- A capped Q1 test, split across LinkedIn ABM, Capterra and Google Search, with Meta retargeting a small remainder.
- The launch event as its own line.
- A Q2 scale-up only on proven channels.
- The 2026 finding still holds: **engineering time for tracking is the binding constraint, not cash.**
