>
> The May DMU work mapped a classic B2B committee: economic buyer, technical gatekeeper, field champion, procurement. **That committee still exists — it just no longer stands between us and the user.**

# Buying Motion: Bottom-Up, with a Mid-Funnel Unlock

**Not top-down.** No executive decision starts this.
**Not consensus.** No committee convenes.
**Bottom-up, with one gate in the middle** — and the gate is opened by pressure from below.

```
   REP sees placement in Revolution        <- no gatekeeper
        |
        v
   REP downloads, gets inventory + dashboards   <- no DSO involvement
        |
        v
   REP hits a locked selling tool
        |
        v
   REP taps "tell my company I want this"       <- the demand counter
        |
        v
   ================ THE ONLY GATE ================
   DSO completes ProPay approval                 <- underwriting, not a purchase
   ===============================================
        |
        v
   REP transacts  ->  REVENUE
```

**Every dollar sits below that gate. Every adopter sits above it.**

# The Roles, Remapped

| Role | Who | Influence | Cares about | Reach them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Champion + Decision Maker + End User** | **The rep** | **All of it** | Earning more, losing fewer sales, fewer subscriptions | **Revolution in-product placement** |
| **Amplifier** | **The team leader (R2)** | **High** | Her downline's performance — **which is her income** | Downline dashboards; field-leader activation |
| **Unlocker** | Finance / Ops at the DSO | **High — but only on ProPay** | Effort, liability, paperwork | Beat 2, opened by the demand count |
| **Approver of the unlock** | CEO / Founder (H1) | High | Field productivity, margin | *"Your reps are asking"* |
| **Blocker** | 🔴 **CTO (H2), where one exists** | **High, negative** | Not adding a second source of truth | **Path B, and a technical track that does not exist yet in the deck** |
| **Non-participant** | VP Sales (H3) | Low in this motion | Field visibility | Not required to unlock anything |
| ⛔ **Not in the DMU at all** | Procurement, Legal | — | — | **No new contract. The signed terms already cover it.** |

> ### Two roles worth naming precisely
>
> **The team leader is not an influencer in the classic sense — she is a distribution channel.** One activated leader with 20 downline reps is worth 20 individually-persuaded reps and costs a fraction to reach. Direct selling hands us a referral network with financial incentives already wired in; **no other software category gets one free.**
>
> **The CTO is the only true blocker left, and he is invisible in the current assets.** ProPay approval routes through technical review at any account with one. Color Street's Edmond Kim blocked on architecture in May and cleared it once Path B was drawn correctly — **and there is still no slide for that conversation.**

# The Buying Process

| Stage | Who acts | Trigger | Where it stalls |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Discovery** | Rep | Sees the placement | 🔴 **Unknown — the placement has never run** |
| **2. Adoption** | Rep | Downloads; inventory is already there | **First run.** Empty state kills USP 1 at the only moment it can be proved. |
| **3. Demand** | Rep | Hits a locked selling tool | 🔴 **The counter does not exist. This stage currently produces frustration and no signal.** |
| **4. Unlock** | DSO Finance/Ops, CTO if present | Enough reps ask | 🔴 **The most likely stall — ProPay is real paperwork and nobody owns it internally at the DSO** |
| **5. Revenue** | Rep | Tools switch on | Activation: adopted ≠ transacting |

> **Stages 3 and 4 are where this motion will actually fail if it fails**, and both are under-instrumented. Stage 3 has no event; stage 4 has no named owner at any account.

# GTM Implications

1. **Target the rep with marketing. There is no one else to target for adoption.**
2. **Target the team leader for leverage** — identifiable from downline data we already hold.
3. 🔴 **The demand counter is the DMU's connective tissue.** Without it stage 3 produces nothing and stage 4 never begins. **It is a DMU dependency, not a product nicety.**
4. **Add a technical track for the CTO.** He is the only blocker with veto power and no asset speaks to him.
5. **Find the ProPay owner early.** Usually Finance or Ops — **not the person in the room.** The close is an introduction, not a decision.
6. **Nothing here needs a salesperson.** Which is what makes this motion reachable with one closer already committed elsewhere.
