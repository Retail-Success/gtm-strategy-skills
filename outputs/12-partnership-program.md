## v01 Program Definition: Wayroo + Freedom Independent Consultant Referral Program

### What It Is

A formal referral program with 5–10 independent DSO industry consultants who advise direct selling organizations and can credibly recommend Wayroo + Freedom when their clients face the pains the platform solves (rep tool fragmentation, Shopify TCO at scale, rep activation/retention failures, FTC compliance exposure).

### What Each Side Gets

**The consultant gets:**
- A formal referral compensation structure (see operating model below)
- Co-branded marketing collateral they can use with their clients
- First-look access to roadmap, case studies, and product updates
- Direct line to ByDesign sales (Daniel Lang) and product (Sam Atieh / Autumn Fowers) — no bottlenecking through a generic partner portal
- Quarterly partner-only briefings (product updates, customer wins, market intel)

**ByDesign gets:**
- Warm, high-trust introductions to mid-market DSOs that match the ICP
- Pre-qualified leads (the consultant has already heard the DSO's pain firsthand)
- Faster deal velocity (warm intro converts 7x faster than cold)
- Industry credibility by association with respected consultants
- Pattern intelligence — consultants see across multiple DSOs and surface signal Wayroo wouldn't otherwise capture

## v01 Operating Model

### How Introductions Flow

```
1. Consultant identifies a DSO client matching the ICP
2. Consultant emails ByDesign sales (Daniel Lang) with:
   - DSO name + brief context
   - Decision-maker name + role + email
   - Specific pain the consultant has observed
   - Recommended next step (warm intro email vs. consultant-mediated call)
3. ByDesign acknowledges within 24 hours
4. ByDesign drafts warm intro email; consultant reviews; consultant sends from their account (highest trust transfer)
5. ByDesign takes over from first reply — discovery, demo, scoping, close
6. ByDesign updates consultant monthly on deal status until close or close-loss
7. On close: commission paid within 30 days of customer payment of first invoice
8. On close-loss: consultant receives written close-loss reason + $500 finder's fee
```

### Compensation Structure (v01)

| Event | Compensation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified intro (discovery call held) | **$500 flat** | Paid within 30 days of discovery call; capped at 6/year/consultant to prevent gaming |
| Closed deal — first-year value | **15% of first-year contract value** | Paid within 30 days of customer's first invoice payment |
| Closed deal — multi-year contract | 15% of year 1 + 5% of years 2–3 | Renews on each contract anniversary if deal stays active |
| Co-branded case study | $1,000 honorarium | When ByDesign publishes a case study referencing the consultant's intro |

**v01 contract terms:**
- 12-month initial term with auto-renewal
- 30-day mutual exit clause
- Consultant must disclose to all parties that they are receiving compensation for the introduction (FTC compliance; protects everyone's reputation)
- Non-exclusive (consultant can partner with non-competing vendors; ByDesign can partner with multiple consultants)
- Clear conflict-of-interest clause (consultant cannot accept compensation from competing rep-tool vendors during the term)

### Partnership Structure (Working Hypothesis — Subject to Negotiation)

This is what we'd want from the partnership; final terms TBD with Aice.

**Exclusivity:**
- **Wayroo + Freedom as Aice's exclusive ecommerce provider** — when an Aice customer needs storefront, POS, payments, inventory, customer records, replicated sites, or commission engine functionality, Wayroo + Freedom is the recommended (and integrated) solution
- **Wayroo does not commit to Aice exclusivity in the AI sales enablement category** — keeps optionality for other AI vendor partnerships (Klaviyo AI, OpenAI direct, etc.) and for Wayroo's own AI roadmap

**Customer flow direction:**
- **Aice → Wayroo:** Aice surfaces ecommerce needs in its sales conversations; introduces Wayroo + Freedom team for those conversations; integration handles seamless data exchange
- **Wayroo → Aice:** Wayroo surfaces AI sales enablement needs in its customer conversations; introduces Aice for those capabilities; joint positioning becomes available for prospects who want the full stack

**Joint go-to-market motion:**
- Co-marketing: joint webinars, joint case studies (once first joint customer goes live), DSA conference joint presence
- Joint sales enablement materials (Wayroo + Aice combined deck for prospects who want both)
- Pricing alignment: each platform prices independently; no joint pricing or bundle discount in v01 (avoids regulatory complications; preserves each vendor's pricing autonomy)

**Technical integration:**
- API surface defined for data exchange between Aice and Wayroo (rep data, customer data, sales data, ProPay transaction records)
- Single-sign-on for joint customers (rep logs into one environment, accesses both Aice + Wayroo features)
- Customer-facing UX: ideally Aice surfaces a "Wayroo storefront" inside its rep interface; reps don't context-switch
