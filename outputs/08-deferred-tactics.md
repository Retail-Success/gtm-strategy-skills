## Why These Three Are Consolidated

The Phase 8 methodology prescribes separate deliverables for pre-launch social content (Task 4), a newsletter or updates channel (Task 5), and an external marketplace strategy (Task 7).

All three are deferred, and two of them fail on the same constraint, so they are documented together rather than as three separate files. A plan is judged partly on what it declines, and declining clearly beats producing thin artifacts nobody will use.

---

## Task 4 — Pre-Launch Social Content: **DEFERRED**

### The methodology's intent

Five-plus posts on a personal profile so prospects checking you out don't find a ghost. Expertise signal, behind-the-scenes, problem spotlight, hot take, social proof tease.

### Why it doesn't apply here

**1. The premise doesn't fit the stage.** This task exists for pre-launch founders with no track record. ByDesign has 25 years, 51 active clients, $7B+ in payouts processed, a DSA Partnership Award, DSN Premier Supplier status, and 4.8 stars on Capterra. The "don't look like a ghost" problem is already solved by the company's institutional presence.

**2. It violates an explicit, load-bearing constraint.** `outputs/01-one-page-endgame.md` §7 states plainly: *"Be the content creator. Writing and publishing LinkedIn posts, articles, and newsletters consistently is not your motion. If content is part of the GTM strategy, it needs a system or a person — not Sam doing it manually."*

That is not a preference to work around. Producing five drafts Sam won't sustain publishing generates the appearance of a channel without the channel.

**3. The timing is wrong regardless.** LinkedIn takes 2–4 months to traction. Started in August, it produces returns in 2027 — the same window in which it should be resourced properly with a named owner.

### What happens instead

| Instead of | Do this |
|---|---|
| Sam publishing 3–5 posts/week | **Nothing in 2026.** Full stop. |
| Personal-profile credibility building | Use existing institutional proof — awards, logos, Capterra rating — already owned and unused |
| Content as a channel | **2027 ask with a named owner who is not Sam** (see `08-gtm-budget.md`) |

**One low-cost exception worth taking.** When the case studies publish (Action 3), Sam and Daniel should each share them once. That is two posts across the window, tied to a real asset, requiring no calendar and no ongoing commitment. It is not a content program and should not be described as one.

## Task 5 — Newsletter / Updates Channel: **DEFERRED**

### Why it doesn't apply here

**1. Same content constraint.** A newsletter is the most demanding content format there is — it fails visibly and immediately when skipped.

**2. There is no list, and building one is the actual project.** The methodology's version assumes an audience of early adopters and supporters to nurture. ByDesign's audience is 51 active clients and ~40 open opportunities. Those are reached through account relationships and a sales rep, not a newsletter.

**3. The audience that matters is already covered by a better mechanism.** `outputs/12-partnership-program.md` already specifies monthly partner status updates, quarterly partner briefings, and an annual summit. That is the stakeholder-communication function this task is reaching for, and it is already designed and unlaunched. **Launching that beats inventing a second channel.**

### What happens instead

| Instead of | Do this |
|---|---|
| Public newsletter | **Launch the partner communication cadence** already specified in `12-partnership-program.md` |
| Building a subscriber list | Defer to 2027, contingent on a content owner existing |
| Monthly product updates to market | Case studies (Action 3) carry the update function at far lower cost |

## Task 7 — External Marketplace Strategy: **PARTIAL — one active, one deferred, rest declined**

This is the only one of the three with a genuine opportunity inside it.

### Decision by platform

| Platform | Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Capterra** | ✅ **Active — increase investment** | Already the second-best paid channel. ~29% close rate, $213,275 closed on $19,345 in 2025. Treated as a core channel in `08-channel-selection.md`, not a marketplace launch. |
| **G2** | 🟡 **Deferred to 2027 — but flag it** | See below. |
| **Product Hunt** | ❌ Decline | Wrong ICP entirely. Tech early adopters, not DSO founders making $100K decisions on 4–6 month cycles. Also a one-shot event requiring 50+ supporters and a self-serve "aha moment" — Wayroo has neither. |
| **AppSumo** | ❌ Decline | Deal-seeker audience; lifetime-deal economics are actively harmful to a subscription + transaction rev-share model. |
| **Hacker News** | ❌ Decline | Developer audience with zero ICP overlap. |
| **Indie Hackers** | ❌ Decline | Bootstrapper audience; wrong buyer, wrong deal size. |
| **Reddit** | ❌ Decline | Anti-promotional norms; no concentrated DSO-leadership subreddit. |

The methodology's own skip criteria apply almost verbatim: skip marketplaces when the ICP is enterprise, when onboarding is hands-on, and when there is no support network for a launch spike. All three are true.

### The G2 gap — worth naming

`my-gtm-context.md` §3 lists **"Capterra/G2"** as where the ICP researches software. ByDesign is on Capterra with a 4.8 rating. **There is no evidence of a G2 presence, and no G2 line in the budget.**

G2 is the other major B2B software review marketplace and it is a genuine, unexploited surface for a buyer profile that demonstrably converts there — Capterra proves the behavior exists.

**Why it's still deferred to 2027, not added now:**

1. `08-channel-selection.md` sets a hard rule — **no new channels before January.** Three channels is the cap at current capacity, and breaking the rule in the same document that sets it is how plans decay.
2. G2 requires 10+ reviews to rank meaningfully. Collecting those is a Q4 activity that pays off in Q1.
3. Adding a fourth channel now means all four get under-served.

**Recommended 2027 action, low cost:** collect G2 reviews during Q4 from the same clients approached for social proof (Action 3), then activate a G2 presence in Q1 2027 once the review base can support ranking. **The review collection is nearly free if bundled into the social-proof outreach already scheduled for Weeks 1–3.**

## Summary of Decisions

| Task | Decision | Cost of deferring | Revisit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **4 — Social content** | Deferred entirely | Low — institutional credibility already covers the gap | 2027, with a named owner |
| **5 — Newsletter** | Deferred; partner cadence substitutes | Low — no list exists to serve | 2027, contingent on content owner |
| **7 — Marketplaces** | Capterra active; G2 deferred to Q1 2027; rest declined | **Moderate — G2 is a real unexploited surface** | G2 review collection bundled into Q4 social-proof outreach |

**Net effect on the Aug–Dec plan: zero.** None of these three would have produced revenue or pipeline in the window. Documenting the deferral prevents them from being re-proposed as gaps at the Q4 review.
