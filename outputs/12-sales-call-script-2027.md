# Phase 12 — Sales Call Script 2027: One Call, Two Decks

**Phase:** 12 — Executing Sales, Task 9 (Sales Call Script)
**Date:** 2026-11-27 · **Owner:** Sam Atieh · **For:** Dan Lang, Cassie Lewis, Autumn Fowers, Sam
**Status:** v1 for review. **Supersedes [`12-sales-call-script.md`](12-sales-call-script.md) for new-DSO calls from 2027.** Existing-client calls keep [`12-sales-call-script-land-and-expand.md`](12-sales-call-script-land-and-expand.md).
**Pairs with:** [`12-sales-decks-2027.md`](12-sales-decks-2027.md) · the *ByDesign + Wayroo Deck* and *Wayroo Deck* · [`12-one-pagers-2027.md`](12-one-pagers-2027.md)

A framework, not a script. Every first call answers one question early: **is this DSO changing its back office, or keeping it?** That picks the deck, the demo and the one-pager.

---

## The routing rule

| They say | Deck | Lead with | One-pager |
|---|---|---|---|
| "We're on [platform] and it's up for renewal / not working / we're starting out" | **A: ByDesign + Wayroo** | One system, migration plan, comp plans | A |
| "We're keeping our back office" (homegrown, Exigo, owned vendor) | **B: Wayroo, powered by ByDesign** | Connects to what you run; reps sell and get paid | B |
| Not sure yet | **Start with B** | Wayroo is the lower-risk first step; Deck A can follow | B, then A if it comes up |
| An Aice client or Aice brought them (Ellie MD) | **Aice-led**, with Aice co-presenting | Per the 2026-11-27 brand rule | Aice's, plus A if they're switching |

**Switching mid-call is normal.** If a Deck A room says "we're not moving our back office," say "Good. Wayroo connects to it," and go to Deck B's solution slide. Don't argue the migration.

---

## Before the call (15 minutes)

1. **Back office and renewal date:** the Revenue Tool (company profile, Salesforce) and LinkedIn. This picks the likely deck.
2. **Rep count, and whether reps carry stock** (the Revenue Tool's inventory-model filter).
3. **Who's coming:** founder, CEO, VP sales, field leader, IT. Field leaders hear tap to pay; home offices hear visibility.
4. **Their category:** check that payments can serve it. A nutraceutical or other restricted category needs the Adyen position confirmed before you date a pilot (LivePURE, 2026-11-27).
5. **Open both decks**; fill the cover with their name and the date.

---

## 1 · Opening (2–3 min)

> "Thanks for the time. I'd like to spend most of it learning how your field sells today, then show you two or three things that match. If it fits, we'll agree a next step before we hang up. Does that work?"

Confirm everyone's role and who else decides.

---

## 2 · Discovery (10–15 min)

**Routing (ask first):**
1. "Which back office do you run today, and when is that contract up?"
2. "Are you looking to change it, or keep it and give your field better tools?"

**Situation:**
3. "How many active reps, and roughly what share sell in person?"
4. "What do reps use today to take payment and track their product?"

**Problem:**
5. "What can't the home office see about what reps sell?"
6. "Where do new reps stall before their first sale?"

**Impact:**
7. "What does rep churn look like over a year?"
8. "If reps keep selling through Square and Facebook for another year, what does that cost you?"

**Timeline:**
9. "Is there a convention, launch or renewal date this needs to line up with?"

Write their churn and in-person share onto the deck's "If nothing changes" slide for the follow-up.

---

## 3 · Qualification (5 min)

| | Ask | Good sign | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | "How do you fund field tools today?" | An existing line for rep tools or a platform budget | "Reps pay for everything themselves" (fine for Deck B; plan the rep-fee conversation) |
| **Authority** | "Who else would sign off?" | Founder or CEO in the room, IT named | IT absent on a Deck A call: book technical discovery before anything else |
| **Need** | "On a scale of 1–10, how urgent is fixing the field's tools?" | 7+ with a reason | Under 5: nurture, don't propose |
| **Timeline** | "When would you want reps using it?" | A dated event | No date: offer the beta as the way to get one |
| **Payments fit** | "What category are your products?" | Standard retail | Restricted categories: confirm Adyen before dating a pilot |

---

## 4 · Demo (10–15 min): show 2–3 things, chosen by what they said

| They said | Show | Deck |
|---|---|---|
| "We can't see what reps sell" | A sale in Wayroo landing back at the home office | Both |
| "Reps use Square" | Tap to pay on the phone (confirm it's on in production for the demo account) | Both |
| "Inventory is a mess" | Inventory loading from the wholesale order and updating as a rep sells | Both |
| "Our platform is painful" | The commission run and enrollment in ByDesign | A |
| "Our comp plan needs work" | The comp-plan slide; offer a review | A |
| "Reps need training and community" | Aice inside Wayroo | Both |

After each: "Does that match what you were picturing?" Stop at three.

---

## 5 · Objections (acknowledge → explore → respond)

| Objection | Explore | Respond |
|---|---|---|
| "Migration is too risky." | "What went wrong last time?" | A named migration lead, a plan built around your commission run, and a reference call. If it's still a no: Deck B |
| "Our reps won't use another app." | "Which apps do they use now?" | Wayroo replaces several, and their inventory is there on first open |
| "Exigo already has a rep app." | "What do your reps do in it today?" | Show depth: inventory from the wholesale order, payments, one record per sale. Never say Exigo lacks an app |
| "What does it cost?" | "How do you fund field tools today?" | "It's in the proposal." Once D8 is approved: "Start with a paid beta, credited back when you go live." Never "free" |
| "Is Aice part of you?" | "What do you know about Aice?" | "Aice is our partner. Its training and community run inside Wayroo." Never "joint venture" |
| "Do you have proof?" | "What would convince you?" | References (Healthy Home, J.Hilburn, Benevita); Paparazzi only once approved; no Jordan Essentials numbers |

⚠️ **The older beta pitch says the app "stays free for beta partners"** (`12-sales-pitch-beta-program.md`). Don't use that line until D8 decides the 2027 offer (due 2027-01-15).

---

## 6 · Close (5 min)

> "Here's what I heard: [their two problems in their words]. Here's what I'd suggest next: [Deck A: technical discovery with your IT lead / Deck B: technical discovery on your back office, then a demo in your branding]. Can we put that on the calendar now? Does Thursday at 10 work?"

Book it before the call ends.

---

## Within one hour of every call

- [ ] Send the matching one-pager (A or B) with the date of the next step
- [ ] Log in Salesforce: stage, **product (ByDesign or Wayroo)**, interest, amount (even a range), next step with a date, lead source
- [ ] Note which deck you used and any switch mid-call (feeds the brand test)
- [ ] Write their churn and in-person share on the deck's follow-up copy
- [ ] Rate the deal: hot, warm or cold

The product and amount fields matter: in 2026 only 17 of 64 open opportunities carried an amount, which made forecasting impossible (`08-bydesign-2027-gtm-plan.md`).
