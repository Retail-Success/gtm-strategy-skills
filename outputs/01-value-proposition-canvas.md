## Unconnected on the value side

**Media library** relieves no rep pain. # The Positioning Anchor

> ### The strongest fit is not a feature. It is the moment the app opens already populated.
>
> **Tap-to-Pay is the strongest *hook*** — it draws the loudest reaction from every audience that sees it. **But pre-loaded inventory is the strongest *fit***, because it kills the pain that stops adoption before any other value can be delivered: *"another app I have to set up."*
>
> **They are sequenced, not competing.** Tap-to-Pay gets her to download. **Populated inventory on first open is what makes her stay** — and it is the only claim no competitor can reach without building to ByDesign.
>
> This is why the master positioning leads on *"your inventory's already in it"* and why first-run must show her own data. **If first-run shows an empty state, the best fit in this canvas is destroyed at the only moment it can be proved.**
