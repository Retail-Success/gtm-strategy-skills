# The Two Motions

| | **DSO motion** (sales-led, top-down) | **Product-led motion (PLG)** (rep-led, bottom-up) |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | DSO C-suite: CEO / Founder, CTO, VP Sales / Field Ops, Finance / Ops | Individual reps |
| **How they're reached** | Sales conversations, events, the booth, proposals | In-product, app stores, rep word of mouth |
| **Who acts** | The DSO buys or approves | **The rep adopts, then recommends Wayroo to her DSO** |
| **Conversion event** | Signed deal, or ProPay approval | Download → active use → recommendation |
| **Sales cycle** | Yes | **None.** The product sells itself to the rep |

### The two motions feed each other

```
PLG MOTION                                  DSO MOTION
Rep adopts / wants Wayroo  -- recommends -->  DSO conversation opens
                                              ("your reps are asking")
```

**The PLG motion's output is the DSO motion's input.** Rep demand opens DSO conversations that a cold sales call can't: *"forty-seven of your reps are asking"* beats *"may I show you a rep app."* That's true at ByDesign clients (the demand counter feeds ProPay approval) and at non-ByDesign DSOs (rep recommendations feed a new-logo conversation).

# DSO Personas × Motion

**The C-suite.** Reached through the DSO motion only.

| Persona | Their job | **D1 — Enterprise new logo** first sentence | **D2 — ProPay unlock** first sentence | 🚫 Never |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1 — CEO / Founder** | Field productivity, retention, margin | **"Control to the back office, simplicity to the field, from one vendor."** Then: your reps are paying $60–100/month for apps you can't see | **"It costs you nothing, and it pays you."** Wayroo is the reason reps buy Pro, and you keep the spread | ProPay as an opener · implementation detail · compliance as an opener |
| **H2 — CTO / Technical Gatekeeper** | Not adding another system of record | **"It extends your back office to the field without exposing more complexity."** One vendor, one system of record | **"Wayroo consumes from your system of record. It doesn't become a second one."** Then Path B | Feature breadth · rep enthusiasm · "seamless integration" |
| **H3 — VP Sales / Field Ops** | See the field, catch struggling reps early | **"Know your customers, see every rep sale, and reach reps where they work."** Rank reporting, alerts, media library | **"Every rep sale becomes a record you can see."** Then spotting the stalled rep in week two | Payments mechanics · merchant accounts · rates |
| **H4 — Finance / Ops** | Liability, effort, operating cost | **"Stay protected."** Rep-owned merchant accounts support FTC contractor rules and move chargeback liability off corporate | **"The lock is honest. It's underwriting, not billing."** Then the onboarding effort, plainly | Rep excitement · adoption numbers |

> **Two rules that apply across every DSO persona:**
>
> 1. **FTC protection is a pillar, never the opener.** It leads only with H4, and even there it's paired with chargeback liability.
> 2. **The media library is a DSO hook.** Use it with H1 and H3 (brand consistency: *"push unified content so your brand stays consistent"*). Keep it out of rep copy, where reps rate it low.

# Rep Personas × Motion

**The field.** Reached through the PLG motion only.

| Persona | Their job | **P1 — ByDesign rep** first sentence | **P2 — Non-ByDesign rep** first sentence | 🚫 Never |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 — In-Person Seller** | Don't lose the sale in front of her | **"Take card payments on your phone, and your inventory's already in it."** | **"Take card payments on your phone, and see your company's inventory in the same app."** | Compliance · "the company can see" · brand control |
| **R2 — Team Leader** | Know what her team is doing without a browser | **"Your downline, on your phone."** | **"Your downline and your numbers on your phone, once your company connects it."** Team leaders are the strongest recommenders | POS-first framing, unless she also sells in person |
| **R3 — Catalog-Only Rep** | Earn more, get paid sooner | **"Send an invoice she can pay from her phone, and have the money tomorrow."** Then the storefront | **"Payment links for every custom order, and one app instead of the stack you're paying for."** | POS · barcode scanning · inventory sync |
| **R4 — Hobbyist** | — | ⛔ **Not a target.** Subtract from adoption denominators | ⛔ Same | — |

> **Two rules that apply across every rep persona:**
>
> 1. **Lead with Tap-to-Pay** (R1) or the persona's hero capability. **Never lead with anything that benefits corporate.** A rep doesn't care that the home office can see her sales.
> 2. **Concede price against Square.** *"Roughly the same rate, and it does what Square can't."* Never claim cheaper.

# Messaging That Never Crosses Motions

| Message | ✅ Belongs to | 🚫 Never in |
|---|---|---|
| "All-in-one: back office and field" | D1 | Any rep copy. Reps don't buy back offices |
| "Included / you already pay for it" | P1 (Pro holders only) | P2, and P1 reps without Pro |
| "It costs you nothing, and it pays you" | D2 | D1. A new logo *is* paying |
| Tap-to-Pay as the opener | P1, P2 | D1, D2 |
| "Your reps are asking" + demand count | D2 (and D1 once P2 recommendations exist) | Cold outreach with no count behind it |
| FTC / stay protected | D1, D2 (H4 especially) | Any rep copy |
| Media library / brand consistency | D1, D2 | Rep first-run |
