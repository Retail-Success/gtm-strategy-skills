# Rep Call & Walkthrough Script

**Phase:** 12 — Executing Sales *(rep-facing variant)*
**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Owner:** Sam Atieh · **Run by:** Autumn Fowers
**Companion:** [Rep deck](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/7595853a-03cb-4a05-8368-18fd2623049b) · [`07-demo-script.md`](07-demo-script.md) · [`06-persona-positioning.md`](06-persona-positioning.md)

> ### Three settings, one script
>
> | Setting | Format | Notes |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Standing group walkthrough** | 20 min, weekly, recurring | **The default.** Scales to any volume. |
> | **Convention session** | 45–60 min, live | The proven format — repeat attendees, trainers named by name |
> | **1:1 after a demo request** | 15 min | Rare. Treat the request itself as a signal, not just a lead. |

> 🔴 **You are not selling.** It is free, it is already hers, and she owes you nothing. **The job is to get her to open it once and see her own data.** Everything else follows or it doesn't.

---

# Before you start — one thing to check

**Is her company ProPay-approved?**

| | What you can show |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Approved** | Everything, including Tap-to-Pay |
| ❌ **Pending** | 🚫 **Do not demo Tap-to-Pay.** You will create demand you cannot fulfil and she will remember. |

**At a pending account, the walkthrough is about her back office in her pocket** — and that is a complete story, not a consolation prize.

---

# 1 · Open (2 min)

> *"This is going to be short, because there's almost nothing to set up. I want you to open it once and look at one screen — and then I'll answer whatever you've got."*

**Then, immediately:**

> *"Quick show of hands — who here carries their own inventory and sells in person?"*

⚠️ **This single question routes the whole session.** Do not skip it and do not assume. Every company we have checked was wrong about its own split, in both directions.

| Mostly hands up | Mostly hands down | Mixed |
|---|---|---|
| Run **Track A** | Run **Track B** | **Run B, then A.** B is true for everyone; A only for some. |

---

# 2 · Track A — she carries inventory

## Open the app

> *"Open it. Don't set anything up — just look."*

**Wait. Let them actually do it.**

> *"That's your inventory. Everything from your last wholesale order, already counted, already priced. Nobody typed that in."*

**The line that lands:**

> *"You know the spreadsheet you keep meaning to update? That's what this replaces. It's not a better spreadsheet — there just isn't one anymore."*

## Then the sale

> *"Say you're at a market Saturday. She wants two things, she doesn't have cash."*
>
> *[If approved]* *"You tap them, she taps her card on your phone, done. No reader to remember."*
>
> *"And the money's on your card the next day — spend it, move it to your bank, or put it straight into your next order."*

> ### 🔴 The payout is the part they react to, not the card reader
>
> Taking a card solves her customer's problem. **Getting paid tomorrow solves hers.** If you only have thirty seconds, say the second one.

---

# 3 · Track B — everyone, inventory or not

> *"Whether or not you carry stock, here's the part that's for all of you."*
>
> *"Your sales. Your downline — who's selling, who's gone quiet. Your KPIs. Your company's announcements. The photos you're supposed to be posting."*
>
> **"All of it, without logging into the back office."**

**Pause there.** This is the line that lands hardest with reps who don't carry inventory, and it is the one they've never been offered.

> *"How many of you have opened the back office on your phone and given up?"*

**Every room has hands. That is the pain, and it's been invisible because nobody thought of it as a pain — just as how it is.**

---

# 4 · Money (2 min) — say it plainly, early

> *"It's included with Revolution Pro. If you've got Pro, it's already yours and there's nothing to buy."*
>
> *"If you're not on Pro, it's $5 a month and Wayroo comes with it — and you can turn that on yourself in about a minute."*

🚫 **Never say "free" to a room you haven't screened.** At Jordan Essentials, 510 of 764 who downloaded it did not have Pro. **Telling them it's free and then switching their store off is the fastest way to lose this audience.**

> ### The frame, if anyone pushes back on $5
>
> *"Most reps tell us they're spending $60 to $100 a month across six to ten apps to do this. The question isn't whether it's worth $5 — it's which $5 of your current $60 it replaces."*

---

# 5 · Close (2 min)

> *"Two things. Open it this week and look at your inventory — that's it, that's the whole first step."*
>
> *"And if you get the chance, take one payment. The reps who take one in the first couple of weeks are the ones still using it in month three. The ones who wait for the perfect moment don't."*

**At a ProPay-pending account, close differently:**

> *"Card payments aren't switched on yet — your company's finishing the setup with the processor. It's banking paperwork, not something you buy. When you see that screen, tap the button that says you want it. We pass the count to your company so they know the field's asking."*

# Objections

| She says | You say |
|---|---|
| **"I already use Square."** | 🔴 **The most common one. Do not attack Square.** *"Square's good, and honestly our rate's about the same. The difference is Square doesn't know what you ordered from [company], doesn't fill your inventory, and nothing you sell on it shows up in your back office. It's not cheaper — it's connected."* |
| **"Is this another thing I have to learn?"** | *"Open it and tell me. If your inventory's sitting there, there's nothing to learn. If it's empty, I want to know — that's a bug, not your fault."* |
| **"What does my company see?"** | 🔴 **Answer honestly and don't dress it up.** *"Your sales, and the customers you enter. Same as if you'd typed it into the back office — it just happens by itself. They don't see your bank, your personal stuff, or anything outside your business."* |
| **"I don't carry inventory."** | *"Then skip that part. The reports, your downline, your KPIs, the announcements, the photos — that's all there and it's the part you'd actually open."* |
| **"I tried an app like this before and it was useless."** | *"What happened?"* **Then listen.** Usually setup. *"That's the thing this is built against. There's nothing to configure — it's already loaded."* |
| **"Why is it free?"** | *"It's included with what you already pay for. We make money when you sell through it — so we want you selling, not paying us more."* |
| **"When do I get my money?"** | *"Next day, on your card."* *[If pending: "Once your company finishes payment setup."]* |
| **"Can I text my customers from it?"** | 🔴 **Not yet — say so.** *"No, and it's the thing reps ask for most. I pass every one of these along."* **Do not invent a timeline.** |

# After the session

- [ ] **Names of anyone who asked about card payments** — that is demand-counter data with a face on it, and it feeds the ProPay conversation
- [ ] **Anyone with a big downline who engaged** — field-leader candidate, per [`11-influencer-strategy.md`](11-influencer-strategy.md)
- [ ] **Every objection you got**, verbatim
- [ ] 🔴 **Anyone whose app opened empty** — that is a product bug and the single most damaging thing that can happen in the room
- [ ] Requests for things we don't have *(SMS keeps coming up)*

# What never to say to a rep

| 🚫 | Why |
|---|---|
| **"Free"** without checking Pro status | 510 reps at one account would have been told wrong |
| **Anything about compliance or corporate visibility** | Not her problem, and it sounds like surveillance |
| **"We're cheaper than Square"** | **We're roughly the same.** The argument is connection, not price. |
| **A date for Tap-to-Pay at a pending account** | Not ours to promise |
| **Reply to Buy** | 3 reps use it, 24 struggle with it |
| **"Upgrade," "Pro," "unlock," "premium"** | Paywall grammar. There is nothing to buy. |
