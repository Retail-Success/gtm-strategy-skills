# 🔴 The answer to the sequencing question

> ## Run both at once. The ad ships and the three pilot pitches go out in the same week.

**Revised 2026-09-02.** An earlier draft argued the ad must precede the pilot pitches, because the DSO conversation opens with *"[N] of your reps are already using this"* and you cannot say that before anyone has adopted.

**That conflated two different pitches with two different levers.**

| | **Pilot pitch — 3 DSOs** | **Wider ProPay pitch — the other 52** |
|---|---|---|
| **The lever** | 🔴 **Free branding, $25,000, first three only** | *"[N] of your reps have asked for card payments"* |
| **Needs demand data?** | **No** | **Yes** |
| **Proven?** | ✅ **Yes — Jordan Essentials was sold and onboarded cold** | Untested |
| **Gate** | Nothing. Go now. | The demand counter |

**The branding offer is its own persuasion mechanism.** It does not need a demand count behind it, and Jordan Essentials is the proof: sold, onboarded through ProPay, launched — with no prior adoption at the account and no counter in existence.

**So waiting is pure cost.** Ship the ad, pitch the three, and let the demand data accumulate underneath both.

## What each motion actually needs

| | **The ad** | **The pilot** |
|---|---|---|
| **Blocked by** | Nothing — reps need no permission | Nothing — the branding offer stands alone |
| **Improved by** | The demand counter *(ranking + the Beat 2 opener)* | Nothing; it is already the strongest offer we have |
| **Reaches** | All 75,000 Revolution Pro reps | 3 named accounts |

> ### Where the original argument still holds
>
> **For the other 52 accounts, the demand count is the pitch.** They get no branding, so *"forty of your reps have asked"* is the entire reason the conversation happens. **Ship the counter before you work down that list** — not before the pilots.

**You are not choosing which reps get Wayroo.** They can already have it, and every Revolution Pro rep gets **Wayroo Essentials — media library, dashboards, notifications — on download, confirmed 2026-09-02.** Selling tools unlock when their DSO completes ProPay.

**The only thing you are choosing is the order you approach DSOs about switching payments on.**

# Why the ad costs nothing to run early

Three facts make early-running safe, and they are the reason this sequence works at all:

| Fact | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Reps need no DSO permission to download** | Nothing to wait for |
| **Inventory, reports, downline, KPIs, notifications and media library all work with zero DSO involvement** | A rep at an untouched account gets a **working product**, not a waiting room |
| **The ad is an in-product placement in software we own** | ~Zero marginal cost. Running it wide costs the same as running it narrow. |

**The old objection — "reps will hit locked tools and get frustrated" — is weaker than it looks.** She gets four of the things she cares about immediately. What she cannot do is take a card, and the app tells her honestly why and lets her register that she wants it.

**That registration is the asset.**

# 🔴 The one thing worth waiting on — and what it does *not* hold up

> ### Ship the demand counter. It gates Track C, not Track A or B.

Running the ad without it produces adoption **and no signal**. Reps arrive, hit the locked selling tools, and their interest evaporates unrecorded.

| Without the counter | With it |
|---|---|
| Adoption you cannot see | **A ranked queue of which DSOs to approach next** |
| A cold ask at account #12 | *"47 of your reps have asked"* |
| Rep frustration | Rep participation |

**But it does not hold up the ad, and it does not hold up the three pilot pitches.** The ad delivers real rep value from day one, and the pilot pitch runs on the branding offer. **Ship the counter urgently; do not wait on it.**

# Three tracks, all live in the same week

## 🅰️ Track A — The Revolution ad *(all 55 clients, week 1)*

### Step 1 · Notify every client before their reps see anything

Not an ask — a courtesy. One short email:

> *"Your Revolution reps will see Wayroo in their back office from [date]. It's included in what they already pay for, there's nothing for you to approve, and we handle their support. Here's what they'll see. One thing will need you eventually — I'll follow up separately."*

> ### Why notify rather than ask
>
> **We do not need permission, and asking for it invents a gate that does not exist** — plus a chance to be told no. But a DSO that hears about a campaign to their field *from a rep* has a grievance, and it is the same DSO we need for ProPay.
>
> **One email removes the only real relationship risk in this plan**, and it warms every conversation in Tracks B and C: neither is a cold approach afterwards.

### Step 2 · Turn the ad on — all 55, not a subset

**It is a placement in software we own.** Running it wide costs the same as running it narrow, and the constraint is never the ad — it is how many ProPay conversations you can hold at once, which you pace separately.

⚠️ **Gate the creative on account ProPay status.** Where ProPay is not approved, **do not advertise Tap-to-Pay** — advertise Essentials and let the demand counter capture the interest.

## 🅱️ Track B — The three pilots *(same week, no waiting)*

**You already know the candidates.** Jordan Essentials proves the pitch converts cold, so there is nothing the ad tells you that changes who you would approach.

### 🔴 The frame — say it exactly this way

> *"We're rolling Wayroo out to every Revolution client — your reps will see it either way. What we're doing separately is picking a small group of founding partners to launch it properly with, and for those we're building custom branding at no cost. That's a $25,000 build. There are three slots."*

> ### Why this frame is the strong one
>
> **It is true, and it inverts the power position.** "We need you to try this" is a request. **"This is happening anyway — we're choosing who gets the branded version"** is an invitation, and it removes the DSO's best objection *(why should we go first?)* before they raise it.
>
> **The branding is not payment for adoption — adoption needs nothing from them.** It is payment for **going through ProPay onboarding**, which is the only thing we actually need.

**The exchange, stated plainly:** free custom branding, in return for ProPay approval, a launch moment, and named reference rights **agreed in writing at kickoff**.

⚠️ **Expect to approach four or five to land three.** Use [`12-sales-call-script-land-and-expand.md`](12-sales-call-script-land-and-expand.md).

### Protect the portfolio when you pick

| Slot | Profile | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **C&C-heavy**, convention on the calendar | Can we reproduce JE's 29%? |
| **2** | 🔴 **Catalog-heavy** | **Does it work without in-person selling?** |
| **3** | **Large, or non-Freedom back office** | Does the back-office-agnostic claim hold? |

> **Three C&C accounts is one finding, three times.** Slot 2 is the one to protect — it will show worse numbers, and **that is the finding, not a failure.**

## 🅲 Track C — Every other client *(starts week 1, sharpens week 3)*

**Do not wait for pilot outcomes.** While the three decide, work the rest of the list. Same ProPay ask, no branding, different framing:

> *"Wayroo is now available to your reps as a free download — that part's already happening. What's worth a conversation is that both of you get considerably more if you complete ProPay onboarding: they can take cards and get paid next day, and you get a revenue line you don't have today."*

**Once the counter has data, this becomes the strongest opener you have:** *"[N] of your reps have already asked for it."*

| Priority | Account type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 🔴 **Already ProPay-approved** | **Revenue today.** No gate, no wait, no meeting needed. |
| 2 | High demand, ProPay pending | The counter does the arguing |
| 3 | Moderate demand | Standard approach |
| 4 | Low demand | Diagnose before pitching — is it the placement, or the field? |

> ### 🔴 Priority 1 is the group everyone will overlook
>
> **Paparazzi: ~16,000 un-adopted reps, ProPay already cleared.** Every adopter there can transact from day one, with no DSO conversation at all.
>
> **Nobody needs to say yes for that revenue to start.** It is the fastest money in the plan and it requires no pilot, no meeting and no branding.

# 🔴 The DSO markup — offer it as a lever, not a default

The DSO can charge reps **$3–$5/month for selling tools** and keep it. **Essentials is free to every Revolution Pro rep and cannot be charged for** — so the chargeable thing is precisely the tier the DSO's own ProPay work unlocks, which makes it defensible to their field.

### But understand what it costs us before offering it

| | DSO gains | We lose |
|---|---|---|
| Per rep who **pays** the fee | **+$60/yr** | $0 |
| Per rep who **declines** and therefore never transacts | $0 | 🔴 **~$216/yr in forgone processing** |

**Every markup suppresses some adoption, and suppressed adoption is our only revenue line.** Which produces a clean rule:

> ### ✅ Offer the markup when it is what converts a reluctant DSO to ProPay onboarding.
> **A markup that buys onboarding is enormously net-positive** — onboarding gates *all* revenue at that account.
>
> ### 🚫 Do not volunteer it to a DSO who is already willing.
> There, it is pure value destruction: they were going to onboard anyway, and the fee only shrinks the transacting base.

**And when you do offer it, give the recommendation with it:** *"Launch it included first, see what adoption looks like, then price it against a real number."* Giving away the immediate upsell buys credibility and is also correct.

# What runs in parallel, and what does not

```
WEEK 1  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Ship: demand counter + 4 events + first-run audit   [urgent, not blocking]
  Notify all 55 clients                               [must precede the ad]
        |
        +--- A: Ad live, all 55 -----------------------------------> ongoing
        |
        +--- B: Pitch 3 pilot candidates (branding offer) ---------> weeks
        |
        +--- C: Work the other 52 (ProPay ask, no branding) -------> ongoing

WEEK 3+ -----------------------------------------------------------
  Demand data arrives -> re-ranks Track C, sharpens every opener
  Pilot decisions land -> branded launches begin
```

**Only two things are strictly ordered:** the notification email precedes the ad, and ProPay approval precedes any Tap-to-Pay claim at an account. **Everything else runs at once.**

# The two motions, and how they interlock

| | **Track A — the ad** | **Track B — the pilots** |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaches** | All 75,000 Revolution Pro reps | 3 DSOs |
| **Needs approval?** | **No** | Yes — ProPay |
| **Produces** | Adoption, revenue, and the demand ranking | Branded reference accounts + evidence |
| **Cost** | ~$0 | ~$75,000 forgone branding |
| **Speed** | Immediate | Weeks |
| **Fails how?** | Placement doesn't convert | Can't land 3, or slot 2 stays unfilled |

> **The pilot's job is evidence and reference, not revenue.** **The ad's job is revenue and ranking.** Do not judge either by the other's metric.

# Risks

| Risk | Severity | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Counter doesn't ship in week 1** | Medium | **Run everything anyway.** Track C loses its best opener; A and B are unaffected. |
| 🔴 **A DSO objects to the ad** | Medium | **Notify first.** The entire mitigation, and it costs one email. |
| 🔴 **A non-pilot DSO asks why they didn't get free branding** | **Medium — expect it** | **Answer honestly: three slots, first movers, in exchange for being a reference.** Bounded scarcity is defensible; a vague "maybe later" is not. |
| **Placement converts poorly** | Medium-High | **A finding by week 4, while it's still cheap.** Fall back to field leaders, which need no placement. |
| **Reps adopt where we can't service them for months** | Medium | They still get Essentials, working, on download. The counter tells them they're heard. |
| **Markup suppresses transacting** | Medium | Offer it only where it buys onboarding. Recommend "included first, price later." |
| **Slot 2 never gets filled** | Medium | **Run two pilots and say so.** Do not fill it with a third C&C account. |
