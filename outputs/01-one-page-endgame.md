## 1. Your Endgame

> **Every rep inside the ByDesign installed base runs their business on Wayroo — and ByDesign earns on every transaction they make.**

**The 3-year picture:** 75,000 Revolution Pro subscribers across 55 clients, with a majority transacting through Wayroo rails. At the Paparazzi benchmark (~$216/transacting rep/year in payments) full penetration is **$8.75M/year** — from accounts that have already signed.

**The near-term picture that matters more:** the financial model's entire **Year-0 residual line ($3.24M, 15,000 transacting reps)** sits inside the existing base and needs **37% adoption of it.** No new logos. No sales cycle. No new contracts.

**What "won" looks like operationally:** ByDesign stops selling software to companies and starts distributing software to people who already work for its customers — and earns the way its customers earn, when a sale happens.

---

## 2. Why This Matters to You

**2026 revenue targets were missed and are no longer the operative planning frame.** The company sells like a SaaS business and earns like a payments business, and GTM has never been organised around that.

**The reason this motion is the answer, in one line:** it is the only path to the financial model's Year-0 number that does not require closing new logos with one closer on a 4–6 month cycle.

**Personal stake:** this is the first motion where product marketing owns the whole loop — positioning, channel, and the measurement that proves it — because there is no sales cycle in the middle to absorb the outcome.

## 6. What You're Willing to Do

- **Give the product away.** Essentials and all selling tools, free, permanently. Payments is the business.
- **Give away white-label to the first three pilots** — a stated $25,000 value each — to buy branded, ProPay-approved reference accounts
- **Advertise to reps without asking their companies**, backed by a courtesy notification rather than a permission request
- **Run a paywall experiment on live users** (the JE 510), designed as a trial rather than a shutoff
- **Publish a weak pilot result** if slot 2 shows catalog-heavy DSOs adopt poorly. **That is the finding.**
- **Let the DSO keep the margin** on the rep subscription — their revenue interest and ours point at the same number

## 7. What You're NOT Willing to Do

- 🚫 **Gate any transactional feature behind a paid tier.** Suppressing a transacting rep costs ~$216/year against an upgrade worth less.
- 🚫 **Charge reps for selling tools.** Ever.
- 🚫 **Lead with compliance.** With no budget ask, regulatory framing reads as a threat rather than an offer.
- 🚫 **Claim feature parity with Hustle** before the merger and before a teardown exists
- 🚫 **Claim "included" to a rep without Revolution Pro**, then switch their store off
- 🚫 **Promise Tap-to-Pay where the DSO is not ProPay-approved**
- 🚫 **Run another launch without instrumentation.** One 764-shaped number with no denominator was enough.
- 🚫 **Build positioning that permanently depends on Revolution Pro** — it is expected to be retired

## Key Insight

> ### The DSO was never the customer. It was the gate — and the gate turned out to be optional.
>
> Two facts confirmed on 2026-08-30 changed the shape of this business more than the August 10 reset did: **reps are reachable without permission**, and **inventory plus dashboards work on download with zero DSO involvement.**
>
> Together they mean **acquisition has no gatekeeper.** A rep at a company that has done nothing at all still opens the app and finds their own inventory and their own numbers — two of the four capabilities JE reps rated most highly.
>
> **What remains gated is revenue, not adoption.** ProPay approval unlocks the selling tools, and the field is what asks for it.
>
> **So the motion is: reach reps directly → they get real value immediately → their demand accumulates in a counter → the counter is the entire DSO conversation.** The company's job is no longer to convince 55 DSOs. It is to convert a placement, instrument a funnel, and let the field make the ask.
>
> **Which means the two critical-path items are not strategic. They are a transaction event and a tap counter.**
