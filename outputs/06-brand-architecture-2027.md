## The change that sets the architecture

Sam, 2026-11-27:

1. **We lead with Wayroo in 2027.**
2. **In Q2 2027, Revolution (the rep back office) merges into Wayroo.**
3. **That leaves Freedom as ByDesign's product.**
4. ByDesign has brand recognition, which we keep.

So the line from the September company story stops being a pitch and becomes the product map: **ByDesign runs the business. Wayroo runs the field.** Before Q2 it is a description of two integrated products. After Q2 there is no rep-facing ByDesign product left to blur it.

**The Q2 launch and the Revolution merge are one event.** Beat 3 (target week of 2027-05-11) is when reps meet Wayroo as their back office, not only their selling app. Plan one launch, not two.

## Brand architecture: two product brands, one company

| | **ByDesign** | **Wayroo** |
|---|---|---|
| **Who it serves** | The home office: CEO, COO, finance, IT, field operations | The field: reps and their leaders, plus the home office's view of the field |
| **What it is** | Freedom: comp engine, genealogy, orders, ecommerce, inventory, enrollment, Cart V3, Branding Studio; comp-plan design and custom development services | The rep's business in one app: selling (POS, invoicing, storefront), inventory, dashboards and back-office data (Revolution's job after Q2), media library, the Aice experience inside |
| **Role in 2027** | Credibility, inbound demand, the client relationship | The lead story and the growth product |
| **Sold to** | DSOs (licence) | DSOs (included or licensed) and, through them, reps |
| **Never** | Sold to reps after Q2 | Described as a back office for the company |

**Retail Success** stays the company name for partners, investors and hiring. It does not appear in buyer-facing marketing.

### Which name leads, by situation

| Situation | Lead | Endorsement | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| A rep anywhere | **Wayroo** | None | App store, push, rep email, the Revolution pop-up |
| A ByDesign client's home office, about the field | **Wayroo** | "from ByDesign" | Client webinar, QBR slide |
| A ByDesign client's home office, about the platform | **ByDesign** | Wayroo included | Cart V3, Enrollment, add-ons, comp-plan reviews |
| A new DSO that keeps its back office (LivePURE, Exigo shops) | **Wayroo** | "powered by ByDesign" | Paid beta pitch, LinkedIn ads |
| A new DSO that needs a back office (Ellie MD, the Aice DSOs on Pillars or Direct Scale, startups) | **ByDesign + Wayroo** | — | "ByDesign runs the business. Wayroo runs the field." |
| Aice's clients and Aice-sourced prospects | **Aice** | Wayroo inside | Per the 2026-11-27 brand rule |
| Search, Capterra, AI assistants | **ByDesign** domain and listing | Wayroo is the first thing on the page | bydesign.com, Capterra MLM listing |

**The lockup:** "Wayroo, powered by ByDesign" for new-DSO material where credibility matters. Reps see "Wayroo" alone.

## ByDesign positioning (Dunford canvas, home office)

### Step 1 — Competitive alternatives

| Alternative | Where buyers start | Where it stops |
|---|---|---|
| **Exigo** | The incumbent at most enterprise DSOs, with a native rep app | ⚠️ Never claim Exigo lacks a rep app. The difference to show is how far our field tools go, which is still unresearched |
| **Pillars, Direct Scale** | Where several Aice DSOs run today (tracker motion 9) | Feature comparison not done; don't claim gaps yet |
| **TrinitySoft and other legacy platforms** | Ellie MD wants off TrinitySoft | Migration pain is the buyer's own words, not our claim |
| **Homegrown or owned vendor** | LivePURE runs Concordia, a vendor it owns, and won't switch | ByDesign isn't the sale there; Wayroo is |
| **Shopify plus plugins** | Startups and cart-led DSOs | No commissions, genealogy or field app; one merchant account carries every fee and chargeback |
| **Stay put** | No migration risk | The field stays invisible; reps pay for 6–10 apps of their own |

### Step 2 — Unique attributes (claims we can stand behind today)

| Attribute | Survives "could a competitor say this?" | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **The back office and the field app are one system, built by one company** | ✅ Exigo has an app, but the depth of Wayroo's inventory, POS and payments connection is ours to prove | Live (Jordan Essentials, Paparazzi) |
| **The rep app comes with the platform** (no separate vendor, contract or integration project) | ✅ for ByDesign clients | Live; pricing for 2027 open (D8) |
| **Comp-plan design done by our own experts** | ⚠️ Others consult; ours connects straight to the engine that pays it | Live service |
| **Reps paid and selling with one record of every sale** (invoices, transactions, 1099 support) | ✅ when selling tools are on | Live where ProPay is onboarded; Adyen for reps from Q1 |
| Cart V3, Enrollment, Branding Studio | ⚠️ Only once released | Beta (Pomifera, Zilis, Pure Haven); **don't claim until GA** |
| AI custom development | ❌ No product to pitch yet (motion 8) | Phase 1 by 2027-06-30; **don't claim** |

### Step 3 — Value

| Attribute | Value to the home office |
|---|---|
| One system, back office to field | You can see every rep's sales, customers and inventory, not just what flows through corporate |
| The rep app comes with it | Your field gets a modern app without a second vendor or integration project |
| Comp-plan expertise on the engine that pays it | The plan you design is the plan that pays, with no translation gap |
| One record of every sale | Compliance and tax questions have answers |

### Step 4 — Who cares most

1. **DSOs that need a new back office:** Aice DSOs on Pillars or Direct Scale, TrinitySoft leavers (Ellie MD), platforms nearing renewal.
2. **Existing ByDesign clients**, where the story is "your platform now comes with the field" (retention).
3. **Startup DSOs**, through the comp-plan door.

### Step 5 — Market category

**Direct selling software, the home-office platform.** It's an existing category that buyers search for ("MLM software," Capterra's MLM category). ByDesign wins it on the field connection, not on back-office features alone.

### Step 6 — Trends

- **Reps run their business from their phone,** and pay for 6–10 apps to do it. The home office can't see any of it.
- **Social and in-person selling are moving off corporate rails** (Square, Venmo, Facebook groups). That drives compliance and visibility worries.
- **Back-office platform consolidation** (Exigo changed hands again in 2026, per the DSU recap) is pushing DSOs to reconsider vendors.

### Positioning statement (draft)

> For **direct selling companies that need a back office their field will actually use**,
> **ByDesign** is the **home-office platform for direct selling**
> that **connects commissions, orders and inventory to every rep's phone through Wayroo**.
> Unlike **Exigo, Pillars or a back office with a stitched-together rep stack**,
> we **build the back office and the field app as one system**,
> which means **the home office sees the field, and reps sell without leaving the company's rails**.
> This matters now because **reps already run their business on their phones, mostly on tools the company can't see**.

**Checklist:** specific target ✅ · real alternatives ✅ · provable claims ⚠️ (the field-depth comparison with Exigo is unresearched) · differentiated ✅ · category clarity ✅ · trend-backed ✅

## Messaging pillars for ByDesign (three maximum)

| Pillar | Headline | Proof | Objection it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Runs the business** | Commissions, orders and enrollment on one platform | 55 active clients (confirm the count before public use) | "Are you big enough to trust?" |
| **2. Reaches the field** | Every rep gets Wayroo, connected from day one | Jordan Essentials and Paparazzi live; case studies due 2027-04-15 | "Our reps won't use another app" |
| **3. Built with you** | Comp-plan design and custom work from the team that runs the engine | Comp-plan consultations; reference clients (Healthy Home, J.Hilburn, Benevita) | "Migration is risky" |

### Tagline candidates

1. **ByDesign runs the business. Wayroo runs the field.** *(recommended: it's the booth line already in use, and after Q2 it's literally true)*
2. The back office your field can feel.
3. Run the company. Reach every rep.
4. Direct selling, run by design.

### One-liners

- **ByDesign (to a home office):** "ByDesign runs your commissions, orders and enrollment, and every rep gets Wayroo, connected from day one."
- **Wayroo (to a home office):** "Wayroo puts your back office in every rep's pocket: their sales, inventory and customers, and a way to get paid on the spot."
- **Wayroo (to a rep):** "Sell anywhere, get paid on the spot, and see your whole business in one app."

### Words

| Say | Don't say | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ByDesign runs the business | ByDesign for reps | After Q2 ByDesign has no rep product |
| Wayroo, powered by ByDesign | Wayroo+, Revolution | Existing marketing rule (Sep 2026); Revolution goes away in Q2 |
| Partnership with Aice | Joint venture, merger | Aice is a partnership (2026-11-23) |
| Connected from day one | All-in-one (to DSOs keeping their back office) | Only true where ByDesign is the back office |

Buyer-facing copy avoids em dashes and the words in `strategic-inputs.md` §3 (leverage, harness, unlock, robust, cutting-edge).

## What ByDesign marketing does in 2027

| Job | Channels | Budget role |
|---|---|---|
| **Capture inbound demand** | bydesign.com (with Wayroo first on the page), search, Capterra, AI-assistant visibility | Demand capture |
| **Own the client relationship** | Client newsletter, QBRs, release announcements (Cart V3, Enrollment, Branding Studio when GA) | Owned, low cash |
| **Win back-office switches** | ByDesign + Wayroo offer to Aice DSOs and legacy-platform leavers | Sales-led |
| **Startup door** | Fixed-price comp-plan design into Freedom | Search + email |
| **Endorse Wayroo** | "Powered by ByDesign" on every new-DSO Wayroo asset | No separate spend |

**No separate ByDesign awareness campaign.** Wayroo creates new demand (launch event, LinkedIn ads to target accounts, case studies); ByDesign captures and keeps it.

## What the Q2 Revolution merge needs from marketing

- **One rep message:** "Revolution is now part of Wayroo." Reps keep their data and log in to one app.
- **One home-office message:** "Your reps' back office and selling app are now one product." Sent ahead of the reps, the same way as the 2026 DSO-first sequence.
- **Name retirement:** remove "Revolution" from bydesign.com, the help center and rep emails at the merge date; keep a redirect page for search.
- ⚠️ **Open:** what happens to the Revolution Pro subscription and its $5 price after the merge, and whether July charging becomes Wayroo charging. This belongs to D8 (Sam + Morgan, due 2027-01-15).
