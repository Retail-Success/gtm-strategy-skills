# Battlecard — Wayroo + Freedom vs. Rallyware

**Audience:** Sales reps (Dan, Autumn, Cassie)
**Format:** 1-page field reference. Print, bookmark, or open before any deal where Rallyware is in the picture.
**Last updated:** 2026-05-12
**Source material:** `outputs/02-competitor-analysis-rallyware.md` (full deep dive), `outputs/06-uvp-usp.md`, my-gtm-context.md §6
**Related battlecards:** Shopify (live). Aice (pending partnership confirmation). Exigo (pending rep app feature-scope research).

---

## Headline frame

**Rallyware is not a head-to-head Wayroo competitor.** Different category, different tier of DSO. Coexistence is more likely than displacement.

> **Rallyware enables reps to perform. Wayroo gives reps the tools to transact. Neither replaces the other.**

The competitive risk is *category framing*: if a buyer believes "rep enablement" and "rep operating system" are the same category, Rallyware's $22M war chest and Mary Kay/Avon/Nu Skin logo wall set the narrative. Re-frame the category. Don't fight inside Rallyware's.

---

## How they describe themselves

> "Peak Performance. Every Rep. Everywhere."

Rallyware is an **AI sales performance orchestration platform** for distributed sales teams — direct selling, retail, and brand reps. Founded 2012 (MIT), HQ Mountain View, $22M raised 2022, 200+ enterprise customers, 15M users, 90+ countries. They coach, gamify, and "decide what matters next for each seller" via AI.

---

## Honest strengths (don't sugarcoat)

- **AI category leadership.** AI Guided Selling, AI Sales Coach, AI Learning Management — three production AI modules already in market. Years ahead of us on this surface.
- **Enterprise reference wall.** Mary Kay, Nu Skin, Avon, Tupperware, Yanbal, Hugh & Grace + retail (Samsung, Adidas, Gibson's). Marquee logos. Hard to argue with.
- **Capital advantage.** $22M raised 2022. Funds sustained mid-market extension and content marketing.
- **Content engine.** Monthly thought leadership shaping the "modern enablement architecture" narrative in direct selling.
- **Global footprint.** 90+ countries. Useful for enterprise DSOs with international rep bases.
- **Multi-vertical.** Cross-pollinates best practices from retail enablement into DSO enablement.

---

## Strategic weaknesses (where we win)

- **No commerce primitives.** No POS, no payments, no inventory, no invoicing, no shipping labels, no payouts. The buyer who needs these gets nothing from Rallyware.
- **No FTC/tax compliance story.** Rallyware does not transact. Therefore Rallyware cannot create the auditable retail transaction record the FTC asks about in enforcement actions. Wayroo creates it natively.
- **No party plan.** No host rewards, no in-home parties, no party-attendee capture. ByDesign Party 2.0 is a green field.
- **No cash & carry support.** No rep inventory, no vendor-event POS, no barcode scanning, no "My Stash"-style storefronts, no rep-to-rep transfers. C&C DSOs (Color Street, Paparazzi-class) are functionally unservable by Rallyware.
- **Enterprise-only profile.** No published mid-market motion. A 500-rep DSO in jewelry or skincare can't read the Rallyware website and feel like they're the customer.
- **Generic "performance" framing.** "Peak Performance. Every Rep. Everywhere." is universal — every enablement vendor says some version of this. Our outcome-specific story ($31M, next-day pay, 60% YoY) is more concrete.
- **No native back-office integration.** Rallyware sits on top of whatever back-office a DSO already runs. Wayroo + Freedom is one native stack.

---

## When to expect to win

- **Mid-market DSOs (200–10K reps) where the problem is commerce, not coaching.** Wayroo replaces the broken middle (transactions, payouts, inventory, compliance). Rallyware doesn't.
- **Cash & Carry DSOs.** Rallyware is silent on this segment. Color Street pattern → Wayroo wins by default.
- **DSOs facing FTC enforcement or contractor classification concerns.** Rallyware has no compliance story. Wayroo's three-pillar pass-through is structural.
- **DSOs whose biggest rep complaint is "I'm juggling 4 tools to run my business."** Wayroo collapses that into one. Rallyware adds a 5th tool.
- **Coexistence plays in enterprise DSOs already on Rallyware.** Position Wayroo as the commerce layer Rallyware doesn't service — particularly for the C&C / vendor / party-rep cohort inside a larger Tier 1 DSO.

---

## When to expect to lose (qualify out or coexist)

- **Tier 1 enterprise DSOs ($1B+) with enablement-first buying thesis.** Rallyware's logo wall and AI depth wins this conversation. Don't fight it head-to-head; offer coexistence.
- **DSOs where AI-driven rep coaching is the #1 buying criterion.** We don't have the AI features on Rallyware's surface and won't for the next 2–3 years. Reframe the criterion or move on.
- **DSOs whose rep pain is "we don't know which reps to focus on" rather than "our reps can't take a card payment."** Different problem; different category; Rallyware wins.

---

## Top 3 objections + responses

Format: **Acknowledge → Explore → Respond.**

### Objection 1: "Rallyware has the AI features we want."

- **Acknowledge:** "They do — they lead the AI sales enablement category, and we don't compete on that surface."
- **Explore:** "What's your reps' biggest problem today — knowing what to do next, or having the tools to actually transact and get paid?"
- **Respond:** "AI coaching is worthless if a rep still can't take a card payment at a vendor table. Coaching a rep who's using Venmo for sales is coaching them into FTC liability. Rallyware tells reps what to do; Wayroo gives them the tools to do it. If you need both, run them side by side — we're not in conflict. But for most mid-market DSOs, the commerce problem is the urgent one. That's why Paparazzi runs us, not Rallyware."

### Objection 2: "Rallyware works with Mary Kay and Nu Skin — that's the reference wall we want."

- **Acknowledge:** "Strong logo wall. Hard to argue with."
- **Explore:** "Are you operating at the size and complexity of a $1B+ enterprise DSO with a mature back-office stack, or are you mid-market with the broken-middle commerce problem?"
- **Respond:** "Rallyware is enterprise-tier — likely $150K to $1M+ ARR, multi-year procurement cycles, 6–12+ month implementations. Built for organizations that already have their commerce and back-office stack figured out and want an intelligence layer on top. For mid-market DSOs (200–10K reps) without that stack in place, Wayroo + Freedom is the entire commerce layer for ~$50K–$300K and ships in months, not years. Different problem, different price point, different timeline. We don't compete in their reference accounts — we compete for the next 200 DSOs that come behind them."

### Objection 3: "We already run Rallyware; we don't need another vendor."

- **Acknowledge:** "Right — Rallyware is doing the enablement job."
- **Explore:** "Which segment of your rep base is most reliant on cash-and-carry, vendor events, or party plan?"
- **Respond:** "That segment is using Rallyware for coaching but they're still using Square + Venmo + Excel + Shopify for transactions. We're not a Rallyware replacement — we overlay on top for the commerce layer Rallyware doesn't do. POS, invoicing, rep storefronts, payouts, inventory, FTC-grade transaction records. Most of our enterprise prospects end up running both. Rallyware coaches them; we equip them."

---

## Proof points for this matchup

- **Paparazzi:** 4,000 monthly active reps, $31M+ processed, 60%+ YoY growth. Outcome density per logo that Rallyware's "32x ROI" / "25-40% productivity uplift" percentages can't match for concreteness.
- **Per-rep ProPay merchant accounts:** zero competitor equivalent — including Rallyware. Rallyware has no merchant infrastructure at all.
- **Three-pillar FTC compliance pass-through:** retail transaction record + contractor classification fix + automatic 1099-NEC. Rallyware doesn't transact, so it can't create the record the FTC asks for.
- **Cash & Carry coverage:** vendor-event POS, barcode scanning, "My Stash" storefronts, rep-to-rep transfers, in-app wholesale ordering, shipping label printing. Rallyware does none of this.
- **ERP-tier rep scope:** Wayroo replaces 4–6 rep tools (Square + Venmo + Excel + PayPal + Canva + invoicing). Rallyware adds one more tool on top of that stack rather than collapsing it.

---

## The coexistence pitch (for enterprise accounts already on Rallyware)

This is the high-value path. Don't fight Rallyware in their accounts — extend into them.

> "You're already using Rallyware to coach your reps. We're not here to replace that. We're here for the part Rallyware doesn't do: the commerce layer. Your cash-and-carry segment, your party reps, your in-person event reps — those reps need POS, inventory, payouts, and a compliance-grade transaction record. Rallyware tells them what to do; we give them the tools to do it. Run both. The combined stack is the only one in the market that covers performance AND transactions."

Long-horizon: Mary Kay's personal-use-product model and Nu Skin's consultant-inventory segment are both functionally cash-and-carry. Rallyware doesn't service those segments. That's the 2027–2028 strategic expansion path.

---

## What NOT to say

- ❌ "We're better than Rallyware." (We're not — at what they do. Reframe the category.)
- ❌ "We have AI too." (We don't, on their surface. Don't fight there.)
- ❌ Compare logo walls. (We lose. Pivot to per-logo outcome density.)
- ❌ "Rallyware is too expensive." (Maybe true — but it's also a different tier. Argue ICP fit, not price.)
- ❌ Lead with FTC compliance. (Supporting argument. Lead with POS + per-rep ProPay first.)
