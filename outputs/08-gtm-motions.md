## Two Facts That Decide Everything

### Fact 1 — The cycle math forbids new-logo revenue in 2026

> **The sales cycle is 4–6 months. There are 4.7 months left in 2026.**

Any new-logo pipeline opened in August 2026 closes in Q1–Q2 **2027**. That is the documented cycle length from `my-gtm-context.md` §8 applied to a calendar.

| | Can move 2026 revenue | Cannot move 2026 revenue |
|---|---|---|
| **Why** | No cycle to traverse, or already partly traversed | Cycle exceeds the remaining year |
| **Motions** | Existing-client upsell; the 48 open opps; partner-sourced warm intros | New-logo outbound; inbound/content; community; PLG |

The second column is not worthless — it must be funded and defended as **2027 pipeline investment**, never presented as a 2026 revenue play. Presenting it as the latter is how a GTM plan loses credibility at the Q4 review.

### Fact 2 — The wedge is backwards

This is new as of the Jordan Essentials launch, and it is the more consequential of the two.

**The current motion sells Freedom — a back-office replacement — with Wayroo attached.** That is an expensive, high-switching-cost, 4–6 month enterprise sale to corporate.

**Every piece of evidence in the prospect library points the other way:**

| Evidence | Source |
|---|---|
| Both live prospect records are **Wayroo-first**. Color Street is Wayroo-only with no Freedom pitch at all. | `prospects/color-street.md` |
| Jordan Essentials closed in **~11 weeks**, not 4–6 months, as a Wayroo attach to an existing client | `prospects/jordan-essentials.md` |
| **28% of the rep base adopted in 48 hours.** Rep enthusiasm described as palpable. | Launch, July 17–18 |
| The founder **voluntarily shortened her own free period**, pulling paid start to September | Launch |
| Wayroo **integrates with Exigo and other back offices** — confirmed capability | Confirmed Aug 8, 2026 |

The implication is structural, not cosmetic:

| | Freedom-led (today) | Wayroo-led (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| The ask | "Replace your back office" | "Add the app your reps already want" |
| Cycle | 4–6 months | ~11 weeks observed |
| Addressable market | DSOs willing to rip out a back office | **Any DSO — including Exigo's installed base** |
| Champion | Corporate only | Corporate **and the field** |
| Expansion path | — | Wayroo → Freedom at renewal; Wayroo → Back Office Lite for smaller DSOs |
| Competitive effect | Exigo is a wall | **Exigo's installed base becomes a market** |

**What this document does with that.** It does not rewrite category positioning — `strategic-inputs.md` §9 makes positioning category a joint Unified-Strategy/GTM decision, not a Phase 8 call. What it does is **sequence the Aug–Dec plan so that it tests the Wayroo-led thesis cheaply, using only the product that exists today**, and produces the evidence leadership needs to make the category decision in Q4.

**And it deliberately excludes unshipped product from the plan.** Wayroo Back Office Lite (cloud genealogy + commissions) is in build. Rep-added personal inventory is under consideration and deferred to 2027 per `strategic-inputs.md` §6. Both would strengthen the Wayroo-led case enormously. Neither is in this plan. Tap-to-Pay demonstrated, at 2,500 reps, what happens when go-to-market gets ahead of shipping. *(It ships the week of 2026-08-17 — the lesson stands, the exposure closes.)*

### 1. Partner-Led — 23/25 (SELECTED)

**ICP Fit 5.** Direct selling is referral-driven and tight-knit. `my-gtm-context.md` §11: "one strong DSO reference creates outsized referral weight."

**Resource Match 5.** The only motion that fits both of Sam's exclusions cleanly — Sam builds assets, Daniel and the consultants do the talking.

**Time to Results 4.** Docked one point honestly: 30–45 days of recruitment before the first intro. Brett Duncan is already aligned and needs structure, not persuasion.

**The evidence is the strongest in the file, and so is the failure.** 2025 partner channel: 5 opps → $159,300 closed at **71.4% lead-to-opportunity** vs. Google Ads at 2.5% — roughly 29x. And it supplied **5 of 67 opportunities (7.5%)**.

> The channel is not underperforming. It is **under-supplied**. That is the largest unforced error in the current motion, and it is fixable with structure rather than spend.

### 2. Outbound — Existing-Client Upsell — 22/25 (SELECTED — the 2026 number)

**Time to Results 5 — the only motion scoring a 5.** Jordan Essentials is now *proof*, not hypothesis: existing Freedom client, upsold to Wayroo, ~11-week cycle, convention as forcing function, **live and adopting**. Eleven weeks fits inside the remaining year. Nothing else does.

**Resource Match 4.** Docked for an organizational constraint, not a resourcing gap: **this motion is not Daniel's.** Wayroo upsell into existing accounts is owned by **Cassie Lewis (account) and Autumn Fowers (product/demo)**. Daniel sells Freedom. Any plan routing Wayroo upsell to Daniel stalls on ownership.

**Scalability 3 — the honest ceiling.** 51 active Freedom clients; 7 minority-C&C candidates identified May 7. This motion is *harvested*, not scaled. That is exactly right for 4.7 months and exactly why it cannot be the 2027 answer.

### 3. Inbound — 18/25 (SELECTED, reframed — protect, don't build)

**ICP Fit 5, and it already works with zero deliberate investment.** 2025 organic produced **50 of 67 opportunities (75%)** and **$1.47M of $1.84M closed (80%)**:

| Channel | Opps | Closed |
|---|---|---|
| Website organic | 34 | $570,910 |
| Google Natural Search | 16 | $900,000 |
| **Subtotal** | **50** | **$1,470,910** |

**Resource Match 2 — the structural blocker.** Inbound at 2–4 articles/week needs a content function that does not exist and that Sam has declined to become.

**The reframe:** this is not a build. It is an asset generating 80% of revenue unattended, and the Aug–Dec job is to **protect and feed it cheaply** — reallocate dead Google Ads spend into Capterra, and produce **one** asset (the case studies) serving inbound, partner enablement, and sales simultaneously. One asset, three uses, no content calendar.

### 4. Product-Led (PLG) — 13/25 ⬆ from 10 (DEPRIORITIZED for 2026 — but the reasoning inverted)

**This score moved, and the reason it moved matters more than the number.**

v1.0 scored PLG a 10 and deprioritized it as a poor ICP fit — enterprise buyer, $100K deals, no self-serve path. That was correct about the product as it exists and wrong about the product being built.

Two roadmap directions change the analysis:

- **Wayroo Back Office Lite** — cloud genealogy + commission engine connected to Wayroo. Wayroo becomes a *standalone* product; a small DSO could buy it alone and have a functioning business.
- **Rep-added personal inventory** — a rep buys Wayroo, loads their own inventory, **pushes the app up to the DSO**, then sells corporate inventory through existing inventory sync.

The second is a textbook **bottom-up land-and-expand**: individual user adopts → user evangelizes upward → organization adopts → organization expands into the full stack. It is precisely the Track 2 (Direct-to-Rep) motion `my-gtm-context.md` §2 describes as planned but not active, and it is the mechanism that would activate it.

**Time to Results 1 — why it still ranks last for 2026.** Personal Inventory is deferred to 2027 (`strategic-inputs.md` §6). Back Office Lite is in build. Neither ships in this window.

**Scalability 5 — the highest score any motion receives on any dimension.** A rep-paid motion addresses a population orders of magnitude larger than the ~200 mid-market DSOs in the current ICP.

**Recommendation:** exclude from the 2026 plan; escalate as a **Q4 2026 planning decision**. Leaving it unexamined into 2027 concedes the rep-paid segment to AiCE by default — and if the AiCE partnership signs, ByDesign would be partnered with the company holding the position it declined to contest. AiCE already runs this motion successfully at $9.99–$49.99/month rep-paid.

### 5. Community-Led — 11/25 (DEPRIORITIZED — maintain, don't build)

ByDesign is already in the community: DSA Partnership Award, DSN Premier Supplier 2026, DSU/ANMP presence. `outputs/12-partnership-program.md` correctly classifies marketing partnerships as mature. Building a *new* owned community is a 6–12 month audience play against a 4.7-month window, and lands on the content constraint.

**Retained as an input to the partner motion** — industry events are the highest-density venue for consultant recruitment — not as a motion of its own.

### Action 1 — Hybrid DSO Decline-Scan Upsell Sprint

**Motion:** Existing-client upsell · **2026 revenue: YES — this is the number**

Extend the Jordan Essentials pattern across the installed base. The intake exists (`outputs/02-hybrid-dso-qualification-questions.md`); 7 candidates were identified May 7 and have now sat for three months.

The pattern to screen for:
1. Existing Freedom client with a meaningful C&C rep cohort
2. Measurable 6–12 month decline in rep sales or engagement
3. An upcoming convention or event as forcing function
4. Two aligned Economic Buyers, no technical gatekeeper

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Cassie Lewis + Autumn Fowers — **not Daniel** |
| **Sam's role** | Qualification scoring, segment positioning, Spendback + customer-auto-creation one-pagers |
| **Timeline** | Wks 1–3 qualify all 7 + scan remaining 44 for decline signal · Wks 4–12 discovery + demo · Wks 12–21 close |
| **Success metric** | 4+ of 7 qualified; 3 in active discovery by Sept 30; **2 closed by Dec 31** |

**Change from v1.0 — the pitch is now evidence-backed.** Jordan Essentials supplies: an 11-week cycle, 28% rep adoption in 48 hours, eSuite pass-through live at $15/mo, and a founder who pulled her own paid start forward. A prospect in this pipeline recognizes themselves in Jordan Essentials in a way they never will in Paparazzi.

**Two screening corrections from the launch:**

1. **Stop trusting self-reported C&C share.** Jordan Essentials' ~20% was confirmed understated — at an account where ByDesign holds the back-office data. Derive the number from Freedom data instead of asking. DSO leadership appears not to know.
2. **Screen for the forcing function in week 1, not week 8.** Jordan Essentials closed on a convention deadline. Candidates without a Q4 event or self-imposed deadline revert to a slow cycle and fall outside the 2026 window.

`my-gtm-context.md` §9 lists this as a key constraint: *"zero published case studies, zero testimonials."* The OPE calls it *"the #1 constraint on GTM credibility."* The 90-day checkpoint requiring a published Paparazzi case study was due **August 2026 — now** — and has not shipped.

| **Paparazzi** | 4,000 reps, $31M in 2025, 60%+ YoY | Needs approval; proves scale |

**Jordan Essentials is now the more strategically valuable of the two.** Paparazzi proves scale at a client already live for years. Jordan Essentials proves **repeatability in the exact segment Action 1 sells into, on an 11-week timeline, with a named forcing function** — and it is fresh.

**Motion:** Inbound (protect) · **2026 revenue: yes, via cost avoidance**

2025: Google Ads consumed $17,000, produced 5 opportunities at 2.5% lead-to-opp, closed **$0**. Capterra consumed $19,345 and closed $213,275 at ~29%.

| | |
|---|---|

The argument is **opportunity cost against a documented 29x-better alternative in the same budget line** — not proof the channel cannot work. That is defensible; overclaiming is not.

### Action 5 — Bounded Exigo-Base Test (NEW in v2.0)

**Motion:** Partner-Led / new-logo outbound · **2026 revenue: NO — deliberately. This buys evidence, not bookings.**

The single cheapest way to test the Wayroo-led thesis with the product that exists today.

**The hypothesis:** a DSO running Exigo will take a meeting about a rep app that sits *on top of* their existing back office, at a rate materially higher than they would take a meeting about replacing it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Daniel Lang |
| **Sam's role** | Wayroo-only pitch variant (no Freedom), back-office-agnostic one-pager |
| **Scope** | **5–10 Exigo-based DSOs. Hard cap.** |
| **Timeline** | Wks 3–4 build the Wayroo-only pitch · Wks 5–14 outreach · Wk 15 read the result |
| **Success metric** | **Meeting acceptance rate vs. the Freedom-led baseline.** Secondary: objections raised, whether integration is the first question. |
| **Explicitly NOT a metric** | Closed revenue. Cycle math forbids it. Judging this on 2026 bookings would kill a working experiment. |

**Why bounded at 5–10.** Enough to read a signal; small enough that a null result costs a few weeks of one rep's time. And it uses only shipped capability — Wayroo integrating with Exigo — with no dependency on Back Office Lite or personal inventory.

**What the result buys.** If acceptance rates beat the Freedom-led baseline, leadership has real evidence for the Q4 category decision instead of two anecdotes. If they don't, the Wayroo-led thesis is weakened cheaply and early. Either outcome is worth the cost.
