## Selection Criteria (Filter Order)

| Criterion | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **ICP match** | Mid-market DSO (1K–10K reps preferred sweet spot; 200–30K acceptable range), jewelry/nail/beauty/accessories/cosmetics/skincare/wellness/personal care/party plan, US-primary |
| **Pain signal** | Evidence they have the problems Wayroo solves — rep fragmentation, Shopify/Square-based stack, recent rep churn signals, FTC/compliance scrutiny, public stylist complaints |
| **Reachability** | Warm-connection path identifiable (LinkedIn 2nd-degree, DSA member, mutual partner, conference attendance) — or active LinkedIn presence at C-suite level |
| **Strategic value** | Would winning them create a reference story that opens 10+ similar doors? (e.g., a recognized C&C jewelry brand becomes the C&C ICP exemplar) |
| **Anti-criteria** | Skip enterprise tier ($1B+ — Rallyware territory, not ICP fit); skip retail/non-DSO; skip recently-funded competitor portfolio companies (likely locked) |

## Lookalike Expansion Process (Quarterly)

**Every quarter, refresh Tier D with 20–30 new candidates:**

1. From each Tier A/B/C account, identify pattern attributes (size, vertical, stack, geography, growth signals)
2. Build LinkedIn Sales Nav saved search per pattern (e.g., "Jewelry DSOs, 1K–10K reps, US, custom or Shopify back office")
3. Apollo / Clay lookalike search anchored on Tier A/B/C accounts
4. Filter for "currently hiring" signals — DSOs hiring for "field operations," "rep enablement," "distributor relations," "VP of Sales" are showing buying-trigger activity
5. Output: 20–30 new accounts that match the pattern; promote to Tier B over time based on engagement

**Channel watch list (for signal-based prospecting):**
- DSA Member Directory (new members + recently active members)
- DSN industry news (M&A activity, leadership changes, product launches, FTC actions)
- ANMP speaker rosters + attendee lists
- Capterra reviews of competing platforms (DSOs leaving Aice / Exigo / PPS / Thatcher are searching for alternatives)
- LinkedIn industry hashtag activity (#directselling, #directsales, #MLM, #partyplan)

## Anti-Patterns / Off the List

**Do NOT include on the dream client list:**

| Pattern | Why |
|---|---|
| Enterprise tier ($1B+ DSOs — Mary Kay, Avon, Tupperware, Doterra, Young Living) | Rallyware territory; Wayroo + Freedom is not built for that complexity. Co-existence possible (commerce layer for C&C segments) but not primary target. |
| <200-rep DSOs | Below ICP economic threshold; deal economics don't justify Wayroo's onboarding + support investment |
| Pure retail (not DSO) | Wayroo is purpose-built for direct selling; retail prospects waste cycles |
| Recently funded competitor portfolio companies | Locked into competing platform; switching cost prohibitive |
| Aspirational logos without ICP match | "Would be cool to land Sephora" — they're not a DSO. Skip. |
| Defunct or distressed brands | Verify status before adding (e.g., Premier Designs closed in 2020; Cookie Lee defunct) |
