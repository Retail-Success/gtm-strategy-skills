## GTM Implications

### ⭐ POST-LAUNCH IMPLICATIONS (July 17–18, 2026) — supersede where they conflict with the May 6 reading below

**1. This account is now the proof asset for the entire existing-client upsell motion.**
An ~11-week cycle (vs. 4–6 months standard), 28% rep adoption in 48 hours, eSuite pass-through working in production, and a founder voluntarily shortening their own free period. That combination is the case for `outputs/08-gtm-motions.md` Action 1 (Hybrid DSO decline-scan sprint) and should be the lead evidence in any Aug–Dec 2026 exec material.

**2. Rep-side positioning must lead with selling, not organizing.**
Every top-tier rep reaction was a tool that helps them *earn* (Tap-to-Pay, invoicing with payment links) or *see* (downline reports, dashboard widgets, back-office data on the phone). Compliance, media library, and inventory-organization features did not register at the rep level. Current rep-facing positioning underweights the earning frame.

**3. The $60/mo rep tool spend is the strongest rep-economics argument yet captured.**
Reps pay a minimum $60/mo across Canva, ChatGPT, Square, Boards, and Project Broadcast. Wayroo arrives for $5 incremental. This is a concrete, quotable substitution argument for both the rep pitch ("one app, less spend") and the DSO pitch ("your reps keep more of what they earn"). It also validates the *"more selling time, less admin time"* theme already cross-account confirmed at four accounts.

**4. The ~20% C&C estimate was wrong — and that has implications beyond this account.**
More reps sell in person than corporate or ByDesign believed. Since this misread happened at an **existing client where ByDesign holds back-office data**, the same estimate is likely unreliable across the six remaining Hybrid candidates where visibility is lower. The qualifying question *"What % of your reps carry personal inventory?"* is producing bad data — probably because DSO leadership genuinely does not know. **Recommend replacing self-reported estimates with a back-office-derived measurement wherever ByDesign has the data.**

**5. ByDesign's own back office was part of the fragmented rep workflow.**
Reps previously logged into Freedom after each sale to manage inventory, or kept spreadsheets. Part of Wayroo's rep-side value is fixing a Freedom UX gap. Worth stating plainly internally; not buyer-facing material.

**6. Media Library is mis-attributed in the 2026 plan.** See the persona-split note in Features. It is a DSO retention/brand-control feature, not a rep-adoption driver, and `strategic-inputs.md` §1 currently credits it against WAY-1 (Wayroo active users). Flag to Unified Strategy.

**7. Tap-to-Pay now carries customer-committed expectation risk at ~2,500 reps.** The most exciting feature at the launch does not exist yet. Every week of slip erodes the launch's adoption momentum at the reference account the rest of the motion depends on.

### What does this tell us about positioning?
Three positioning gaps surfaced:

1. **Growth-and-recovery framing is missing.** Current Wayroo positioning leads with rep activation, FTC compliance, and customer data ownership. None of these is the lead Hope needed. The lead she needed was *"Wayroo is the lever to reverse a sales and engagement decline at an existing DSO."* This is the Paparazzi story (4,000 MAUs, $31M, 60%+ growth) — but it isn't currently positioned that way. Recommendation: revisit `outputs/06-*` positioning work to add a recovery/growth narrative aimed at existing-client upsell.

2. **"Customer auto-creation in DSO back office" should be elevated to a headline value prop.** Two-account validated (Nancy + Edmond), both unprompted. Currently embedded in broader "customer data ownership" language in `my-gtm-context.md`. Should become a named, headline-level pitch.

3. **Compliance-led positioning is a C&C-majority pitch only.** FTC and tax compliance was not raised once at Jordan Essentials despite the ~20% C&C cohort. For Hybrid and Standard DSOs, growth and customer data lead — not compliance. Don't lead with FTC for accounts where C&C is a minority.

### What does this tell us about the product?
- **Rep performance dashboards** are higher-resonance than current positioning suggests. Hope explicitly named this as "important." The AI Report builder roadmap item (currently de-prioritized for 2026) has a real-customer signal behind it — worth surfacing to product even at low priority.

Current positioning treats POS, media library, and rep enablement as separate feature lines. The bundled outcome chain is the more powerful frame.
- **Spendback's DSO cost-savings angle** is a confirmed two-account-resonant feature with a quantified proof point (Paparazzi). It deserves the one-pager already on the Color Street next-steps list (Sam Atieh owns).

### What does this tell us about the sales motion?
Two new motions surfaced, both worth operationalizing:

1. **Decline-driven existing-client Wayroo upsell.** Existing Freedom clients (51 active) should be reviewed for sales/engagement decline signals — each is a potential Wayroo-recovery upsell candidate. Add "When is your next convention?" to Daniel's standard discovery questions — convention dates create urgency that organic timelines lack.

DMU note: Nancy + Hope are both Economic Buyers. Two-EB accounts move faster than single-EB accounts when both are aligned (which they are here) — but the announcement plan also doubles the stakeholder risk if delivery slips.

### What does this tell us about pricing?
- **Need to clarify eSuite pass-through applicability for existing-client Wayroo upsells.** Pricing model assumes new-logo deals; the upsell-only motion may need a distinct contract structure.
- **Convention timeline implies pilot vs. full rollout decision.** 11 weeks is tight for full deployment + rep enablement at scale. Pilot pricing for the C&C cohort first (with full rollout post-convention) may de-risk the launch.
