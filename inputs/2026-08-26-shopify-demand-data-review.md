## Reading notes — added on capture, not in the original

These are cautions for anyone using the figures above. **They do not change the data.**

**1. The cluster total exceeds the sum of the three listed keywords.** Sep 23 shows 50 against a visible sum of 40; Aug 26 shows 320 against 300. The gap is consistent (~20–30/mo), so the cluster evidently contains additional unlisted terms and the three rows are the top three, not the whole set. **Do not try to reconcile the table — cite the cluster total or the individual terms, never both as if they add up.**

**2. Absolute volumes are small.** 320 searches/mo is the whole cluster. **The 3.5× trend against a flat core category is the argument; the volume is not.** Presenting 320 to an executive as market validation invites the obvious rebuttal.

**3. The core category is flat, not declining** — and it is ~6× the Shopify cluster in absolute volume (1,900 vs. 320). The claim is a shift in where growth is, not that the core category is dying.

**4. "mlm software" is volatile.** It moves 1,900 → 1,000 → 1,900 → 1,600 → 1,900 across the period. "FLAT" describes endpoints; the series is noisy. Fine as stated, but do not build a trend claim on any single interior point.

**5. The delisted partner is unnamed in the original.** The PDF says only "a partner removed from the preferred partner marketplace in June 2026 for non-payment." **Sam identified this as ShapeTech in conversation on 2026-08-27.** The identification is not sourced in this document. 🔴 **Confirm independently and agree the permitted external wording with legal before any client-facing use** — it is the lead message of the connector motion. See open item #7 in the positioning doc.

**6. "Existing clients researching Shopify: 2" is corroborated by name** — Youngevity and Pomifera appear in the page-researching list. These two are Segment C in the positioning doc and the most time-critical retention audience in the motion.

**7. This is a build-the-connector business case, not an existing-client migration argument.** It was written to justify investment. Its value to the existing-client motion is narrower and specific: it proves Shopify support is resourced core product rather than a side project, which is what earns a client's willingness to depend on the connector.
