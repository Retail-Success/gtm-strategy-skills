## Sam's clarifications on the ad (2026-09-23)

Recorded because they settled the positioning review. Paraphrased from conversation:

- **Enterprise new-logo DSOs get the all-in-one pitch:** ByDesign for the back office plus Wayroo for the field.
- **Wayroo replaces Square and several other apps.** Merged with Aice later, it will replace nearly all the apps a rep needs to sell and create content.
  - *Follow-up:* the "nearly every app" claim **can be made now, regardless of Aice.**
- **Reps at DSOs not on ByDesign:** Wayroo integrates with their back-office provider.
  - *Follow-up:* **a rep can recommend Wayroo to her DSO.** This is the product-led growth motion, separate from the DSO motion. DSO personas (CEO, founder, C-suite) belong to the DSO motion; the rep persona belongs to the PLG motion. Recorded in [`outputs/06-persona-motion-messaging-map.md`](../outputs/06-persona-motion-messaging-map.md).
- **ByDesign's own reps:** Wayroo is already integrated. Inventory shows up and is managed automatically as they sell.
- **Custom branding is an upsell.** Not a messaging risk.
- **"Protected" means protected from the FTC.** A reasonable argument that appeals to DSOs.
- **The media library** lets a DSO push unified content to its reps, which keeps the brand consistent.
