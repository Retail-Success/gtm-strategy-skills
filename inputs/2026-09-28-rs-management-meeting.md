## 2. What fails and what works — Autumn Fowers, from 15+ Wayroo demos

> *"And because in my experience with the **15 plus demos that I've done of Wayroo**, when you talk about compliance and the selling tools, they just... they don't even respond. Like **that's not really our business**, you know what I mean? And then they're **done before the call even ends**."*

> *"But since we've shifted to this **extension of Revolution, that Revolution can now be in the palm of their consultant's hands** — or parts of it, right? — then **there's interest**. And now they're like, well, can it do this? Oh, **it would be great if it could do wholesale ordering**."*

## 3. Back office is the wrong lead at an event — Scott Maher / Morgan Purvis / Autumn Fowers

## 5. Jordan Essentials — selling tools launch, day 3 (Autumn Fowers / Philip Beedle)

Numbers as of the morning of 2026-09-28, run by Philip. Selling tools launched to all JE reps the preceding **Friday (2026-09-25)**.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Signed up for a merchant account (incl. betas) | **41** |
| Nancy's pre-launch expectation | 25 |
| Of the 41, **without** Revolution (must start paying to keep it) | **17** |
| Of the 41, who have **added inventory** | **9** |
| Transaction count / volume | **zero so far** |
| Trial period | Requested extension from **Oct 1 → Nov 1**; awaiting Hope/Nancy |

> **Autumn:** *"That's even already higher than Nancy thought it would be, she said 25... Of those 41 users, **17 do not have Revolution**. So in order to keep it, they would have to start paying for Revolution — which is again **what we were hoping that this would do**."*

**The adoption blocker is onboarding, again:**

> *"Only nine of the 41 have actually added inventory. And that's one of the things I think we need to do better when we launch these clients: **better communication, training, step by step, how to eat the elephant**."*

> *"One of the roadblocks for Jordan Essentials is **nothing is barcoded**, and Wayroo is very barcode centric. That's how you sell fast, that's how you sell easy... So our marketing team came up with like their **top best sellers barcode sheets** that we could send, and we got those out to them on Friday... that has been a roadblock and **will be a roadblock for any of these companies that come on to selling tools that don't use barcodes**."*

**The counter-argument she wants the field to use:**

> *"What they're doing in Square is they just type in the price. But what I showed Cassie is... **if you bring up 10 products to me that you're going to buy at a show and I have to add them up — that's the same amount of time as punching them into Wayroo** if we can make it easy enough. So then you have all of the data of exactly what you sold and who you sold it to."*

## 6. Two product facts that constrain what can be claimed today

**Tap-to-Pay has NOT shipped.**

> **Scott:** *"The Tap-to-Pay thing, we are going around with ProPay on it. It's really close. There's **an issue with iOS** right now, but we're just waiting on that to get solved and then we'll hopefully be able to get that turned on. It **works on Android** apparently."*
>
> **Autumn:** *"We've had successful transactions on both platforms, but then there's like little things and then they go to fix those little things and break something else. So again, we're at the final stages. It's promising, but you know."*

**Without it, Wayroo is slower than Square — stated plainly:**

> **Autumn:** *"Right now, if they go to a sale, they have to **manually enter the credit card**, because **we're not selling anyone $106 card readers**, right? So compared to Square, **Wayroo is a lot slower until we have Tap-to-Pay**."*

**Price override at checkout shipped missing.**

> **Autumn:** *"The other big thing that came out during the launch of this is **the ability to change the price**... Nancy said if she realized that that wasn't available, she wouldn't have even let it launch. But here we are... **There is not a point of sale out there that you can't manage your own prices**."*
>
> Development diverted to a minimum fix — *"just overriding the price, not discounts, not promos"* — with discounts and promos to build on top of it later. **Scott:** *"We're a FinTech company with a FinTech app. I think that **every selling tool feature that we need needs to be prioritized**... We have a client lined up here that has people to use it and they're not using it."*
