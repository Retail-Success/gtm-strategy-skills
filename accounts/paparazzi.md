## 5. Survey Evidence — Break Through Convention, 2026

**50 responses, 2026-07-26 → 08-09.** Full analysis: [`outputs/02-survey-results.md`](../outputs/02-survey-results.md).

> 🔴 **Sampling caveat, and it matters more than any finding.** Respondents attended a **Premiere training workshop**. 50 of ~20,000 = **0.25% of the field**, and 70% have used the product over a year. **The 86% adoption figure describes the sample, not Paparazzi.** Field penetration is ~20%. Anyone quoting 86% will be wrong by 4x.

**Feature usage (of 43 users):** Inventory Management 38 · Online Store 38 · Invoicing 35 · **Pink Card 31** · **Reply to Buy 3**

**The three findings:**

1. 🔴 **Onboarding is the barrier, not the product.** 6 of 7 non-adopters: *"getting started seemed too complicated."* **None said the product was worse.** These reps attended a training session and still did not start.
2. 🔴 **Reply to Buy: 3 use it, 24 struggle with it.** The only feature where friction exceeds usage — by 8x. **Unlanded, not under-marketed.**
3. **Adoption rises with rank.** Director **100%** (18/18) → Star Consultant **80%** → Consultant **50%**. Corroborates the team-leader persona from a second account by a second method.

**Where they go instead:** **Square (5 of 7)**, Cash App (1), Zelle (1).

The pilot buys branded reference accounts and answers the catalog-only question. Both are valuable. **Neither is urgent, and both require ProPay conversations that Paparazzi has already had.**

**Two parallel tracks:**

**A — Re-engagement.** Survey the ~16,000, then run the onboarding-led placement. See [`outputs/08-channel-strategy.md`](../outputs/08-channel-strategy.md) creative D.

**B — Hustle displacement.** Ask the question nobody has asked:

⚠️ **Track B is gated on the Aice merger, which is unsigned.** Wayroo's Media Library is a *partial* answer today — **do not claim parity.** Track B is a conversation to open, not a claim to make.
