# Accounts Index

Strategy docs for **existing ByDesign clients** who are simultaneously Wayroo prospects, expansion targets, churn risks, or partnership catalyst accounts.

## Why this folder exists (and how it differs from `prospects/`)

- **`prospects/`** = net-new acquisition. Companies ByDesign does not yet have a commercial relationship with. Used by `analyzing-call-transcripts` and `sdr-agent` for cold/early-stage motion.
- **`accounts/`** (this folder) = existing ByDesign clients with active expansion, retention, or repositioning motion. The sales motion, available levers, risks, and stakeholder dynamics are different — these accounts already have contracts, incumbent products, account history, support relationships, and switching costs we both benefit from and have to navigate.

Treating an existing client as a "prospect" misframes the work and leaks into how the team approaches them. Keep the distinction.

## When to create an `accounts/[slug].md` file

Create one when an existing ByDesign client has any of:
- An active Wayroo-sell motion (we want them to adopt Wayroo)
- Churn risk requiring a retention play
- A partnership catalyst role (the account is the forcing function behind a vendor partnership — e.g., Bravenly ↔ Aice)
- An expansion opportunity beyond their current product footprint (Freedom → Wayroo, Revolution → Wayroo migration, Canada/international expansion via ProPay, etc.)
- A strategic risk (using a competitor's product alongside ours, building integrations to displace our surface area, etc.)

A routine renewal with no expansion or risk does not need an `accounts/` file. Account management lives in the CRM.

## Document structure (template)

Each `accounts/[slug].md` should cover:

1. **Status snapshot** — current product footprint, ARR, churn risk level, key relationships
2. **Relationship map** — DMU on the client side, owner(s) on the ByDesign side
3. **Current ByDesign footprint** — what they're paying for, what they've refused, what they've worked around
4. **Strategic situation** — what's actually going on (e.g., third-party integrations, competing vendors in the stack, expansion plans)
5. **The Wayroo opportunity** — sizing, blockers, conversion path
6. **The play** — the strategy, including any vendor-partnership or product-roadmap dependencies
7. **90-day plan** — owner, action, deadline
8. **Risks** — what could go wrong, how we mitigate
9. **Success metrics** — what "won" looks like
